## A model complaint email

Marc's flight was cancelled, and he had to pay for a hotel. He writes to the airline.

*Objet : Vol du 12 juin annulé, numéro de dossier 45127*

*Madame, Monsieur,*

*J'avais réservé avec votre compagnie aérienne un vol Lyon-Lisbonne pour le 12 juin, à 18 heures. Le jour du vol, à l'aéroport, le vol a été annulé. Il ne restait qu'un vol le matin suivant, à 7 heures.*

*Au comptoir, on ne m'a proposé ni hôtel ni repas. J'ai donc été obligé de payer une nuit d'hôtel près de l'aéroport : 120 euros. Ce n'est pas normal.*

*Je joins à ce mail la facture de l'hôtel et ma carte d'embarquement comme justificatifs.*

*Je vous serais reconnaissant de me rembourser la nuit d'hôtel dans les plus brefs délais, et de me dire si j'ai droit à un dédommagement.*

*En vous remerciant par avance,*

*Bien cordialement,*

*Marc Dubois*

(Subject: Flight of 12 June cancelled, reference number 45127. Dear Sir or Madam, I had booked a Lyon-Lisbon flight with your airline for 12 June, at 6 pm. On the day of the flight, at the airport, the flight was cancelled. There was only one flight left, the next morning at 7 am. At the desk, I was offered neither a hotel nor a meal. So I had to pay for a night in a hotel near the airport: 120 euros. This is not acceptable. I attach to this email the hotel bill and my boarding pass as proof. I would be grateful if you could refund the hotel night as soon as possible, and tell me whether I am entitled to compensation. Thanking you in advance, Kind regards, Marc Dubois.)

## How the email uses the grammar

- In *j'avais réservé*, the plus-que-parfait shows that the booking came before the problem.
- In *le vol a été annulé*, the passive puts the result first, because what matters is the cancellation, not who decided it.
- In *il ne restait qu'un vol* and *ni hôtel ni repas*, restriction and double negation say exactly what was missing.
- In *j'ai donc été obligé de payer*, *donc* links the cause to its consequence, and *être obligé de* shows that Marc had no choice.
- The email follows the format of a formal email: *l'objet* with the reference number, *Madame, Monsieur,*, the facts, the attachments, the request, and the closing.

## Useful phrases for each part

| Part | Phrase |
|---|---|
| The subject line | *Vol du… annulé, numéro de dossier…* |
| What you had done | *J'avais réservé…* / *J'avais confirmé…* |
| What went wrong | *Le vol a été annulé.* / *Le chauffage est en panne depuis…* |
| The cost | *J'ai été obligé de payer…* |
| The proof | *Je joins à ce mail…* / *Vous trouverez ci-joint…* |
| The request | *Je vous serais reconnaissant de…* |

## Your turn

Choose one problem: a cancelled flight, a lost suitcase, or a flat with a problem your landlord hasn't fixed. Write a complaint email of eight to ten sentences, on paper or in a notes app.

1. Give an *objet* with a reference number or the address.
2. Say what you had done, with the plus-que-parfait, and what went wrong.
3. Give the cost, and say what you're attaching as proof.
4. Make one clear request, and say what you'll do next if nothing happens, with *sinon*.

Try to use *ne… que* or *ni… ni* once, and one passive.

## Check your work

- Did you use *vous* all the way through, with no informal words?
- Is there a date for each fact?
- After *ne… que*, did you keep the article, and after *ni*, did you drop *un*, *une* and *des*?
- Did you close with *En vous remerciant par avance,* and *Bien cordialement*?

<Callout type="warning" title="Joindre, not attacher">
  In English, you "attach" a file. In French, **attacher** means to tie or fasten something, like a seat belt. For an email, use **joindre**: *Je joins la facture*, or the fixed phrase **Vous trouverez ci-joint la facture**. *J'attache la facture* sounds as if you've tied it to something.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Reference number in the subject line">
  Whenever you write to a company, put your *numéro de dossier*, order number or flat address in *l'objet*, and repeat it in the first sentence. Companies sort their emails by these numbers, and an email that can be matched to a file at once is answered much faster.
</Callout>
