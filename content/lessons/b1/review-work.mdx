## A model cover letter

Thomas works in a bank, and he wants to change career and work in a bookshop.

*Madame, Monsieur,*

*Je me permets de vous adresser ma candidature au poste de vendeur dans votre librairie. Votre librairie est celle que je préfère à Lyon, et j'aimerais beaucoup y travailler.*

*Actuellement, je travaille dans une banque depuis cinq ans, mais je souhaite me reconvertir : j'adore les livres depuis toujours. Mes qualités ? Je suis rigoureux, ponctuel et j'aime travailler en équipe. Je suis aussi ambitieux : à l'avenir, j'aimerais organiser des soirées avec des auteurs.*

*Je me tiens à votre disposition pour un entretien. Je vous remercie de votre attention.*

*Je vous prie d'agréer, Madame, Monsieur, l'expression de mes salutations distinguées.*

*Thomas Martin*

(Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing to apply for the position of sales assistant in your bookshop. Your bookshop is my favourite in Lyon, and I would very much like to work there. I have been working in a bank for five years, but I would like to change career: I have always loved books. My qualities? I am thorough, punctual, and I enjoy working in a team. I am also ambitious: in the future, I would like to organize evenings with authors. I am available for an interview. Thank you for your time. Yours faithfully, Thomas Martin)

## Model interview answers

Here are Thomas's answers to three questions he's likely to hear.

- **Recruiter:** *Parlez-moi de vous.* (Tell me about yourself.)
- **Thomas:** *Ça fait cinq ans que je travaille dans une banque. Je suis chargé des clients. Mais je veux changer de vie : une reconversion, c'est un défi, et j'aime les défis.* (I've been working in a bank for five years. I'm in charge of customers. But I want to change my life: a career change is a challenge, and I like challenges.)
- **Recruiter:** *Quel est votre défaut ?* (What's your weakness?)
- **Thomas:** *Je lis trop ! Mes collègues me prêtent leurs romans, et je les leur rends toujours en retard.* (I read too much! My colleagues lend me their novels, and I always give them back late.)
- **Recruiter:** *Pourquoi notre librairie ?* (Why our bookshop?)
- **Thomas:** *Parce que les autres librairies sont bien, mais la vôtre est celle où je passe tous mes samedis.* (Because the other bookshops are fine, but yours is the one where I spend all my Saturdays.)

## Useful phrases for each part

| Part | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Opening the letter | *Je me permets de vous adresser ma candidature au poste de…* |
| Your situation | *Actuellement, je travaille…* / *Ça fait… que je…* |
| Your qualities | *Je suis rigoureux, ponctuel et j'aime travailler en équipe.* |
| Your wish | *Je serais ravi de…* / *J'aimerais beaucoup…* |
| Closing | *Je me tiens à votre disposition pour un entretien.* |

## How Thomas uses the grammar

- In *les autres librairies… la vôtre*, he avoids repeating *librairie* with a possessive pronoun.
- In *celle que je préfère* and *celle où je passe mes samedis*, he uses a demonstrative pronoun with a relative.
- In *je les leur rends*, he replaces both *les romans* and *mes collègues* with two pronouns.
- In *ça fait cinq ans que je travaille*, he says how long he's been doing his job.

## Your turn

Choose a job you'd really like, and write two pieces, on paper or in a notes app.

1. **A cover letter** of eight to ten sentences, with the three parts (*vous*, *moi*, *nous*), the formal opening and the full closing formula.
2. **Three interview answers**: to *Parlez-moi de vous*, to *Quel est votre point fort ?* and to *Pourquoi notre entreprise ?* Use *ça fait… que* once, and try to fit in one double pronoun, one possessive pronoun and one *celui* or *celle*.

## Check your work

- Does the letter use *vous* all the way through, with no informal words?
- Did you use *actuellement* for "currently", not for "actually"?
- In your double pronouns, is *le*, *la* or *les* before *lui* or *leur*, but after *me*, *te* or *vous*?
- Does each possessive and demonstrative pronoun agree with the thing, not the person?

<Callout type="warning" title="Une librairie isn't a library">
  **Une librairie** is a bookshop: you buy books there. A library, where you borrow them, is **une bibliothèque**. The same trap appears in the word for the person: *un libraire* sells books, and *un bibliothécaire* works in a library. If your dream job is with books, choose the right word in your letter.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Recycle your letter in the interview">
  Recruiters often start from your cover letter, so your interview answers should match it. Take each quality you wrote in the letter and prepare one example for it, in one or two sentences. In the interview, you'll then be repeating ideas you've already written, which makes you sound consistent, and calmer.
</Callout>
