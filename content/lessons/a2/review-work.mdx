## A model: your working life

Here's a nurse called Fatima, describing her working life. Look at each time expression, and the tense that goes with it.

<Example fr="Je suis infirmière à l'hôpital de Nantes." en="I'm a nurse at the hospital in Nantes." />
<Example fr="Je travaille ici depuis cinq ans." en="I've been working here for five years." />
<Example fr="Il y a dix ans, j'ai commencé mes études." en="Ten years ago, I started my studies." />
<Example fr="J'ai étudié à Rennes pendant trois ans." en="I studied in Rennes for three years." />
<Example fr="C'est une entreprise importante et mes collègues sont sympas." en="It's an important company and my colleagues are nice." />
<Example fr="Je sais parler anglais et je connais bien Nantes." en="I can speak English and I know Nantes well." />
<Example fr="Je viens de finir un stage, et je suis en train de chercher du travail." en="I've just finished an internship, and I'm looking for work." />

Notice *Je travaille ici depuis cinq ans*, in the present, because it's still true. *J'ai étudié pendant trois ans* is in the passé composé, because it's finished.

## Your turn

Write a short paragraph about your job or studies, on paper or in a notes app. It can be true or invented. Aim for seven or eight sentences, and include:

1. what you do, with *je suis* and no article
2. where you work or study
3. how long you've been doing it, with *depuis*
4. something that lasted a certain time, with *pendant*
5. when something began, with *il y a*
6. a skill, with *savoir*, and a person or place, with *connaître*
7. something you've just done, with *venir de*

Then write a short phone message, leaving your name and your number for someone who isn't available. Read your number in pairs.

## Check your work

Go through your paragraph once for each question below.

- Is there no article after *je suis* before a plain job title?
- Did you use *c'est* with a small word and a noun, and *il est* or *elle est* otherwise?
- Is *depuis* with the present, and *il y a* with the passé composé?
- Did you use *savoir* for skills and facts, and *connaître* for people and places?

<Callout type="warning" title="Depuis takes the present">
  In a paragraph about your work, the past tense can spread. *J'ai travaillé ici depuis cinq ans* is wrong. If you still work there, it's **Je travaille ici depuis cinq ans**. If you left, and you're saying how long it lasted, it's **J'ai travaillé ici pendant cinq ans**. Read your *depuis* sentences one more time.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Draw your timeline">
  Before you write about your working life, draw a line across a page. Mark the moment you started, what you did for a while, and today. Each mark suggests a phrase: *Il y a…* for the start, *pendant…* for a closed period, and *depuis…* for what's still going on. Then write one sentence for each mark.
</Callout>
