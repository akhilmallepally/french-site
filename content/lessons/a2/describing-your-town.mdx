## Building a description

You can now combine several structures in one description.

**Your home.** Use the words from <LessonLink id="a2-home-and-chores" text="the lesson on home and household chores" />, and *qui*, *que* and *où* from <LessonLink id="a2-relative-qui-que-ou" text="the lesson on qui, que and où" />.

<Example fr="J'habite dans un appartement qui a trois pièces." en="I live in a flat that has three rooms." />

**Your neighbourhood.** *Il y a* tells you what's there, and *près de* and *loin de* tell you where.

<Example fr="Il y a une boulangerie près de chez moi." en="There's a bakery near my place." />

**Your town.** A superlative, from <LessonLink id="a2-superlatives" text="the lesson on superlatives" />, can pick out the highlight.

<Example fr="C'est la plus belle ville de la région." en="It's the most beautiful town in the region." />

## A model description

*J'habite à Rennes depuis 2021. Mon appartement est au deuxième étage d'un immeuble qui est près du centre-ville. Il y a trois pièces et un petit balcon. Mon quartier est calme, mais le centre-ville est très animé. C'est la ville que je préfère.* (I've lived in Rennes since 2021. My flat is on the second floor of a building that's near the town centre. There are three rooms and a small balcony. My neighbourhood is quiet, but the town centre is very lively. It's the town I prefer.)

## Comparing with where you used to live

To talk about somewhere you lived before, use the imparfait from <LessonLink id="a2-imparfait-formation" text="the lesson on the imparfait" />. Then bring in *maintenant* for the contrast.

<Example fr="Avant, j'habitais à Nantes. Maintenant, j'habite à Lille." en="I used to live in Nantes. Now I live in Lille." />
<Example fr="J'ai habité à Lyon pendant deux ans." en="I lived in Lyon for two years." />

*Pendant* and *depuis* come from <LessonLink id="a2-depuis-pendant-il-y-a" text="the lesson on depuis, pendant and il y a" />.

## A model comparison

*Avant, j'habitais à Paris. C'était une grande ville, très animée, mais bruyante. Maintenant, j'habite dans un village qui est plus calme et plus propre. Il y a moins de magasins, mais les gens sont plus sympas. Je vais rester ici.* (I used to live in Paris. It was a big city, very lively, but noisy. Now I live in a village that's quieter and cleaner. There are fewer shops, but the people are nicer. I'm going to stay here.)

## Your turn

Write six to eight sentences about where you live now and somewhere you've lived before, or an invented place. Use at least one comparative, one superlative, and one sentence with *qui*, *que* or *où*.

## En français

- *Tu habites où maintenant ?* (Where do you live now?)
- *Dans un petit appartement, en banlieue.* (In a small flat, in the suburbs.)
- *C'est plus pratique que le centre-ville ?* (Is it more convenient than the town centre?)
- *Non, mais c'est plus calme et moins cher.* (No, but it's quieter and cheaper.)

<Callout type="warning" title="Make the adjective match the place">
  It's easy to learn *animé* and use it everywhere. The ending depends on the noun: **le quartier est animé**, but **la ville est animée**, and **les rues sont animées**. With a comparison, it's the same: **cette ville est plus animée que l'autre**. Check the noun first.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Zoom in as you describe">
  A description is easy to organize if you work like a camera: start wide and move closer. Begin with the town, then the neighbourhood, then the building, then your home. Each step gives you new words, and your listener can follow where you are. Zoom out again at the end, with how you feel about the place.
</Callout>
