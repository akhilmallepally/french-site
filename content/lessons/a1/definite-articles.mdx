Every French noun is either **masculine** or **feminine**. The word for "the" changes to match it. This is one of the first habits to build: learn each noun *with* its article.

You've already met some nouns without noticing: in <LessonLink id="a1-greetings" />, *bonjour* is just *bon* + *jour* (good + day), and *le jour* is masculine.

## The three forms

<VocabTable group="articles" />

Before a vowel or a silent *h*, both **le** and **la** shorten to **l'**: *l'ami* (the friend), *l'heure* (the hour).

<VocabTable group="words" />

<Callout type="note">
  Gender is mostly about the word, not the meaning. A table isn't "female" in any real sense. It's just a feminine noun.
</Callout>
