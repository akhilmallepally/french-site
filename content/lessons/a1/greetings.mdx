Greetings are the first thing you'll say in any French conversation, and the French take them seriously. Walking into a shop without saying *bonjour* is considered rude.

## Formal vs informal

French has two registers. The **formal** one is for strangers, shopkeepers, colleagues, and anyone older than you. The **informal** one is for friends, family, and people your own age in casual settings.

- **Formal:** *bonjour*, *au revoir*, *s'il vous plaît*
- **Informal:** *salut* (works for both hello and goodbye), *s'il te plaît*

<Callout type="tip" title="When in doubt, be formal">
  Nobody will be offended if you say **bonjour** to a friend. Saying **salut** to a stranger can sound too familiar.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Bonsoir is mainly a greeting">
  *Bonsoir* is mainly an evening hello, but you'll sometimes hear it as a goodbye late in the day. If you're unsure, *au revoir* or *bonne soirée* always works.
</Callout>
