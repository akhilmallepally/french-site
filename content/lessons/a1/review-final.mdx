You've reached the end of A1. *Félicitations !* This lesson looks back over the whole level, unit by unit, so you can see how far you've come and spot anything you'd like to practise again.

## Your can-do checklist

Read each line and be honest with yourself. If one feels shaky, follow the link and spend a few minutes there.

**Getting started.** I can:

- greet people, and choose between *tu* and *vous*
- spell my name and give my age, nationality and phone number
- use *être* with every pronoun

Go back to <LessonLink id="a1-review-getting-started" text="the Getting started review" /> if you need to.

**People and things.** I can:

- use the right article with a noun, and make it plural
- talk about my family, with *mon*, *ma* and *mes*
- use *avoir*, and describe people with adjectives that match

Go back to <LessonLink id="a1-review-people" text="the People and things review" /> if you need to.

**Everyday life.** I can:

- use regular verbs, and make them negative
- say what I like and don't like
- give the date and the time, and describe my day

Go back to <LessonLink id="a1-review-everyday-life" text="the Everyday life review" /> if you need to.

**Free time.** I can:

- talk about hobbies and sports with *jouer* and *faire*
- ask yes/no questions and use question words
- talk about the weather and the seasons

Go back to <LessonLink id="a1-review-free-time" text="the Free time review" /> if you need to.

**Food and shopping.** I can:

- order in a café, and say "some" with *du* and *de la*
- understand the shape of a French meal
- buy clothes, talk about colours and prices, and use *ce* and *cette*

Go back to <LessonLink id="a1-review-food-and-shopping" text="the Food and shopping review" /> if you need to.

**Getting around.** I can:

- name places in town, say where they are, and ask the way
- give instructions and make suggestions
- talk about plans, and about what I can, want to and must do
- write a short text or a postcard

Go back to <LessonLink id="a1-writing-a-short-message" text="the lesson on writing a short message" /> if you need to.

## Your turn

Write one last piece about yourself, on paper or in a notes app. Aim for around ten sentences, and use at least one thing from each unit. You could include:

1. who you are, where you live and how old you are
2. someone in your family, with a description
3. what you do on a typical day
4. what you like doing when the weather's good and bad
5. your favourite thing to eat or drink
6. a plan for next weekend, and one thing you have to do

These words may help you to talk about your progress.

<Callout type="warning" title="Five last mistakes to check for">
  Read through your paragraph and look for these. Age and hunger use *avoir*: **J'ai vingt ans**. The weather uses *il fait*: **Il fait froid**. *Son* and *sa* match the noun, not the owner: **sa mère**, even for a man's mother. Liking takes *le*, and eating takes *du*: **J'aime le pain**, **Je mange du pain**. And *un*, *une* and *des* change to *de* after a negative verb: **Je n'ai pas de voiture**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Teach one rule to someone">
  The fastest way to find out if you really know something is to explain it. Pick one rule from A1, such as *à + le = au* or when to use *si*, and explain it to a friend or even out loud to yourself. If you get stuck halfway through, you've found exactly what to review.
</Callout>
