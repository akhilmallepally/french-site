## 13. b1-plus-que-parfait: The plus-que-parfait

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 13
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-passe-compose-vs-imparfait: the plus-que-parfait sits behind both past tenses.
- **Teaches:** formation (imparfait of *avoir* / *être* + participle), the same auxiliary and agreement rules as the passé composé, reflexives (*je m'étais levé*), the past before the past, with *déjà* and *pas encore* (revisited from a2-time-expressions-past). Reuses: childhood memories, travel stories.
- **Not yet taught:** *si* + plus-que-parfait (b2-si-plus-que-parfait): avoided. *Il venait de partir* (*venir de* in the imparfait) isn't in the syllabus: see the overview, section (g).
- **Vocab (13):**
  - *j'avais* + participle: I had (done)
  - *j'étais* + participle: I had (gone, arrived…)
  - *je m'étais* + participle: I had (got up, washed…)
  - *le réveil*: the alarm clock
  - *un oubli*: an oversight, something forgotten
  - *trop tard*: too late
  - *plus tôt*: earlier
  - *auparavant*: before, previously
  - *le contrôleur / la contrôleuse*: the ticket inspector
  - *fermer à clé*: to lock
  - *se rendre compte que*: to realize that
  - *s'apercevoir que*: to notice that
  - *disparaître*: to disappear
- **Dialogue:** A friend explains why they missed the train: *quand je suis arrivé, le train était déjà parti*.
- **Production tasks:** translate "When I arrived, the film had already started."; rewrite three sentences to show what had happened first.
- **Needs review:** "The plus-que-parfait uses the same auxiliary and agreement rules as the passé composé." "English often uses the simple past where French needs the plus-que-parfait."

## 14. b1-time-connectors: Time connectors

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 14
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-plus-que-parfait: connectors order events, often across the plus-que-parfait.
- **Teaches:** *quand*, *lorsque*, *pendant que*, *dès que* / *aussitôt que* (with the futur for the future), *au moment où*, *avant de* + infinitive, *après* + noun, *après avoir* + participle as a chunk, *jusqu'à*, *à partir de*. Reuses: anecdotes, daily routine.
- **Not yet taught:** *après avoir / être* + participle as grammar (b2-infinitif-passe): fixed chunk, listed in review-needed.md. *avant que* + subjunctive (b2-subjunctive-conjunctions): avoided. The gérondif (b1-gerondif): avoided.
- **Vocab (13):**
  - *lorsque*: when (more formal)
  - *pendant que*: while
  - *dès que*: as soon as
  - *aussitôt que*: as soon as
  - *au moment où*: just as, at the moment when
  - *avant de* + infinitive: before …ing
  - *après avoir* + participle: after …ing (fixed phrase for now)
  - *jusqu'à*: until
  - *à partir de*: from
  - *tout de suite*: straight away
  - *finalement*: in the end
  - *aussitôt*: immediately
  - *entre-temps*: in the meantime
- **Dialogue:** Someone describes a disastrous morning: *dès que je me suis levé, tout est allé de travers*.
- **Production tasks:** join pairs of sentences with *avant de* and *dès que*; translate "As soon as I get home, I'll call you."
- **Needs review:** "After *dès que* or *quand* about the future, French uses the futur where English uses the present." "*Lorsque* is more formal than *quand*."

## 15. b1-telling-an-anecdote: Telling an anecdote

- **Type:** communication · **Order:** 15
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-time-connectors: the connectors that move the story on.
  - a2-telling-a-story: the scene-setting (imparfait) and events (passé composé) pattern.
- **Teaches:** anecdote structure (setting, event, reaction, ending); building suspense (*et là…*, *tout à coup*, *figure-toi que*); listener reactions (*Ce n'est pas vrai !*, *Sans blague !*, *Et alors ?*); wrapping up (*bref*, *au final*).
- **Not yet taught:** reported speech in the past (*il m'a dit que…*, b2-reported-speech-past): avoided. The anecdote uses direct speech instead.
- **Vocab (17):**
  - *une anecdote*: an anecdote
  - *figure-toi que / figurez-vous que*: guess what, believe it or not
  - *et là*: and then, at that point
  - *Ce n'est pas vrai !*: No way!
  - *Sans blague !*: No kidding!
  - *Et alors ?*: And then what?
  - *alors*: so, then
  - *bref*: anyway, in short
  - *au final*: in the end
  - *incroyable*: unbelievable
  - *bizarre*: weird
  - *La honte !*: How embarrassing!
  - *Quelle horreur !*: How awful!
  - *Quelle chance !*: How lucky!
  - *deviner*: to guess
  - *raconter*: to tell (a story)
  - *un fou rire*: a fit of giggles
- **Dialogue:** Léa tells Karim how she got locked out of her flat; he reacts at each step.
- **Longer text:** A group-chat thread in which someone tells friends what happened at a wedding, with reactions after each message.
- **Production tasks:** rewrite a flat story using connectors and reactions; write out a six-sentence anecdote from prompts.
- **Needs review:** "*Figure-toi que* is informal." "*La honte !* is informal." "*Au final* is common in speech, but some people consider it incorrect."

## 16. b1-cinema-and-books: Cinema and books

- **Type:** vocabulary · **Order:** 16
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-expressing-opinions: describing films and books always comes with an opinion.
- **Teaches:** genres, people and plot vocabulary; *ça se passe à / en…*; *ça parle de…*; describing adjectives.
- **Not yet taught:** the passive (*réalisé par*, b1-passive-voice-intro): avoided; use *un film de* + name.
- **Vocab (22):**
  - *une comédie*: a comedy
  - *un drame*: a drama
  - *un film d'horreur*: a horror film
  - *un film policier*: a crime film
  - *un dessin animé*: a cartoon, an animated film
  - *un documentaire*: a documentary
  - *un roman*: a novel
  - *un roman policier*: a crime novel
  - *une bande dessinée / une BD*: a comic book
  - *un auteur / une autrice*: an author
  - *un réalisateur / une réalisatrice*: a director
  - *la distribution*: the cast
  - *un personnage*: a character
  - *le personnage principal*: the main character
  - *l'intrigue*: the plot
  - *une scène*: a scene
  - *Ça se passe à…*: It's set in…
  - *Ça parle de…*: It's about…
  - *émouvant / émouvante*: moving
  - *passionnant / passionnante*: gripping
  - *les sous-titres*: subtitles
  - *en version originale / en VO*: in the original language
- **Dialogue:** Two friends choose a film at the cinema, discussing the genre and what each one is about.
- **Production tasks:** translate "It's set in Paris in 1960."; write out a four-sentence description of a book.
- **Needs review:** "*Autrice* is now the usual feminine of *auteur*." "*En VO* means in the original language, usually with subtitles."

## 17. b1-reviewing-a-film-or-book: Reviewing a film or book

- **Type:** communication · **Order:** 17
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-cinema-and-books: the vocabulary of the review.
  - b1-ce-qui-ce-que: *ce qui m'a plu, c'est…*.
- **Teaches:** review structure (presentation, summary in the present, opinion, recommendation); recommending (*à voir absolument*, *ça vaut le coup*) and warning off (*à éviter*); rating with stars.
- **Not yet taught:** *je vous le conseille* (double pronouns, b1-double-object-pronouns): fixed phrase, listed in review-needed.md. *Il faut que vous le voyiez* (subjunctive): avoided; use *il faut le voir*.
- **Vocab (16):**
  - *une critique*: a review
  - *un / une critique*: a critic
  - *recommander*: to recommend
  - *à voir absolument*: a must-see
  - *à éviter*: best avoided
  - *ça vaut le coup*: it's worth it
  - *le scénario*: the screenplay
  - *le jeu des acteurs*: the acting
  - *les effets spéciaux*: the special effects
  - *la bande originale*: the soundtrack
  - *décevant / décevante*: disappointing
  - *réussi / réussie*: well made, successful
  - *raté / ratée*: botched, a failure
  - *une étoile*: a star (in a rating)
  - *le public*: the audience
  - *Je vous le conseille.*: I recommend it (fixed phrase for now)
- **Dialogue:** Two friends leaving the cinema compare their verdicts.
- **Longer text:** A 150-word online review of a French film, with a star rating and a full translation.
- **Production tasks:** write out a short review from prompts; translate "What I liked was the music."
- **Needs review:** "*Une critique* is a review, and *un critique* is a critic." "*Ça vaut le coup* is informal but common in reviews."

## 18. b1-culture-french-cinema: French cinema

- **Type:** culture · **Order:** 18
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-cinema-and-books: the vocabulary for talking about films.
- **Teaches:** la Nouvelle Vague; popular comedies; the Cannes festival and the Palme d'or; the César awards; going to the cinema in France (new films on Wednesdays).
- **Not yet taught:** the passive (*a été réalisé par*): avoided; use *un film de* and active verbs.
- **Vocab (14):**
  - *la Nouvelle Vague*: the French New Wave
  - *un festival*: a festival
  - *la Palme d'or*: the Palme d'or (top prize at Cannes)
  - *les César*: the César awards
  - *un prix*: a prize, an award (new meaning: a1-plural-nouns teaches *le prix* as "the price")
  - *récompenser*: to award, to reward
  - *le jury*: the jury
  - *un succès*: a hit, a success
  - *le box-office*: the box office
  - *une entrée*: a cinema admission
  - *une sortie*: a release
  - *une salle de cinéma*: a cinema screen, a cinema
  - *tourner un film*: to shoot a film
  - *un tournage*: a film shoot
- **Dialogue:** Two friends discuss whether to watch a Truffaut classic or a recent comedy.
- **Production tasks:** translate "The film came out in 1959."; write out three sentences about a French film.
- **Needs review (facts):** the Nouvelle Vague dates (late 1950s to 1960s); *Les Quatre Cents Coups* (1959); Cannes takes place in May; the César awards started in 1976; new films come out on Wednesdays in France; *Bienvenue chez les Ch'tis* (2008) and *Intouchables* (2011) are among the most-watched French films in France.

## 19. b1-review-stories: Review: Telling stories

- **Type:** review · **Order:** 19
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-telling-an-anecdote: the anecdote task.
  - b1-reviewing-a-film-or-book: the review task.
  - b1-culture-french-cinema: the film reviewed in the model.
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *un récit*: an account, a story
  - *un narrateur / une narratrice*: a narrator
  - *inoubliable*: unforgettable
  - *marquant / marquante*: memorable, striking
  - *un moment fort*: a highlight
  - *une surprise*: a surprise
  - *une aventure*: an adventure
  - *vivre une aventure*: to have an adventure
  - *un rebondissement*: a twist
  - *captivant / captivante*: captivating
  - *dès le début*: from the start
  - *à la fin*: at the end
- **Model text:** An anecdote about a trip where everything went wrong, then a six-sentence review of a French film.
- **Production tasks:** two write-out paragraphs (anecdote and review) and one translation.
- **Needs review:** none beyond the unit's lessons.
