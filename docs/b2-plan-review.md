# B2 plan: decisions for review

The plan is in docs/plans/b2/: unit-1.md to unit-8.md, plus overview.md, which has the checks. It covers 59 lessons; ids and types match the syllabus. For each decision below, the recommendation is marked **Rec**.

**Checks that came back clean:**
- no prerequisite cycles
- no lesson over 6 prerequisites (the final review is exempt)
- no planned B2 word used before the lesson that defines it
- every lesson within 15 to 30 vocab entries (reviews 5 to 6)
- every exercise plan within the B2 mix (14 to 16 exercises, at least 3 production, 1 reorder, 1 reformulation)
- no repeat of an A1 to B1 entry, apart from the new meanings in decision 15

1. **Longer-text length.** B1 longer texts average 89 French words (range 45 to 141); the convention proposed 250 to 400.
   - Options: (a) 250 to 400; (b) 150 to 250; (c) 180 to 300.
   - **Rec (b):** a clear step up from B1 that keeps communication lessons under the 1,800-word cap.
2. **Nominalisation (planned 41) comes after the lessons that need it.** Summarising (21), presenting data (36) and the report (37) all need nominal style.
   - Options: (a) move b2-nominalisation into Unit 3, just before summarising, with news vocabulary; (b) keep the order and allow labelled nominal chunks; (c) a mini-section in 21.
   - **Rec (a).**
3. **Logical connectors (42) overlap concession (4) and come after the report (37).**
   - Options: (a) 4 owns opposition and concession (with *en revanche*); 42 owns addition, explanation and consequence (*bref* is B1); 42 moves to Unit 1 after 4; (b) split the same way but keep 42 in Unit 6, and restrict 37 and 38 to B1 connectors.
   - **Rec (a).**
4. **Regret and reproach (11) needs the subjonctif passé (13).**
   - Options: (a) reorder Unit 2 to 11 futur antérieur, 12 subjonctif passé, 13 regret; (b) keep the order and label *je regrette que tu sois parti*.
   - **Rec (a).** Either way, *regretter d'avoir* is a labelled fixed phrase (b2-infinitif-passe teaches it, at 33).
5. **Pour que appears in both 3 and 5.**
   - Options: (a) 5 owns all purpose, including *pour que*; (b) 3 keeps it.
   - **Rec (a):** 3 has six conjunctions without it.
6. **Relatives 24 and 25 overlap, and 4 is too big.**
   - Options: (a) 24 takes the *lequel* family and *n'importe lequel*; 25 takes *qui* / *quoi* / *ce dont* and all the concessive relatives (*quoi que*, *qui que*, *où que*), moved out of 4; (b) merge 24 and 25 into one lesson and add a new grammar lesson elsewhere.
   - **Rec (a).** The plans already follow it.
7. **Passive overlaps.**
   - Options: (a) 19 covers all tenses and *par* / *de*; 20 owns avoiding the passive; 31 owns *se faire* + infinitive; (b) keep the syllabus wording.
   - **Rec (a).**
8. **Lessons at the grammar limit (1,200 words).**
   - 16 (four tense shifts plus time words): move the time words to 18 (vocabulary).
   - 31 (*faire*, *se faire*, *laisser*, pronouns): shorten *laisser* to a brief section, practised in 38.
   - **Rec:** both; alternatively, keep them as planned and trim during writing.
9. **The passé simple goes in 46, a vocabulary lesson already at 1,100 words.**
   - Options: (a) about 150 words, 3rd person only, plus 2 recognition exercises; (b) a separate section in 47 instead.
   - **Rec (a):** 47, 51 and 52 can then use it in glossed texts.
10. **History (47) has six topics.**
    - Options: (a) five topics (Napoleon, the World Wars, decolonization, Europe; the Revolution only as a recap); (b) all six, with shorter paragraphs.
    - **Rec (a):** the Third and Fourth Republics are cut.
11. **Registers (53), colloquial French (54) and slang (55) overlap.**
    - Options: (a) 53 takes register triplets and formal structures; 54 takes spoken grammar and fillers; 55 takes verlan and slang words; (b) the syllabus wording, where 54 also has "common slang".
    - **Rec (a).**
12. **Hedging appears in both 1 and 58.**
    - Options: (a) 1 owns hedging; 58 keeps persuasion, rhetorical questions and strategic concession; (b) 58 reviews hedging at length.
    - **Rec (a).**
13. **False friends (56):** B1 already teaches *actuellement*, *librairie* and *portable*, and 10 B2 lessons teach one each.
    - Options: (a) a recap table for those plus 18 new false friends; (b) reteach the classics.
    - **Rec (a).**
14. **Placing the inventory gaps.** Planned:
    - concordance des temps (16, 13)
    - expletive *ne* (3, 5)
    - *n'importe lequel* (24)
    - *quiconque* / *quelconque* (49)
    - extended cause and consequence (42)
    - *depuis que* (12)
    - concessive relatives (25)
    - neutral *le* (27)
    - complex and stylistic inversion and *ne… guère* (53)
    - *au cas où* (10)
    - *faillir* (9)
    - Not yet planned: *le fait que* + subjunctive and *soit… soit* (proposed for 42), and *à* + infinitive (*maison à vendre*; proposed for 20).
    - **Rec:** accept, and add the three to those lessons.
15. **Allowlists.** 7 entries reuse an A1 to B1 `fr` with a new meaning: *un bilan*, *un entretien*, *le marché*, *près de*, *un état des lieux*, *quoi*, *trop*. The plans also list about 60 cognates.
    - **Rec:** add both lists to scripts/b2-lists.ts as each unit is written.
16. **Sensitive topics:** immigration (2, 3), decolonization and Algeria (47), attacks on the satirical press (22), ethnic slang (55).
    - Options: (a) neutral facts only; no figures; no attacks in 22; no ethnic terms in 55; (b) leave them to each unit's plan.
    - **Rec (a).**
17. **Grammar lessons need 15 vocab entries** (B1 had 12). The plans reach 15 by adding topic words.
    - Options: (a) keep 15; (b) allow 12 for grammar lessons.
    - **Rec (b):** it's less padding. If (a), the plans stand as they are.
18. **Prerequisite changes to the syllabus**, for "Structures used": 11, 12, 13, 16, 21, 26, 31, 36, 37, 47 (listed in overview.md, section 3).
    - **Rec:** apply them with decisions 2 to 4 in one syllabus update.
19. **Whole-lesson cap.** Lessons 21, 37, 44 and 57 come close to 1,800 words because of their longer texts.
    - **Rec:** decide with decision 1. With (b), they fit.
