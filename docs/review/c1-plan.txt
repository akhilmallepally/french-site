# C1 plan: overview

Status: plan only. No C1 lesson file is written. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions" (on top of the B2 conventions). Coverage of A1 to B2: docs/c1-coverage.md, checked with `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/c1-find.ts "phrase"`. Lesson numbers follow docs/syllabus.md.

## Shape

57 lessons in 8 units, with exactly one review at the end of each unit (the last unit ends with its review and the final review).

| Unit | Title | Lessons | Count | Plan file |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Literary French | 1 to 8 | 8 | unit-1.md |
| 2 | Style and emphasis | 9 to 17 | 9 | unit-2.md |
| 3 | Academic writing | 18 to 25 | 8 | unit-3.md |
| 4 | Debate and rhetoric | 26 to 32 | 7 | unit-4.md |
| 5 | Professional mastery | 33 to 38 | 6 | unit-5.md |
| 6 | Ideas and society | 39 to 44 | 6 | unit-6.md |
| 7 | Living the language | 45 to 50 | 6 | unit-7.md |
| 8 | Towards DALF C1 | 51 to 57 | 7 | unit-8.md |

**Vocab in the plans.** Each lesson's "key vocab" lists headline items only. The writer fills the lesson to the minimum (12 entries in grammar lessons, 15 elsewhere) with words used in complete sentences. Items marked "(recap)" are taught earlier and are not new words. Review lessons have 3 to 8 truly new words.

## Writing order

1. Unit 2 (clear grammar rules; mostly easier), then Unit 1 lessons 1 to 4 (the literary and narrative tenses).
2. Unit 3 (it now holds concession and hypothesis and formal relatives, which Units 4 and 8 use) and Unit 5.
3. Unit 4 and Unit 7.
4. Unit 1 lessons 5 to 8 and Unit 6 (literature, ideas and culture; the quotation and fact rules make these harder).
5. Unit 8 last, because its practice lessons and reviews draw on everything.

Before writing, extend the B2 checks to C1: test:b2-exercises and scripts/b2-links-ids.ts, the C1 rows in audit:content (lengths, résumé 120 to 180, long texts 250 to 400), the C1 structure rules in check:structures (imparfait du subjonctif, passé antérieur, passé surcomposé), and scripts/scan-alexandrines.ts for the poetry lesson.

## Decisions taken

- **8 units**, with these moves from the first plan: c1-future-in-the-past to Unit 1; c1-tricky-prepositions to Unit 2; c1-formal-relatives-and-apposition and c1-concession-and-hypothesis to Unit 3, so the debate and essay lessons can use them.
- **Hedging:** B2 already teaches the rumour conditionnel and hedging (b2-conditionnel-passe, b2-nuancing-opinions, b2-persuasion-and-hedging). What's new (*à en croire*, *selon toute vraisemblance*, *sous réserve de*) is too little for a lesson, so c1-conditionnel-journalistique is merged into c1-advanced-connectors ("Advanced connectors and academic hedging"). Total: 57 lessons.
- **The imparfait du subjonctif** is never required in a typed answer; where it's possible, the present or passé subjunctive is also accepted.
- **Quotations** come only from the named safe list in CLAUDE.md (authors who died before 1900), under 15 words, in our own translations. Later authors are paraphrased or pastiched, and labelled.
- **DALF practice** names no timings, task formats or scoring: "a short exposé from a short document", "a synthesis of two documents".

## Still open

- **Poetry:** if scan-alexandrines.ts can't verify a classical poem, the lesson uses free verse. Decide once the script exists.

# C1 Unit 1 plan: Literary French

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions". Key vocab lists headline items; fill to the minimum with new words; "(recap)" items aren't new.

## 1. c1-passe-simple: The passé simple (grammar · easier)

b2-review-final mentions this lesson as the start of C1 (as plain text; restore the LessonLink when the file exists).

- **Recap (2 sentences):** recognition of the 3rd person and *fut, eut, fit, vint, prit* from b2-arts-and-literature; the passé composé and imparfait from A2 and B1.
- **Teaches:**
  - the three regular patterns, all persons: *-ai, -as, -a, -âmes, -âtes, -èrent*; *-is, -is, -it, -îmes, -îtes, -irent*; *-us, -us, -ut, -ûmes, -ûtes, -urent*
  - full paradigms of *être (je fus)*, *avoir (j'eus)*, *faire (je fis)*, *venir / tenir (je vins, je tins)*, *voir (je vis)*
  - third person, for recognition, of the verbs a reader meets most: *dire (il dit)*, *prendre (il prit)*, *mettre (il mit)*, *pouvoir (il put)*, *vouloir (il voulut)*, *savoir (il sut)*, *croire (il crut)*, *lire (il lut)*, *vivre (il vécut)*, *écrire (il écrivit)*, *connaître (il connut)*, *courir (il courut)*, *naître (il naquit)*, *mourir (il mourut)*
  - use: the narrative past of written stories and history; the passé simple for events, the imparfait for background; not in everyday speech or in modern dialogue
  - forms that look like the present (*il finit*, *il dit*) and how context decides
- **Key vocab (new):** *le récit* (the narrative), *s'écrier* (to exclaim), *se mettre à* (to start to), *à l'aube* (at dawn). Recaps: *soudain* (a2-passe-compose-vs-imparfait), *s'apercevoir que* (b1-plus-que-parfait), *aussitôt que* (b1-time-connectors), *le narrateur* (b2-arts-and-literature), *le lendemain* and *à ce moment-là* (b2-reporting-verbs). Fill to 12 with narrative verbs and time words.
- **Model examples:**
  - *Le roi entra, regarda la foule et se tut.* (The king came in, looked at the crowd and fell silent.)
  - *Ils partirent à l'aube et arrivèrent le soir.* (They left at dawn and arrived in the evening.)
  - *Nous fûmes surpris : personne ne nous attendait.* (We were surprised: no one was waiting for us.)
  - *« Attention ! » s'écria-t-elle, et tout le monde se mit à courir.* ("Watch out!" she cried, and everyone started running.)
  - *Elle naquit à Lyon en 1820.* (She was born in Lyon in 1820.) [invented person]
- **Exercises (16):** fill-in (forms, all persons); multiple-choice (passé simple or present: *il finit*); reformulation (passé composé → passé simple in a story); error-correction (*ils partèrent* → *partirent*); translate; connector-choice (passé simple or imparfait in a tale); reorder; a write-out of a five-sentence tale.
- **Links:** b2-arts-and-literature, b1-plus-que-parfait, a2-telling-a-story, b2-culture-french-history.

## 2. c1-passe-anterieur: The passé antérieur (grammar · easier)

- **Teaches:** formation (*il eut fini, elle fut partie*); use after *quand, lorsque, dès que, après que, aussitôt que, à peine… que* when the main verb is in the passé simple. Its spoken equivalent is the passé composé or the passé surcomposé (*quand il a eu fini, il est parti*), not the plus-que-parfait: the plus-que-parfait pairs with the imparfait (*quand il avait fini, il partait*).
- **Vocab (new):** *à peine… que*, *le passé surcomposé*. Recaps: *dès que* and *aussitôt que* (b1-time-connectors), *après que* (b2-subjunctive-conjunctions, text).
- **Exercises:** fill-in; multiple-choice (choose the tense by the main verb's tense: passé simple → passé antérieur; passé composé → surcomposé; imparfait → plus-que-parfait); reformulation; translate; reorder.
- **Links:** b1-plus-que-parfait, b1-time-connectors, b2-futur-anterieur.

## 3. c1-future-in-the-past: The future in the past and tense nuance (grammar · easier)

Moved from Unit 5: it belongs with the narrative tenses.

- **Recap:** the conditionnel as future in the past in reported speech (b2-reported-speech-past).
- **Teaches:** *allait* + infinitive versus the conditionnel; *devait* + infinitive (was to); the conditionnel passé as a future perfect in the past; the historical present in narrative.
- **Vocab (new):** *devait* (was to), *ne pas tarder à*, *le présent de narration*. Recap: *allait* + infinitive.
- **Exercises:** fill-in, reformulation, multiple-choice, translate.
- **Links:** b2-reported-speech-past, b2-futur-anterieur, b2-conditionnel-passe.

## 4. c1-imperfect-subjunctive: Imperfect and pluperfect subjunctive (grammar · harder)

- **Teaches:** recognition of *qu'il fût, qu'il eût, qu'il fît, qu'il vînt* and *qu'il eût fini*; where they appear (classic literature, very formal writing); their modern equivalents. Never required in a typed answer; where it's possible, the present or passé subjunctive is accepted.
- **Vocab (new):** *qu'il fût*, *qu'il eût*, *le registre littéraire*. Recap: *la concordance des temps* (b2-reported-speech-past).
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (identify), reformulation (to modern French), translate, fill-in with both forms accepted.
- **Links:** b2-subjonctif-passe, b2-reported-speech-past, b2-registers.

## 5. c1-reading-literary-narrative: Reading literary narrative (communication · harder)

- **Teaches:** reading a narrative in the passé simple: tense pairs, free indirect speech (*le discours indirect libre*), point of view.
- **Vocab (new):** *le point de vue*, *le discours indirect libre*, *l'intrigue*, *le dénouement*. Recap: *le personnage* (b1-cinema-and-books).
- **Longer text:** an original pastiche of a 19th-century short story, labelled as invented, 300 to 350 words.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (comprehension), reformulation (free indirect → reported speech), translate, write-out.
- **Links:** b2-arts-and-literature, b2-reported-speech-past.

## 6. c1-culture-literary-canon: The French literary canon (culture · harder)

- **Teaches:** a short neutral overview: the classical 17th century, the Enlightenment (link to Unit 6), the 19th-century novel, the 20th century; how French schools read these works.
- **Vocab (new):** *un classique*, *le roman réaliste*, *un courant littéraire*, *une œuvre majeure*.
- **Quotations:** only authors on the CLAUDE.md safe list (died before 1900), under 15 words, our own translations. 20th-century authors are named with their works and paraphrased, never quoted.
- **Links:** b2-arts-and-literature, a2-culture-french-school-system.
- **Flags:** facts and dates to review.

## 7. c1-culture-french-poetry: French poetry (culture · harder)

- **Teaches:** the alexandrine and the sonnet, rhyme and rhythm, reading a poem aloud (the mute *e*, the diérèse); two or three poets from the safe list.
- **Vocab (new):** *l'alexandrin*, *le sonnet*, *la césure*, *le e muet*, *la diérèse*.
- **Longer text:** an original poem in classical form, labelled as written for the lesson, with a commentary. Each line's syllable count (with the mute *e* and diérèse rules) is checked by scripts/scan-alexandrines.ts, written with the lesson; the lesson is listed in docs/review-needed.md for the native speaker. If a verse can't be verified, the poem is in free verse.
- **Quotations:** safe-list poets only, under 15 words, our own translations.
- **Links:** b2-arts-and-literature.

## 8. c1-review-literary: Review: Literary French (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *le style*, *une tournure*, *pasticher*. Recap: *soutenu* (b2-registers).
- **Model:** an original tale of 300 words in the passé simple and imparfait, with one passé antérieur and one recognized imparfait du subjonctif.
- **Exercises:** one per lesson of the unit, plus reformulation, error-correction and a write-out.

# C1 Unit 2 plan: Style and emphasis

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions". Mostly easier to write: rules are clear and B2 has the models.

## 9. c1-stylistic-inversion: Stylistic inversion (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** complex inversion and inversion after *peut-être*, *sans doute*, *aussi* (b2-registers).
- **Teaches:** inversion after *à peine*, *ainsi*, *du moins*, *encore*; after direct speech (*dit-il*, *répondit-elle*); when inversion is required, optional or wrong (a rule of thumb where usage varies).
- **Vocab (new):** *du moins*, *encore* (= even so), *toujours est-il que*. Recap: *à peine… que* (c1-passe-anterieur).
- **Exercises:** reformulation, error-correction, fill-in, multiple-choice, reorder.
- **Links:** b2-registers, a1-yes-no-questions.

## 10. c1-ne-expletif: The ne explétif (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** *avant que… ne*, *à moins que… ne* (b2-subjunctive-conjunctions); *avoir peur que… ne* (b1-subjonctif-wishes-emotions); *de crainte que* (b2-purpose-expressions).
- **Teaches:** after comparisons (*plus que je ne pensais*), *empêcher que*, *éviter que*; its register; how to tell it from a negation.
- **Vocab (new):** *empêcher que*, *il s'en faut de peu que*, *plus… que je ne*.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (negative or expletive?), fill-in, translate, reformulation.
- **Links:** b2-subjunctive-conjunctions, b2-purpose-expressions.

## 11. c1-literary-negation: Literary negation (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** *ne… guère* and *ne* alone with *pouvoir, savoir* (b2-registers); *ne… que* and *ni… ni* (b1-ne-que-ni-ni).
- **Teaches:** *ne… point*, *ne… nullement*, *ne… aucunement*, *nul ne*, *ne* alone with *oser* and *cesser*.
- **Vocab (new):** *ne… point*, *nullement*, *nul ne*, *je n'ose*.
- **Exercises:** register-shift (literary → standard), multiple-choice, fill-in, translate.
- **Links:** b2-registers, b1-ne-que-ni-ni.

## 12. c1-dislocation-and-fronting: Dislocation and fronting (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** *c'est… qui / que* and *ce qui…, c'est* (b2-mise-en-relief); the repeated subject in speech (b2-colloquial-french).
- **Teaches:** left and right dislocation (*ce film, je l'ai adoré*; *je l'ai adoré, ce film*); fronting in writing (*Rares sont ceux qui…*, *Reste la question de…*); choosing emphasis by register.
- **Vocab (new):** *rares sont ceux qui*, *reste à savoir*. Recap: *toujours est-il que* (lesson 9).
- **Exercises:** reformulation, register-shift, reorder, error-correction.
- **Links:** b2-mise-en-relief, b2-colloquial-french.

## 13. c1-adverb-placement: Adverb placement and nuance (grammar · easier)

- **Teaches:** adverbs that change meaning by position (*seulement*, *même*, *aussi*), sentence adverbs (*heureusement*, *visiblement*), long adverbs in compound tenses.
- **Vocab (new):** *visiblement*, *décidément*, *forcément*. Recap: *apparemment* (b1-conditionnel-present).
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice, reorder, reformulation, translate.
- **Links:** a2-adverbs-ment, b2-certainty-and-probability.

## 14. c1-tricky-prepositions: Tricky prepositions (grammar · easier)

Moved from Unit 6: it's a precision point, like the rest of this unit.

- **Teaches:** verbs with two constructions (*manquer à / manquer de*, *penser à / penser de*, *tenir à / tenir de*), prepositions with islands and regions, *de* versus *par* after the passive (recap of b2-passive-voice).
- **Vocab (new):** *manquer à*, *tenir de*, *penser de* (an opinion).
- **Exercises:** fill-in, error-correction, multiple-choice.
- **Links:** a2-verbs-with-a-and-de, b2-passive-voice, a2-prepositions-with-places.

## 15. c1-precise-verbs: Precise verbs (vocabulary · easier)

- **Teaches:** replacing *faire, mettre, dire, avoir* with precise verbs.
- **Vocab (new, 20 to 25):** *effectuer*, *établir*, *disposer de*, *aborder*, *susciter*, *engendrer*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation, fill-in, register-shift, translate.
- **Links:** b2-reporting-verbs, b2-nominalisation.

## 16. c1-collocations: Collocations (vocabulary · easier)

- **Teaches:** fixed noun + verb and noun + adjective pairs.
- **Vocab (new):** *émettre une hypothèse*, *une vive inquiétude*, *un rôle clé*, *prendre en compte*. Recap: *porter plainte* (b1-making-a-complaint, text).
- **Exercises:** connector-choice (the right verb), fill-in, error-correction, translate.
- **Links:** b2-science-and-research, b1-making-a-complaint.

## 17. c1-review-style: Review: Style and emphasis (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *la mise en valeur*, *un effet de style*, *la sobriété*. Recap: *sobre* (b2-describing-a-work-of-art).
- **Model:** a short editorial (300 words) using inversion, dislocation, a literary negation and precise verbs.

# C1 Unit 3 plan: Academic writing

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions". It now holds formal relatives and concession and hypothesis, so the debate (Unit 4) and the DALF practice (Unit 8) can use them.

## 18. c1-participle-clauses: Participle clauses (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** the participe présent, *ayant fini*, the gérondif and the verbal adjective (b2-participles-and-gerund).
- **Teaches:** the absolute participle clause (*La réunion terminée, chacun partit*), *étant donné* and *vu* as fixed participles, the dangling participle as a mistake (the test: the same subject).
- **Vocab (new):** *compte tenu de*, *une fois… terminé*. Recaps: *étant donné* (b2-participles-and-gerund), *vu*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation, error-correction (dangling participle), fill-in, translate.
- **Links:** b2-participles-and-gerund, b2-infinitif-passe.

## 19. c1-formal-relatives-and-apposition: Formal relatives and apposition (grammar · easier)

Moved from Unit 8.

- **Recap:** *dont* (b1-relative-dont), *lequel* and its forms, *au cours duquel* (b2-relative-lequel), *ce dont / ce à quoi* (b2-relative-prepositions).
- **Teaches:** *dont* + noun in long sentences (*l'auteur dont on connaît l'œuvre*), *duquel* after a preposition phrase (*à l'issue duquel*), apposition and detached adjectives (*Fatiguée, elle rentra*), commas in formal prose.
- **Vocab (new):** *à l'issue de laquelle*, *l'apposition*, *en raison duquel*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation (two sentences → one), error-correction, reorder.
- **Links:** b2-relative-lequel, b2-relative-prepositions, c1-participle-clauses.

## 20. c1-advanced-connectors: Advanced connectors and academic hedging (grammar · easier)

Merges the planned c1-conditionnel-journalistique: B2 already teaches the rumour conditionnel and hedging, and the new hedging items are too few for a lesson.

- **Recap:** the B2 connectors (docs/c1-coverage.md), including *or* (b2-logical-connectors) and *en revanche* (b2-concession-opposition), with C1 usage notes; the rumour conditionnel (b2-conditionnel-passe) and hedging (b2-nuancing-opinions, b2-persuasion-and-hedging).
- **Teaches (new):** *dès lors*, *en l'occurrence*, *a fortiori*, *à cet égard*; *par contre* versus *en revanche* as a register point; academic hedging with *à en croire*, *selon toute vraisemblance*, *sous réserve de*.
- **Vocab (new):** *dès lors*, *en l'occurrence*, *a fortiori*, *à cet égard*, *par contre*, *à en croire*, *selon toute vraisemblance*, *sous réserve de*.
- **Exercises:** connector-choice, reformulation (fact → hedged), error-correction, translate.
- **Links:** b2-logical-connectors, b2-concession-opposition, b2-conditionnel-passe.

## 21. c1-concession-and-hypothesis: Concession and hypothesis at C1 (grammar · harder)

Moved from Unit 8, after the connectors.

- **Recap:** *bien que*, *quoique*, *même si* (b2-concession-opposition); *si* + plus-que-parfait and mixed conditionals (b2-si-plus-que-parfait, b2-mixed-conditionals); *au cas où* + conditionnel (b2-mixed-conditionals, verified).
- **Teaches:** *quand bien même* + conditionnel; *pour peu que*, *à supposer que*, *si tant est que* + subjunctive; *encore que*; the inverted hypothesis, both literary: *Fût-il malade, il viendrait* (imparfait du subjonctif, recognition) and *Serait-il malade, il viendrait* (conditionnel).
- **Vocab (new):** *quand bien même*, *pour peu que*, *à supposer que*, *si tant est que*, *encore que*.
- **Exercises:** fill-in (mood, with forms that differ), reformulation, connector-choice, error-correction, translate.
- **Links:** b2-concession-opposition, b2-mixed-conditionals, c1-imperfect-subjunctive.

## 22. c1-abstract-nouns: Abstract nouns (vocabulary · easier)

- **Recap:** the suffixes from b2-nominalisation.
- **Teaches:** abstract nouns for argument; nominal style in academic writing.
- **Vocab (new, 20 to 25):** *la portée*, *la pertinence*, *la légitimité*, *l'ampleur*, *la démarche*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation (verb → noun), fill-in, translate.
- **Links:** b2-nominalisation.

## 23. c1-synthesis: The synthèse de documents (communication · harder)

- **Recap:** summarizing one article (b2-summarizing-an-article).
- **Teaches:** combining two or three documents without quoting them or giving your opinion; the plan and the neutral voice.
- **Longer texts:** two short original documents on a neutral subject (300 words in all) and a model synthesis.
- **Exercises:** reformulation, multiple-choice (which sentence is neutral), write-out.
- **Links:** b2-summarizing-an-article, b2-formal-report.

## 24. c1-dissertation: The French dissertation (communication · harder)

- **Recap:** the dialectical plan and *la problématique* (b2-argumentative-essay).
- **Teaches:** the C1 essay: a problématique that is usually a question opening a debate rather than a yes/no question (a rule of thumb); transitions; the *ouverture*; the analytical plan as an alternative; using concession and hypothesis (lesson 21).
- **Longer text:** an original model essay (350 to 400 words).
- **Links:** b2-argumentative-essay, b2-review-science, c1-concession-and-hypothesis.

## 25. c1-review-academic: Review: Academic writing (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *un corpus*, *mettre en perspective*, *un fil conducteur*. Recap: *la problématique* (b2-argumentative-essay).
- **Model:** a short synthesis paragraph and an essay introduction.

# C1 Unit 4 plan: Debate and rhetoric

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions".

## 26. c1-rhetorical-devices: Rhetorical devices (vocabulary · easier)

- **Teaches:** naming and using *l'anaphore*, *la gradation*, *l'antithèse* (recap of the essay term), *la question rhétorique*, *la litote*, *l'hyperbole*, with original examples.
- **Vocab (new):** *l'anaphore*, *la gradation*, *la litote*, *l'hyperbole*, *une figure de style*.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (identify the device), reformulation, write-out.
- **Links:** b2-persuasion-and-hedging, b2-argumentative-essay.

## 27. c1-subjunctive-nuances: Subjunctive nuances (grammar · harder)

- **Recap:** the B1 and B2 subjunctive lessons; *je ne pense pas que* / *je ne crois pas que* + subjunctive (b1-expressing-doubt-certainty, verified); *le seul, le premier* (b2-subjunctive-relative-clauses).
- **Teaches (new contrasts only):** *penser, croire* in questions (*pensez-vous qu'il vienne / qu'il viendra ?*); *que* + subjunctive at the start of a sentence (*Qu'il soit fatigué, je le comprends*); the mood that changes the meaning (*il comprend que tu sois parti* / *que tu es parti*; *dire que* / *dire de*).
- **Vocab (new):** *que… ou non*, *comprendre que* (two meanings), *admettre que* (two meanings).
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice, fill-in with forms that differ in the two moods, reformulation, error-correction.
- **Links:** b1-expressing-doubt-certainty, b2-subjunctive-relative-clauses, b2-certainty-and-probability.

## 28. c1-formal-debate: Formal debate (communication · easier)

- **Recap:** b2-debating.
- **Teaches:** the formal debate format (opening statement, rebuttal, closing), conceding in a formal register with the C1 concessions (lesson 21), keeping to time.
- **Longer text:** a transcript of an invented formal debate on a neutral subject (working hours, school, technology), every side given its strongest arguments.
- **Links:** b2-debating, b2-persuasion-and-hedging, c1-concession-and-hypothesis.

===== CLAUDE.md (C1 conventions) =====

## C1 conventions
C1 inherits every B2 convention unless it's changed here. Plans are in docs/plans/c1/; the coverage map of what A1 to B2 already teach is docs/c1-coverage.md (built by scripts/c1-lists.ts).
- **Teaching language.**
  - Grammar rules and nuance are still explained in English: a C1 learner reads French well, but a rule misunderstood in French is a rule learned wrong.
  - Short French glosses inside the English are allowed (*le passé simple, temps du récit*), but the English sentence must make sense without them.
  - Every French example, dialogue line and longer text keeps an English translation. The one exception: a line that only repeats an example already translated earlier in the same lesson.
- **Lengths.**
  - Teaching text: 900 to 1,200 words (1,300 for grammar). The whole lesson stays under 2,100.
  - Vocab: 15 to 30 entries (12 to 30 in grammar lessons), not counting glossary entries. Review lessons keep 3 to 8 new words.
  - Exercises: 15 to 18, with at least 4 production tasks, 1 reorder and 1 reformulation.
  - Dialogues: 12 to 16 lines.
  - En résumé (grammar and vocabulary lessons): 120 to 180 words in every unit.
  - Longer texts (communication, culture and review lessons): 250 to 400 French words, with a glossary.
- **Exercise types.** The B2 types stay: multiple-choice, fill-in, translate, reorder, reformulation, register-shift, error-correction and connector-choice. No new type is added, because lib/check-answer.ts can only grade a fixed list of answers; open writing keeps the model-answer pattern.
- **Instructions.** The B2 list stays. Add only **Résumez** (summarize) and **Justifiez** (give the reason), for synthesis and essay tasks graded by model answer, and add them to INSTRUCTION_VERBS in scripts/b2-lists.ts at the same time.
- **Literary tenses.**
  - Passé simple: production is allowed from c1-passe-simple on, in narrative writing only (a story, a historical account). Not in dialogue or an everyday register.
  - Passé antérieur: production in narrative writing from its lesson.
  - Imparfait and plus-que-parfait du subjonctif: recognition, plus light production in formal writing only, from c1-imperfect-subjunctive. No typed answer may require them; where they're possible, the present or passé subjunctive is also accepted.
- **Exam practice.** Practice lessons in the style of the DALF C1 name no timings, task formats, scoring or rules of the exam: "a short exposé from a short document", "a synthesis of two documents". Any statement about the exam goes in docs/review-needed.md.
- **Register labels.** Every example that isn't standard French carries a label in its translation: (literary), (formal), (familiar), (slang) or (spoken). Slang stays out of typed answers unless the instruction asks for it.
- **Sensitive topics.** The B2 rules continue: neutral facts only, no figures, no invented quotes, no named living politicians, and every such sentence in docs/review-needed.md.
  - Contested history (the colonial period, the Algerian war, Vichy): state what happened in neutral words and dates, present historians' debates as debates, and give no verdict.
  - Religion and laïcité: explain the 1905 law and how laïcité is applied as institutions, not as opinions; don't rank or judge beliefs.
  - Immigration and identity: describe policies and facts, never groups; no ethnic slang.
- **Authors, works and quotations.**
  - Name authors and works only when you are sure of the facts (title, date, author).
  - Quote only authors on a named safe list: authors who died before 1900 (for example Molière, Racine, Voltaire, Hugo, Baudelaire, Flaubert). Keep each quotation under 15 words. Why a list rather than a rule: French law adds wartime and "mort pour la France" extensions to the usual term, and terms differ by country.
  - All translations of quotations are our own.
  - For any later author, don't quote: paraphrase the idea, or write an original pastiche "in the style of" and label it as invented.
  - Longer literary texts are original pastiches, labelled as such, never copies or close paraphrases.
- **No repeats.** A C1 lesson recaps earlier material in 2 or 3 sentences with a LessonLink to the lesson that first taught it (see docs/c1-coverage.md), then teaches only what's new. Every "you know X from Y" claim is checked against lesson Y before it's written.
- **C1 checklist** (the B2 self-audit rules, plus the defects that recurred in B2):
  - Accepted answers: list every valid answer built from taught material, or pin the cue; include oe/œ and *Elle est* / *C'est* variants.
  - Mood and tense contrasts use verbs whose forms differ (not *donnent*, *reste*, *change*).
  - Cues are pinned (an infinitive, a structure) wherever more than one answer would be valid.
  - Vocab ids follow display order, and every entry is used in a complete French sentence.
  - Fixed phrases made of known words are taught or glossed at first use.
  - No "always", "never", "most common" or "almost always" unless the lesson's own examples agree; a rule of thumb is called one.
  - Rules are stated by their real test (same subject, completion), not an approximation.
  - Distractors are wrong in their sentence, and every rule tested in an exercise is explained in that lesson or an earlier one.
  - A model text keeps one narrative tense per paragraph, and every dialogue obeys the lesson's own rules.
  - Before finishing a unit, run check:words, check:structures, audit:content, test:b2-exercises (extended to C1) and scripts/b2-links-ids.ts (extended to C1).
