## 27. b1-careers-and-job-hunting: Careers and job hunting

- **Type:** vocabulary · **Order:** 27
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-jobs-and-workplace: builds on the jobs vocabulary.
- **Teaches:** job ads, contracts, pay, skills and the stages of a job hunt.
- **Not yet taught:** none.
- **Vocab (22):**
  - *une offre d'emploi*: a job offer, a job ad
  - *une annonce*: an advert
  - *postuler (à)*: to apply (for)
  - *une candidature*: an application
  - *un candidat / une candidate*: a candidate
  - *un CV*: a CV
  - *un CDI*: a permanent contract
  - *un CDD*: a fixed-term contract
  - *un / une stagiaire*: an intern
  - *brut / net*: gross / net (pay)
  - *à temps plein*: full-time
  - *à temps partiel*: part-time
  - *l'expérience*: experience
  - *une compétence*: a skill
  - *un employeur / une employeuse*: an employer
  - *embaucher*: to hire
  - *licencier*: to dismiss, to lay off
  - *démissionner*: to resign
  - *le chômage*: unemployment
  - *être au chômage*: to be unemployed
  - *une formation*: training, education
  - *un poste*: a position, a job
- **Dialogue:** Two friends read job ads together and compare the contracts and pay.
- **Production tasks:** translate "I'm applying for a full-time job."; write out a four-sentence job ad.
- **Needs review:** "A CDI is a permanent contract and a CDD a fixed-term one." "French CVs often include a photo" (practice is changing; confirm or drop). "A *période d'essai* is standard at the start of a CDI."

## 28. b1-cover-letter: CV and cover letter

- **Type:** communication · **Order:** 28
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-careers-and-job-hunting: the vocabulary of applications.
  - b1-conditionnel-present: *je serais ravi de…*, *je pourrais…*.
- **Teaches:** French CV conventions (one page; *expérience professionnelle*, *formation*, *compétences*, *centres d'intérêt*); the *lettre de motivation* (you, me, us structure); formulas: *Madame, Monsieur,*, *Je me permets de vous adresser ma candidature au poste de…*, *Je me tiens à votre disposition pour un entretien*, *Je vous prie d'agréer, Madame, Monsieur, l'expression de mes salutations distinguées*.
- **Not yet taught:** *En vous remerciant de votre attention* (the gérondif, b1-gerondif): replaced by *Je vous remercie de votre attention*. *Veuillez agréer…* (imperative of *vouloir*): fixed phrase. *Je vous prie d'agréer…*: fixed phrase. Both go in review-needed.md.
- **Vocab (17):**
  - *une lettre de motivation*: a cover letter
  - *Madame, Monsieur,*: Dear Sir or Madam,
  - *le poste de*: the position of
  - *adresser*: to send (formally)
  - *Je me permets de…*: I am writing to… (formal)
  - *actuellement*: currently
  - *Je serais ravi / ravie de…*: I would be delighted to…
  - *Je me tiens à votre disposition.*: I am available (for any questions).
  - *un entretien*: an interview
  - *les centres d'intérêt*: interests
  - *l'expérience professionnelle*: work experience
  - *maîtriser*: to have a good command of
  - *dynamique*: energetic
  - *rigoureux / rigoureuse*: thorough
  - *Je vous remercie de votre attention.*: Thank you for your time.
  - *Je vous prie d'agréer… salutations distinguées.*: Yours faithfully (fixed phrase)
  - *Veuillez agréer…*: Yours faithfully (fixed phrase, variant)
- **Dialogue:** A student asks a careers adviser (*vous*) to check a cover letter.
- **Longer text:** A full cover letter for a summer job in a hotel, with a translation.
- **Production tasks:** write out the middle paragraph of a cover letter; translate two formulas.
- **Needs review:** "A French CV is usually one page." "The cover letter follows a you / me / us structure." "*Je vous prie d'agréer… salutations distinguées* is the standard formal closing." "*Actuellement* means 'currently', not 'actually'."

## 29. b1-job-interview: The job interview

- **Type:** communication · **Order:** 29
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-cover-letter: the interview follows the application, and repeats its formal register. It implies a2-depuis-pendant-il-y-a, which the syllabus also listed.
- **Teaches:** presenting experience (*je travaille depuis…*, *j'ai travaillé pendant…*, *j'ai été chargé de…*); strengths and weaknesses; questions to ask; *vous* throughout; the conditionnel for availability (*je pourrais commencer en septembre*).
- **Not yet taught:** the gérondif (*en travaillant*): avoided.
- **Vocab (16):**
  - *un entretien d'embauche*: a job interview
  - *un recruteur / une recruteuse*: a recruiter
  - *se présenter*: to introduce yourself
  - *un point fort*: a strength
  - *un point faible*: a weakness
  - *être chargé / chargée de*: to be in charge of
  - *gérer*: to manage
  - *travailler en équipe*: to work in a team
  - *autonome*: independent
  - *ponctuel / ponctuelle*: punctual
  - *les horaires*: working hours
  - *disponible*: available
  - *les avantages*: benefits
  - *un défi*: a challenge
  - *évoluer*: to progress (in a job)
  - *Parlez-moi de vous.*: Tell me about yourself.
- **Dialogue:** An interview for an assistant job at a travel agency, *vous* throughout (8 to 10 lines).
- **Longer text:** A follow-up email after the interview, thanking the recruiter.
- **Production tasks:** translate "I have been working here for two years."; write out answers to two interview questions.
- **Needs review:** "*Parlez-moi de vous* is a typical opening question." "Candidates use *vous* throughout, even with a young recruiter."

## 30. b1-double-object-pronouns: Double object pronouns

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 30
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-imperative-with-pronouns: two pronouns together in commands (*Donne-le-moi*). It implies a2-pronouns-in-passe-compose, which the syllabus also listed.
- **Teaches:** the order *me / te / se / nous / vous* → *le / la / les* → *lui / leur* → *y* → *en*; in the present, passé composé (with agreement), negative and with an infinitive; *il y en a*; the imperative revisited, not retaught. Reuses: office tasks, sending documents.
- **Not yet taught:** none.
- **Vocab (13):**
  - *me le / me la / me les*: it / them to me
  - *te le / nous le / vous le*: it to you / us / you
  - *le lui / la lui / les lui*: it / them to him or her
  - *le leur / les leur*: it / them to them
  - *lui en / leur en*: some to him or her / to them
  - *m'en / t'en*: some to me / to you
  - *il y en a*: there is some, there are some
  - *une pièce jointe*: an attachment
  - *un dossier*: a file, a folder
  - *transmettre*: to pass on, to forward
  - *prêter*: to lend
  - *rendre*: to give back
  - *une imprimante*: a printer
- **Dialogue:** Colleagues pass documents around: *Tu me l'envoies ? Je te l'ai déjà envoyé !*
- **Production tasks:** rewrite sentences replacing both objects; translate "I gave it to him yesterday."
- **Needs review:** "The order rule above." "The participle still agrees with a direct object pronoun placed before the verb: *je la lui ai donnée*."

## 31. b1-possessive-pronouns: Possessive pronouns

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 31
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-direct-object-pronouns: pronouns that stand in for a noun. It implies a1-possessive-adjectives, which the syllabus also listed.
- **Teaches:** *le mien, la mienne, les miens, les miennes*, and the same for *tien*, *sien*, *nôtre*, *vôtre*, *leur*; agreement with the thing owned, not the owner; contractions (*au mien*, *du sien*); *c'est à moi* versus *c'est le mien*. Reuses: workplace objects, family.
- **Not yet taught:** none.
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *le mien / la mienne*: mine
  - *les miens / les miennes*: mine (plural)
  - *le tien / la tienne*: yours (tu)
  - *le sien / la sienne*: his, hers
  - *le nôtre / la nôtre*: ours
  - *le vôtre / la vôtre*: yours (vous)
  - *le leur / la leur*: theirs
  - *les nôtres / les vôtres / les leurs*: ours / yours / theirs (plural)
  - *C'est à moi.*: It's mine.
  - *un chargeur*: a charger
  - *un badge*: an access badge
  - *un casier*: a locker
- **Dialogue:** Two colleagues sort out whose mug, charger and umbrella is whose.
- **Production tasks:** rewrite sentences replacing the noun phrase; translate "That's not my phone, it's yours."
- **Needs review:** "*Le sien* agrees with the thing owned, so it can mean 'his' or 'hers'." "*Nôtre* and *vôtre* (pronouns) have a circumflex and a closed o, but *notre* and *votre* (adjectives) don't."

## 32. b1-demonstrative-pronouns: Demonstrative pronouns

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 32
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-relative-qui-que-ou: *celui qui*, *celui que*. It implies a1-demonstratives, which the syllabus also listed.
- **Teaches:** *celui, celle, ceux, celles*; with *-ci* / *-là*; with *qui*, *que*, *où*, *dont*; with *de* (*celui de Paul*); *ça* versus *celui*. Reuses: job offers, shopping.
- **Not yet taught:** *lequel* (b1-interrogative-lequel): avoided in questions; use *Tu préfères celui-ci ou celui-là ?*.
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *celui / celle*: the one
  - *ceux / celles*: the ones
  - *celui-ci / celle-ci*: this one
  - *celui-là / celle-là*: that one
  - *celui de / celle de*: the one belonging to
  - *celui qui / celui que*: the one who, the one that
  - *un modèle*: a model, a version
  - *une version*: a version
  - *un tarif*: a price, a rate
  - *un abonnement*: a subscription
  - *puissant / puissante*: powerful
  - *léger / légère*: light
- **Dialogue:** Two friends shop for a laptop: *Tu préfères celui-ci ou celui-là ?*
- **Production tasks:** rewrite sentences to avoid repeating the noun; translate "The one I prefer is cheaper."
- **Needs review:** "In speech, *celui-là* is often used where writing would distinguish *celui-ci* and *celui-là*."

## 33. b1-culture-work-life: Work life in France

- **Type:** culture · **Order:** 33
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-careers-and-job-hunting: the vocabulary of jobs and contracts.
- **Teaches:** the 35-hour week, paid holidays, RTT days, the lunch break, strikes and unions, *tu* and *vous* at work (*tutoyer* and *vouvoyer* revisited from a1-subject-pronouns), the right to disconnect, working from home.
- **Not yet taught:** the passive (*ont été créés en 1936*): avoided; use *datent de 1936*.
- **Vocab (15):**
  - *la semaine de 35 heures*: the 35-hour week
  - *les congés payés*: paid holiday
  - *les RTT*: extra days off for working over 35 hours
  - *la pause déjeuner*: the lunch break
  - *un ticket-restaurant*: a meal voucher
  - *une grève*: a strike
  - *faire grève*: to go on strike
  - *un syndicat*: a trade union
  - *un cadre / une cadre*: a manager, an executive
  - *la hiérarchie*: the hierarchy
  - *le droit à la déconnexion*: the right to disconnect
  - *les heures supplémentaires*: overtime
  - *le télétravail*: working from home
  - *un patron / une patronne*: a boss
  - *faire le pont*: to take a long weekend (a working day off between a public holiday and the weekend)
- **Dialogue:** A British employee asks a French colleague about holidays and whether to call the boss *tu*.
- **Production tasks:** translate "We have five weeks of paid holiday."; write out three comparisons with your own country.
- **Needs review (facts):** "The 35-hour week dates from 2000." "Paid holidays date from 1936 and are now five weeks a year." "RTT days compensate for hours worked above 35." "The right to disconnect has been law since 2017." "Meal vouchers are common." "Whether colleagues use *tu* depends on the company."
