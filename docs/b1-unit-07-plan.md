## 42. b1-environment: The environment

- **Type:** vocabulary · **Order:** 42
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-media-and-news: environmental topics are introduced through the news.
- **Teaches:** pollution, climate, energy, recycling and everyday eco-habits; *de plus en plus* and *de moins en moins*, with *de* before a noun (*de plus en plus de plastique*).
- **Not yet taught:** none.
- **Vocab (25):**
  - *l'environnement*: the environment
  - *la pollution*: pollution
  - *polluer*: to pollute
  - *le réchauffement climatique*: global warming
  - *le climat*: the climate
  - *l'énergie*: energy
  - *les énergies renouvelables*: renewable energy
  - *l'énergie solaire*: solar power
  - *une éolienne*: a wind turbine
  - *recycler*: to recycle
  - *le recyclage*: recycling
  - *trier ses déchets*: to sort your rubbish
  - *les déchets*: waste, rubbish
  - *le plastique*: plastic
  - *gaspiller*: to waste
  - *le gaspillage*: waste (the act of wasting)
  - *protéger*: to protect
  - *la planète*: the planet
  - *une espèce menacée*: an endangered species
  - *économiser*: to save (energy, water)
  - *le bio*: organic food
  - *écologique*: environmentally friendly
  - *la sécheresse*: drought
  - *de plus en plus (de)*: more and more
  - *de moins en moins (de)*: less and less, fewer and fewer
- **Dialogue:** Two flatmates argue about sorting the recycling.
- **Production tasks:** translate "We must sort our waste." and "There's more and more plastic in the sea."; write out four eco-habits.
- **Needs review:** "Bin colours for recycling vary from town to town in France." "*Le bio* is short for organic food." "*De plus en plus* takes *de* before a noun, with no article."

## 43. b1-cause-and-consequence: Cause and consequence

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 43
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-expressing-opinions: giving reasons for an opinion.
- **Teaches:** cause: *parce que* (revisited), *car* (written, never first), *puisque* (a cause the listener knows), *comme* (at the start), *à cause de* (negative) and *grâce à* (positive) + noun, *en raison de* (formal); consequence: *donc*, *alors*, *c'est pourquoi*, *c'est pour ça que*. Reuses: environment.
- **Not yet taught:** *par conséquent*, *ainsi* (b2-logical-connectors); *du coup* (b2-colloquial-french): avoided.
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *car*: because, as (in writing)
  - *puisque*: since (a reason already known)
  - *comme*: as, since (at the start of a sentence)
  - *à cause de*: because of
  - *grâce à*: thanks to
  - *en raison de*: due to (formal)
  - *donc*: so, therefore
  - *c'est pourquoi*: that's why
  - *c'est pour ça que*: that's why (spoken)
  - *la cause*: the cause
  - *la conséquence*: the consequence
  - *provoquer*: to cause
- **Dialogue:** Two neighbours talk about a heatwave and its consequences.
- **Production tasks:** join sentences with *grâce à* or *à cause de*; translate "As it was raining, we stayed at home." (*comme* + imparfait).
- **Needs review:** "*Car* can't start a sentence." "*Puisque* presents a cause the listener already knows." "*Comme* meaning 'as' goes at the start of the sentence." "*À cause de* is for negative causes, *grâce à* for positive ones."

## 44. b1-passive-voice-intro: The passive voice: introduction

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 44
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-media-and-news: the passive is most common in news reports. It implies a2-passe-compose-etre, which the syllabus also listed.
- **Teaches:** *être* + participle in the present and passé composé (*a été construit*); agreement with the subject; *par* for the agent; when French prefers *on* (preview only). Reuses: news, environment.
- **Not yet taught:** other tenses, *par* versus *de*, and the alternatives to the passive (b2-passive-voice, b2-alternatives-to-passive): avoided.
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *être construit / construite*: to be built
  - *être fabriqué / fabriquée*: to be made
  - *être détruit / détruite*: to be destroyed
  - *être élu / élue*: to be elected
  - *être arrêté / arrêtée*: to be arrested
  - *être sauvé / sauvée*: to be rescued
  - *par*: by (with the passive)
  - *un incendie*: a fire
  - *une tempête*: a storm
  - *une inondation*: a flood
  - *les secours*: the emergency services
  - *un pont*: a bridge
- **Short text:** Three short news items written with the passive, then rewritten with *on*.
- **Production tasks:** rewrite three active sentences as passive ones; translate "The bridge was built in 1900."
- **Needs review:** "The participle agrees with the subject." "French uses the passive less than English and often prefers *on*." "*Par* introduces the agent."

## 45. b1-presenting-an-argument: Presenting a simple argument

- **Type:** communication · **Order:** 45
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-cause-and-consequence: reasons and consequences are the core of an argument.
  - b1-agreeing-disagreeing: answering the other side.
- **Teaches:** structure (introduction, *d'abord / ensuite / enfin* or *premièrement / deuxièmement*, *pour* and *contre*, conclusion); *d'une part… d'autre part*; *il est vrai que…, mais…*; *je suis pour / contre*; *les avantages et les inconvénients*.
- **Not yet taught:** *bien que*, *cependant*, *pourtant* (b2-subjunctive-conjunctions, b2-concession-opposition): avoided; concession uses *il est vrai que…, mais…*.
- **Vocab (15):**
  - *premièrement*: firstly
  - *deuxièmement*: secondly
  - *je suis pour*: I'm in favour
  - *je suis contre*: I'm against
  - *un inconvénient*: a drawback
  - *les avantages et les inconvénients*: the pros and cons
  - *il est vrai que…, mais…*: it's true that…, but…
  - *d'une part… d'autre part*: on the one hand… on the other
  - *en résumé*: in summary
  - *un débat*: a debate
  - *défendre une idée*: to defend an idea
  - *justifier*: to justify
  - *interdire*: to ban
  - *une interdiction*: a ban
  - *la circulation*: traffic
- **Dialogue (8 to 10 lines):** A class debate on banning cars from the town centre: *vous* with the teacher, *tu* between students.
- **Longer text:** A 200-word blog article, *Pour ou contre la voiture en ville ?*, with a translation.
- **Production tasks:** write out a short argument from prompts; translate a sentence with *il est vrai que…, mais…*.
- **Needs review:** "*D'une part… d'autre part* belongs to formal writing." "*Premièrement* is more formal than *d'abord*."

## 46. b1-culture-french-republic: The French Republic

- **Type:** culture · **Order:** 46
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-media-and-news: politics and elections are presented through the news.
- **Teaches:** the motto *Liberté, égalité, fraternité*; Marianne; the flag and *La Marseillaise*; *la laïcité*; the history behind the 14 juillet (the holiday itself is in a2-culture-holidays-and-festivals); how presidential elections work.
- **Not yet taught:** none (the passive is taught in lesson 44).
- **Vocab (17):**
  - *la République*: the Republic
  - *la devise*: the motto
  - *la liberté*: freedom
  - *l'égalité*: equality
  - *la fraternité*: brotherhood
  - *Marianne*: Marianne, the symbol of the Republic
  - *le drapeau*: the flag
  - *l'hymne national*: the national anthem
  - *la laïcité*: secularism (the separation of religion and the State)
  - *l'État*: the State
  - *une élection*: an election
  - *voter*: to vote
  - *un électeur / une électrice*: a voter
  - *un tour*: a round (of voting)
  - *un citoyen / une citoyenne*: a citizen
  - *le maire*: the mayor
  - *la prise de la Bastille*: the storming of the Bastille
- **Dialogue:** An exchange student asks a French friend why there's a bust of Marianne in the town hall.
- **Production tasks:** translate "The president is elected for five years."; write out three facts about the Republic.
- **Needs review (facts):** "The law separating the Churches and the State dates from 1905." "The president has been elected for five years since 2002." "Elections have two rounds." "The voting age has been 18 since 1974." "Town halls display a bust of Marianne." "The 14 juillet recalls the storming of the Bastille (1789) and the Fête de la Fédération (1790)." "*La Marseillaise* was written in 1792."

## 47. b1-review-society: Review: Society and environment

- **Type:** review · **Order:** 47
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-environment: the topic of the model.
  - b1-passive-voice-intro: passive sentences in the model.
  - b1-presenting-an-argument: the structure of the model.
  - b1-culture-french-republic: the citizenship angle (*les citoyens*, voting, petitions).
- **Vocab (6):**
  - *une pétition*: a petition
  - *signer*: to sign
  - *agir*: to act
  - *sensibiliser*: to raise awareness
  - *jetable*: disposable
  - *à usage unique*: single-use
- **Model text:** An argued text about banning single-use plastic in the school canteen.
- **Production tasks:** the writing task; one write-out paragraph; one passive rewrite.
- **Needs review:** none beyond the unit's lessons.
