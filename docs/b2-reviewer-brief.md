# Reviewer brief: Parlons, level B2

## What Parlons is

Parlons is a free, text-only website that teaches French to English speakers, from beginner (A1) to advanced (C1). Each lesson explains one point in English, gives French examples with translations, has a short dialogue, and ends with exercises that the site marks automatically. B2 is the upper-intermediate level: 59 lessons in 8 units.

## The B2 rules, in brief

- Explanations are in English; every French example, dialogue line and longer text has an English translation.
- Grammar and vocabulary lessons end with a short summary in plain French, *En résumé*.
- Exercise instructions are in French (*Complétez*, *Reformulez*, *Corrigez*…); feedback is in English.
- Lessons use only words and grammar taught earlier. Anything informal (dropped *ne*, *on* for *nous*, slang) is labelled as spoken.
- Facts are kept neutral, with no statistics, and invented people or works are labelled as invented.

## How to review

For each item, and for anything else you notice, check:

1. **Accuracy:** is the French correct (grammar, mood, tense, agreement, spelling)?
2. **Naturalness:** would a native speaker say or write this sentence?
3. **Translations:** does the English say the same thing as the French?
4. **Register labels:** are "familiar", "standard", "formal" and "spoken" right?
5. **Answers:** would the site wrongly reject a valid answer?
6. **Facts and culture:** are dates, places and customs right, and is the tone neutral?

**How to reply:** one line per item, `lesson id, item number: OK` or `change to "…"` or `remove`. For example: `b2-tout 2: OK`.

## Where the lessons are

- `docs/review/b2-unit-1.txt` to `b2-unit-8.txt`: every lesson of each unit, in order, each starting with `=== lesson id ===`. In each lesson, the vocab and exercises come first (`fr` is the French, `answers` the accepted answers), then the lesson text.
- `docs/b2-review-items.md`: every item to check, grouped by unit and by type, numbered within each group. Items marked *(possibly resolved)* quote wording that has since changed: glance at the lesson, then answer "OK" if the new wording is fine.

## What to check, by unit

The numbers are items per type in docs/b2-review-items.md (253 in all, duplicates removed). **Language** covers grammar and meaning claims a learner relies on: please do these first. **Culture and register** covers labels, slang and usage. **Facts** covers dates, places and history. **Sensitive** covers topics where neutral wording matters (immigration, decolonization, the press).

## Priorities

1. The **Language** items in Units 1, 2, 4 and 6: the subjunctive, the conditionnel passé, relative pronouns and the probability scale. A wrong rule here would mislead every learner.
2. The **Sensitive** items (19): check that each sentence is neutral and accurate.
3. The **Facts** in Unit 7 (French history and francophone Africa).
4. The **Culture and register** items in Unit 8 (spoken French, slang, false friends).

Thank you: even a partial review, one unit at a time, is very useful.
