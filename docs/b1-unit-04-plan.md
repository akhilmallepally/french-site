# B1 Unit 4 plan: The subjunctive

Status: draft for approval. No lesson files written yet.

The unit teaches the regular forms (20) and the irregular forms (21) first, then the triggers (22, 23, 25), then the choice between subjunctive and infinitive (24). Lessons 20 and 21 use *il faut que* only as a frame for the forms; lesson 22 teaches the impersonal triggers with every verb.

---

## 20. b1-subjonctif-formation: The subjunctive: regular forms

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 20
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-il-faut-and-advice: *il faut* + infinitive becomes *il faut que* + subjunctive. It implies a2-irregular-verbs-present (the *ils* stems), which the syllabus also listed.
- **Teaches:** what a mood is (indicative versus subjunctive); regular formation: the *ils* stem of the present + *-e, -es, -e, -ions, -iez, -ent*; *nous* and *vous* look like the imparfait; two-stem verbs (*prendre*, *venir*, *boire*, *acheter*, *appeler*). *Il faut que* appears only as a frame, with regular verbs; lesson 22 teaches it and the other triggers properly. Reuses: advice, health.
- **Not yet taught:** the irregular subjunctives (*être*, *avoir*, *aller*, *faire*…, b1-subjonctif-irregular): avoided.
- **Vocab (13):**
  - *que je parle / que tu parles / qu'il parle*: that I / you / he speak(s)
  - *que nous parlions / que vous parliez / qu'ils parlent*: that we / you / they speak
  - *que je finisse*: that I finish
  - *que j'attende*: that I wait
  - *que je prenne / que nous prenions*: that I / we take
  - *que je vienne / que nous venions*: that I / we come
  - *que je boive / que nous buvions*: that I / we drink
  - *que j'achète / que nous achetions*: that I / we buy
  - *que j'appelle / que nous appelions*: that I / we call
  - *il faut que*: it's necessary that, someone has to
  - *le subjonctif*: the subjunctive
  - *l'indicatif*: the indicative
  - *un mode*: a mood (in grammar)
- **Dialogue:** A parent and a teenager: *il faut que tu ranges ta chambre et que tu finisses tes devoirs*.
- **Production tasks:** translate "You have to finish your homework." with *il faut que*; rewrite three *il faut* + infinitive sentences with a named person.
- **Needs review:** "The subjunctive stem comes from the *ils* form of the present." "For most verbs, the *nous* and *vous* forms look like the imparfait." "Many -er forms sound the same in the present and the subjunctive."

## 21. b1-subjonctif-irregular: Irregular subjunctives

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 21
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-subjonctif-formation: the irregular forms take the same endings as the regular ones.
- **Teaches:** *être* (*sois, soit, soyons, soyez, soient*), *avoir* (*aie, ait, ayons, ayez, aient*), *aller* (*aille, allions*), *faire* (*fasse*), *pouvoir* (*puisse*), *savoir* (*sache*), *vouloir* (*veuille, voulions*), *pleuvoir* (*qu'il pleuve*). As in lesson 20, *il faut que* is the frame for the examples. Reuses: chores, travel plans.
- **Not yet taught:** none.
- **Vocab (13):**
  - *que je sois / que tu sois / qu'il soit*: that I / you / he be
  - *que nous soyons / que vous soyez / qu'ils soient*: that we / you / they be
  - *que j'aie / que tu aies / qu'il ait*: that I / you / he have
  - *que nous ayons / que vous ayez / qu'ils aient*: that we / you / they have
  - *que j'aille / que nous allions*: that I / we go
  - *que je fasse / que nous fassions*: that I / we do
  - *que je puisse*: that I can
  - *que je sache*: that I know
  - *que je veuille / que nous voulions*: that I / we want
  - *qu'il pleuve*: that it rains
  - *faire ses valises*: to pack
  - *être prêt / prête*: to be ready
  - *une carte d'identité*: an identity card
- **Dialogue:** Friends getting ready for a trip: *il faut que tu sois à la gare à huit heures et que tu aies ta carte d'identité*.
- **Production tasks:** translate "You have to be ready at eight."; write out a checklist paragraph with five irregular subjunctives.
- **Needs review:** "*Que j'aie* and *qu'il ait* sound like *ai*." "*Que nous ayons* has no *i* after the *y*."

## 22. b1-subjonctif-necessity: Subjunctive after il faut que

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 22
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-subjonctif-irregular: the triggers are practised with every verb, including the irregular forms. It implies b1-subjonctif-formation.
- **Teaches:** *il faut que*, *il ne faut pas que*, *il est important / nécessaire / essentiel / indispensable que*, *il vaut mieux que*, *il suffit que*; the choice between *il faut* + infinitive (general) and *il faut que* (a named person); every verb, including *il faut que je fasse* and *il est important que tu sois là*. Reuses: household chores, work.
- **Not yet taught:** none.
- **Vocab (14):**
  - *il ne faut pas que*: someone mustn't
  - *il est important que*: it's important that
  - *il est nécessaire que*: it's necessary that
  - *il est essentiel que*: it's essential that
  - *il est indispensable que*: it's vital that
  - *il vaut mieux que*: it's better that
  - *il suffit que*: all that's needed is that
  - *les tâches ménagères*: the household chores
  - *partager les tâches*: to share the chores
  - *repasser*: to iron
  - *arroser les plantes*: to water the plants
  - *nourrir le chat*: to feed the cat
  - *un / une colocataire*: a flatmate
  - *un planning*: a rota, a schedule
- **Dialogue:** Flatmates draw up a chore rota: *il faut que quelqu'un sorte les poubelles le lundi et que tu fasses les courses*.
- **Production tasks:** rewrite sentences with *il est important que*; translate "We mustn't forget the keys."
- **Needs review:** "*Il ne faut pas que* means 'must not', never 'don't have to'." "*Il vaut mieux que* and *il suffit que* take the subjunctive."

## 23. b1-subjonctif-wishes-emotions: Subjunctive after wishes and emotions

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 23
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-subjonctif-necessity: the last lesson on forms and triggers; it implies b1-subjonctif-irregular, whose *être* and *avoir* forms emotions often need (*je suis content que tu sois là*).
  - b1-relationships: the examples are about family and friends.
- **Teaches:** *vouloir que*, *souhaiter que*, *aimer mieux que*, *préférer que*, *j'aimerais que*; *être content / heureux / triste / surpris que*, *regretter que*, *avoir peur que*, *c'est dommage que*. Reuses: relationships, emotions.
- **Not yet taught:** the subjonctif passé (*je suis content que tu sois venu*, b2-subjonctif-passe): avoided. Reactions stay in the present.
- **Vocab (15):**
  - *vouloir que*: to want (someone) to
  - *souhaiter que*: to wish that
  - *aimer mieux que*: to prefer that
  - *préférer que*: to prefer that
  - *j'aimerais que*: I'd like (someone) to
  - *être heureux / heureuse que*: to be happy that
  - *être triste que*: to be sad that
  - *être surpris / surprise que*: to be surprised that
  - *regretter que*: to be sorry that
  - *avoir peur que*: to be afraid that
  - *c'est dommage que*: it's a shame that
  - *craindre que*: to fear that
  - *ravi / ravie*: delighted
  - *ça me fait plaisir que*: it makes me happy that
  - *maman / papa*: mum / dad
- **Dialogue:** Two siblings discuss their parents' wishes: *maman veut que je rentre à Noël*.
- **Production tasks:** translate "I'd like you to come."; rewrite three news items as reactions with *je suis content que*.
- **Needs review:** "After *avoir peur que*, written French often adds a *ne* that isn't negative (the *ne explétif*). Learners can recognize it without using it." "*Espérer que* takes the indicative, unlike *souhaiter que*." "*C'est dommage que* takes the subjunctive."

## 24. b1-subjunctive-vs-infinitive: Subjunctive or infinitive?

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 24
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-subjonctif-wishes-emotions: the verbs that can take either.
- **Teaches:** same subject → infinitive (*je veux partir*, *je suis content de partir*); different subject → *que* + subjunctive (*je veux que tu partes*); *de* before the infinitive after *être* + adjective and *avoir peur*; general *il faut* + infinitive versus *il faut que*. Reuses: relationships, plans.
- **Not yet taught:** *pour que* and *avant que* (b2-subjunctive-conjunctions): avoided.
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *s'installer*: to settle, to move in
  - *vivre ensemble*: to live together
  - *accepter de*: to agree to
  - *oser*: to dare
  - *faire un effort*: to make an effort
  - *prévenir*: to let (someone) know, to warn
  - *rassurer*: to reassure
  - *s'inquiéter*: to worry
  - *se décider*: to make up your mind
  - *un compromis*: a compromise
  - *proposer de*: to suggest
  - *une condition*: a condition
- **Dialogue:** A couple decide whether to move to another city: *je veux partir* / *je veux qu'on parte ensemble*.
- **Production tasks:** combine pairs of sentences, choosing the infinitive or *que* + subjunctive; translate "I'm happy to be here" and "I'm happy you're here."
- **Needs review:** "When both verbs have the same subject, French uses the infinitive: *je veux que je parte* is wrong."

## 25. b1-expressing-doubt-certainty: Doubt and certainty

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 25
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-subjunctive-vs-infinitive: the subjunctive forms and the choice of mood.
  - b1-agreeing-disagreeing: *je ne suis pas sûr* was taught there as a phrase.
- **Teaches:** *douter que*, *je ne pense pas que*, *je ne crois pas que*, *je ne suis pas sûr que* + subjunctive; *je suis sûr que*, *je pense que*, *il est certain / évident / clair que* + indicative. Reuses: opinions.
- **Not yet taught:** *il est possible que* and *il se peut que* (b2-certainty-and-probability): avoided. Questions with inversion (*Penses-tu qu'il vienne ?*): avoided.
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *douter que*: to doubt that
  - *douter de*: to doubt
  - *un doute*: a doubt
  - *je ne pense pas que*: I don't think that
  - *je ne crois pas que*: I don't believe that
  - *je ne suis pas sûr / sûre que*: I'm not sure that
  - *être certain / certaine que*: to be certain that
  - *il est évident que*: it's obvious that
  - *il est clair que*: it's clear that
  - *sûrement*: surely, most likely
  - *certainement*: certainly
  - *une preuve*: a proof, evidence
- **Dialogue:** Two friends discuss a rumour that their favourite café will close: *je doute que ce soit vrai*.
- **Production tasks:** translate "I don't think he's right."; rewrite affirmative opinions as negative ones, changing the mood.
- **Needs review:** "*Je pense que* takes the indicative, but *je ne pense pas que* takes the subjunctive." "*Douter que* takes the subjunctive even in the affirmative."

## 26. b1-review-subjunctive: Review: The subjunctive

- **Type:** review · **Order:** 26
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-expressing-doubt-certainty: the last lesson of the unit, which implies the rest.
- **Teaches:** mixed practice choosing the indicative, the subjunctive or the infinitive.
- **Vocab (6):**
  - *organiser une fête*: to organize a party
  - *une fête surprise*: a surprise party
  - *un invité / une invitée*: a guest
  - *souffler les bougies*: to blow out the candles
  - *se charger de*: to take care of
  - *garder le secret*: to keep the secret
- **Model text:** An email to friends organizing a surprise party, using *il faut que*, *je veux que*, *je doute que* and *je suis sûr que*.
- **Production tasks:** one write-out paragraph; the mood-choice exercises; one translation.
- **Needs review:** none beyond the unit's lessons.
