# C1 Unit 7 plan: Living the language

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions". Unit 7 now ends with its own review (c1-review-living); the C1 final review moves to Unit 8.

## 44. c1-proverbs-and-sayings: Proverbs and sayings (vocabulary · easier)

- **Teaches:** common proverbs, their meaning and when people use them (often shortened or played with).
- **Vocab:** *qui ne risque rien n'a rien*, *petit à petit, l'oiseau fait son nid*, *il ne faut pas vendre la peau de l'ours…*, *un proverbe*.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (meaning), fill-in (complete the proverb), translate (equivalent, not literal).
- **Links:** b2-idioms-everyday.

## 45. c1-advanced-idioms: Advanced idioms (vocabulary · easier)

- **Recap:** the idioms in docs/c1-coverage.md.
- **Teaches:** idioms for argument and work (*tenir la route*, *ne pas y aller par quatre chemins*, *noyer le poisson*), with register labels.
- **Links:** b2-idioms-everyday, b2-idiomatic-y-en.

## 46. c1-culture-regional-languages: Regional languages (culture · harder)

- **Teaches:** Breton, Occitan, Alsatian, Basque, Corsican and the Creoles; their status and how they're taught today, neutrally, with no speaker figures.
- **Flags:** facts to review; language policy stated neutrally.
- **Links:** b2-culture-francophone-africa, b1-culture-quebec.

## 47. c1-culture-francophonie: La Francophonie (culture · harder)

- **Recap:** b2-culture-francophone-africa and b1-culture-quebec.
- **Teaches:** the institution (the OIF), French in Belgium, Switzerland, Canada, Africa and the overseas territories, with labelled regional words; no speaker figures.
- **Flags:** facts to review; colonial history stated neutrally.

## 48. c1-register-shifting: Shifting register on demand (communication · easier)

- **Recap:** b2-registers, b2-colloquial-french, b2-culture-verlan-and-slang.
- **Teaches:** rewriting one message from slang to administrative French, and back.
- **Exercises:** register-shift (all four directions), error-correction (mixed registers).

## 49. c1-review-living: Review: Living the language (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *une variante*, *le parler*, *une expression régionale*.
- **Model:** one message in three registers, with a proverb and two labelled regional words.
