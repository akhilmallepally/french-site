# C1 Unit 2 plan: Style and emphasis

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions". Mostly easier to write: rules are clear and B2 has the models.

## 9. c1-stylistic-inversion: Stylistic inversion (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** complex inversion and inversion after *peut-être*, *sans doute*, *aussi* (b2-registers).
- **Teaches:** inversion after *à peine*, *ainsi*, *du moins*, *encore*; after direct speech (*dit-il*, *répondit-elle*); when inversion is required, optional or wrong (a rule of thumb where usage varies).
- **Vocab (new):** *du moins*, *encore* (= even so), *toujours est-il que*. Recap: *à peine… que* (c1-passe-anterieur).
- **Exercises:** reformulation, error-correction, fill-in, multiple-choice, reorder.
- **Links:** b2-registers, a1-yes-no-questions.

## 10. c1-ne-expletif: The ne explétif (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** *avant que… ne*, *à moins que… ne* (b2-subjunctive-conjunctions); *avoir peur que… ne* (b1-subjonctif-wishes-emotions); *de crainte que* (b2-purpose-expressions).
- **Teaches:** after comparisons (*plus que je ne pensais*), *empêcher que*, *éviter que*; its register; how to tell it from a negation.
- **Vocab (new):** *empêcher que*, *il s'en faut de peu que*, *plus… que je ne*.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (negative or expletive?), fill-in, translate, reformulation.
- **Links:** b2-subjunctive-conjunctions, b2-purpose-expressions.

## 11. c1-literary-negation: Literary negation (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** *ne… guère* and *ne* alone with *pouvoir, savoir* (b2-registers); *ne… que* and *ni… ni* (b1-ne-que-ni-ni).
- **Teaches:** *ne… point*, *ne… nullement*, *ne… aucunement*, *nul ne*, *ne* alone with *oser* and *cesser*.
- **Vocab (new):** *ne… point*, *nullement*, *nul ne*, *je n'ose*.
- **Exercises:** register-shift (literary → standard), multiple-choice, fill-in, translate.
- **Links:** b2-registers, b1-ne-que-ni-ni.

## 12. c1-dislocation-and-fronting: Dislocation and fronting (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** *c'est… qui / que* and *ce qui…, c'est* (b2-mise-en-relief); the repeated subject in speech (b2-colloquial-french).
- **Teaches:** left and right dislocation (*ce film, je l'ai adoré*; *je l'ai adoré, ce film*); fronting in writing (*Rares sont ceux qui…*, *Reste la question de…*); choosing emphasis by register.
- **Vocab (new):** *rares sont ceux qui*, *reste à savoir*. Recap: *toujours est-il que* (lesson 9).
- **Exercises:** reformulation, register-shift, reorder, error-correction.
- **Links:** b2-mise-en-relief, b2-colloquial-french.

## 13. c1-adverb-placement: Adverb placement and nuance (grammar · easier)

- **Teaches:** adverbs that change meaning by position (*seulement*, *même*, *aussi*), sentence adverbs (*heureusement*, *visiblement*), long adverbs in compound tenses.
- **Vocab (new):** *visiblement*, *décidément*, *forcément*. Recap: *apparemment* (b1-conditionnel-present).
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice, reorder, reformulation, translate.
- **Links:** a2-adverbs-ment, b2-certainty-and-probability.

## 14. c1-tricky-prepositions: Tricky prepositions (grammar · easier)

Moved from Unit 6: it's a precision point, like the rest of this unit.

- **Teaches:** verbs with two constructions (*manquer à / manquer de*, *penser à / penser de*, *tenir à / tenir de*), prepositions with islands and regions, *de* versus *par* after the passive (recap of b2-passive-voice).
- **Vocab (new):** *manquer à*, *tenir de*, *penser de* (an opinion).
- **Exercises:** fill-in, error-correction, multiple-choice.
- **Links:** a2-verbs-with-a-and-de, b2-passive-voice, a2-prepositions-with-places.

## 15. c1-precise-verbs: Precise verbs (vocabulary · easier)

- **Teaches:** replacing *faire, mettre, dire, avoir* with precise verbs.
- **Vocab (new, 20 to 25):** *effectuer*, *établir*, *disposer de*, *aborder*, *susciter*, *engendrer*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation, fill-in, register-shift, translate.
- **Links:** b2-reporting-verbs, b2-nominalisation.

## 16. c1-collocations: Collocations (vocabulary · easier)

- **Teaches:** fixed noun + verb and noun + adjective pairs.
- **Vocab (new):** *émettre une hypothèse*, *une vive inquiétude*, *un rôle clé*, *prendre en compte*. Recap: *porter plainte* (b1-making-a-complaint, text).
- **Exercises:** connector-choice (the right verb), fill-in, error-correction, translate.
- **Links:** b2-science-and-research, b1-making-a-complaint.

## 17. c1-review-style: Review: Style and emphasis (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *la mise en valeur*, *un effet de style*, *la sobriété*. Recap: *sobre* (b2-describing-a-work-of-art).
- **Model:** a short editorial (300 words) using inversion, dislocation, a literary negation and precise verbs.
