# C1 Unit 2 plan: Style and emphasis

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions". Mostly easier to write: rules are clear and B2 has the models.

## 8. c1-stylistic-inversion: Stylistic inversion (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** complex inversion and inversion after *peut-être*, *sans doute*, *aussi* (b2-registers).
- **Teaches:** inversion after *à peine*, *ainsi*, *du moins*, *encore*; inversion in relative clauses and after direct speech (*dit-il*, *répondit-elle*); when inversion is required, optional or wrong.
- **Vocab:** *à peine*, *du moins*, *encore* (= even so), *toujours est-il que*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation, error-correction, fill-in, multiple-choice, reorder.
- **Links:** b2-registers, a1-yes-no-questions.

## 9. c1-ne-expletif: The ne explétif (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** *avant que… ne* and *à moins que… ne* (b2-subjunctive-conjunctions); *avoir peur que… ne* (b1-subjonctif-wishes-emotions).
- **Teaches:** after comparisons (*plus que je ne pensais*), *empêcher que*, *de crainte que*; its register; how to tell it from a negation.
- **Vocab:** *plus… que je ne*, *empêcher que*, *de crainte que* (recap), *il s'en faut de peu que*.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (negative or expletive?), fill-in, translate, reformulation.
- **Links:** b2-subjunctive-conjunctions, b2-purpose-expressions.

## 10. c1-literary-negation: Literary negation (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** *ne… guère* and *ne* alone with *pouvoir, savoir* (b2-registers).
- **Teaches:** *ne… point*, *ne… nullement*, *ne… aucunement*, *nul ne*, *ni… ni* in formal prose, *ne* alone with *oser* and *cesser*.
- **Vocab:** *ne… point*, *nullement*, *nul ne*, *je n'ose*.
- **Exercises:** register-shift (literary → standard), multiple-choice, fill-in, translate.
- **Links:** b2-registers, b1-ne-que-ni-ni.

## 11. c1-dislocation-and-fronting: Dislocation and fronting (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** *c'est… qui / que* and *ce qui…, c'est* (b2-mise-en-relief); the repeated subject in speech (b2-colloquial-french).
- **Teaches:** left and right dislocation (*ce film, je l'ai adoré*; *je l'ai adoré, ce film*); fronting in writing (*Rares sont ceux qui…*, *Reste la question de…*); choosing emphasis by register.
- **Vocab:** *rares sont ceux qui*, *reste à savoir*, *toujours est-il que* (recap from lesson 8).
- **Exercises:** reformulation, register-shift, reorder, error-correction.
- **Links:** b2-mise-en-relief, b2-colloquial-french.

## 12. c1-adverb-placement: Adverb placement and nuance (grammar · easier)

- **Teaches:** adverbs that change meaning by position (*seulement*, *même*, *aussi*), sentence adverbs (*heureusement*, *visiblement*), long adverbs in compound tenses.
- **Vocab:** *visiblement*, *apparemment* (recap), *décidément*, *forcément*.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice, reorder, reformulation, translate.
- **Links:** a2-adverbs-ment, b2-certainty-and-probability.

## 13. c1-precise-verbs: Precise verbs (vocabulary · easier)

- **Teaches:** replacing *faire, mettre, dire, avoir* with precise verbs (*effectuer, établir, souligner, disposer de*).
- **Vocab (20 to 25):** *effectuer*, *établir*, *disposer de*, *aborder*, *susciter*, *engendrer*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation, fill-in, register-shift, translate.
- **Links:** b2-reporting-verbs, b2-nominalisation.

## 14. c1-collocations: Collocations (vocabulary · easier)

- **Teaches:** fixed noun + verb and noun + adjective pairs (*prendre une décision*, *émettre une hypothèse*, *une vive inquiétude*).
- **Vocab:** *émettre une hypothèse*, *porter plainte* (check B1), *une vive inquiétude*, *un rôle clé*.
- **Exercises:** connector-choice (the right verb), fill-in, error-correction, translate.
- **Links:** b2-science-and-research, b1-making-a-complaint.

## 15. c1-review-style: Review: Style and emphasis (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *la mise en valeur*, *un effet de style*, *sobre* (recap).
- **Model:** a short editorial (300 words) using inversion, dislocation, a literary negation and precise verbs.
