# C1 Unit 8 plan: Towards DALF C1

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions". Concession and hypothesis and formal relatives have moved to Unit 3. Practice lessons name no timings, task formats or scoring. Write this unit last.

## 51. c1-euphemism-and-synonyms: Euphemism and nuance in synonyms (vocabulary · harder)

- **Teaches:** euphemisms in public language (*un plan social*, *les personnes âgées*), *euphémiser*; near-synonyms by register and strength (*peur / crainte / effroi*; *dire / affirmer / prétendre*, recap of b2-reporting-verbs).
- **Vocab (new):** *un euphémisme*, *euphémiser*, *un plan social*, *la crainte*, *l'effroi*, *un synonyme*.
- **Flags:** sensitive (public language about social groups): neutral examples only.
- **Links:** b2-reporting-verbs, b2-social-issues, c1-irony-and-understatement.

## 52. c1-spoken-discourse-markers: Spoken discourse markers (vocabulary · easier)

- **Recap:** the fillers of b2-colloquial-french.
- **Teaches:** *enfin* (correcting yourself), *bon*, *quand même*, *tiens*, *disons*, *justement*, *en fait*, and their functions in speech.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (function), connector-choice, register-shift.
- **Links:** b2-colloquial-french.

## 53. c1-culture-laicite-and-institutions: Laïcité and the institutions (culture · harder)

- **Recap:** *la laïcité* and the 1905 law (b1-culture-french-republic).
- **Teaches:** how laïcité is applied in schools and public services, the Constitution and the main institutions, explained as rules, not opinions.
- **Flags:** sensitive (religion): no judgement of beliefs, no figures; every sentence in docs/review-needed.md.
- **Links:** b1-culture-french-republic, b2-politics-and-society, c1-law-and-institutions.

## 54. c1-exam-oral-presentation: Practice: a short exposé (communication · easier)

- **Teaches:** preparing a short exposé from a short document: finding the problématique, a two- or three-part plan, examples, answering questions. Practice only, with no claims about the exam's rules, timing or scoring.
- **Links:** c1-public-speaking, c1-formal-debate.

## 55. c1-exam-written-tasks: Practice: synthesis and essay (communication · easier)

- **Teaches:** planning a synthesis of two documents and an argued essay; checking your own text with a list. Practice only, with no claims about the exam.
- **Links:** c1-synthesis, c1-dissertation, c1-concession-and-hypothesis.

## 56. c1-review-towards-dalf: Review: Towards DALF C1 (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *une épreuve*, *un exposé*, *argumenter*.
- **Model:** a short argued text using *quand bien même*, a formal relative and an apposition (all from Unit 3).

## 57. c1-review-final: C1 final review (review · harder)

- A can-do checklist for the whole level, one model sentence per unit (with LessonLinks), and a final piece of writing: *Mon bilan de C1*.
- Exercises sample all eight units.
