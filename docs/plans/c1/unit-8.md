# C1 Unit 8 plan: Towards DALF C1

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions". New unit: it adds the grammar and lexis the syllabus lacked, DALF-style practice (as practice, not as claims about the exam) and the final review. Write it last.

## 50. c1-concession-and-hypothesis: Concession and hypothesis at C1 (grammar · harder)

- **Recap:** *bien que*, *quoique*, *même si* (b2-concession-opposition); *si* + plus-que-parfait and mixed conditionals (b2-si-plus-que-parfait, b2-mixed-conditionals).
- **Teaches:** *quand bien même* + conditionnel, *pour peu que*, *à supposer que*, *si tant est que* + subjunctive, *encore que*, *au cas où* + conditionnel (recap), the inverted conditional (*Serait-il malade, il viendrait quand même*: literary).
- **Vocab:** *quand bien même*, *pour peu que*, *à supposer que*, *si tant est que*, *encore que*.
- **Exercises:** fill-in (mood), reformulation, connector-choice, error-correction, translate.
- **Links:** b2-concession-opposition, b2-mixed-conditionals.

## 51. c1-formal-relatives-and-apposition: Formal relatives and apposition (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** *dont*, *lequel* and its forms, *ce dont / ce à quoi* (b1-relative-dont, b2-relative-lequel, b2-relative-prepositions).
- **Teaches:** *dont* + noun in long sentences (*l'auteur dont on connaît l'œuvre*), *duquel* after a preposition phrase, apposition and detached adjectives (*Fatiguée, elle rentra*), commas in formal prose.
- **Vocab:** *à l'issue de laquelle*, *au cours duquel* (recap), *l'apposition*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation (two sentences → one), error-correction, reorder.
- **Links:** b2-relative-lequel, b2-relative-prepositions.

## 52. c1-euphemism-and-synonyms: Euphemism and nuance in synonyms (vocabulary · harder)

- **Teaches:** euphemisms in public language (*un plan social*, *les personnes âgées*), near-synonyms by register and strength (*peur / crainte / effroi*; *dire / affirmer / prétendre* recap).
- **Vocab:** *un euphémisme*, *un plan social*, *la crainte*, *l'effroi*, *un synonyme*.
- **Flags:** sensitive (public language about social groups): neutral examples only.
- **Links:** b2-reporting-verbs, b2-social-issues.

## 53. c1-spoken-discourse-markers: Spoken discourse markers (vocabulary · easier)

- **Recap:** the fillers of b2-colloquial-french.
- **Teaches:** *enfin* (correcting yourself), *bon*, *quand même*, *tiens*, *disons*, *justement*, *en fait*, and their functions in speech.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (function), connector-choice, register-shift.
- **Links:** b2-colloquial-french.

## 54. c1-culture-laicite-and-institutions: Laïcité and the institutions (culture · harder)

- **Recap:** *la laïcité* and the 1905 law (b1-culture-french-republic).
- **Teaches:** how laïcité is applied in schools and public services, the Constitution and the main institutions, explained as rules, not opinions.
- **Flags:** sensitive (religion): no judgement of beliefs, no figures; every sentence in docs/review-needed.md.
- **Links:** b1-culture-french-republic, b2-politics-and-society.

## 55. c1-exam-oral-presentation: Practice: an oral presentation (communication · easier)

- **Teaches:** preparing a ten-minute exposé from a short document: problématique, plan, examples, answering questions. Practice only.
- **Links:** c1-public-speaking, c1-formal-debate.

## 56. c1-exam-written-tasks: Practice: synthesis and essay (communication · easier)

- **Teaches:** timing and planning a synthesis of two documents and an argued essay; checking your own text with a list. Practice only.
- **Links:** c1-synthesis, c1-dissertation.

## 57. c1-review-towards-dalf: Review: Towards DALF C1 (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *une épreuve*, *un exposé*, *argumenter*.
- **Model:** a short argued text using *quand bien même*, a formal relative and an apposition.

## 58. c1-review-final: C1 final review (review · harder)

- A can-do checklist for the whole level, one model sentence per unit (with LessonLinks), and a final piece of writing: *Mon bilan de C1*.
- Exercises sample all eight units.
