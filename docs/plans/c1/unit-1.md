# C1 Unit 1 plan: Literary French

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions".

## 1. c1-passe-simple: The passé simple (grammar · easier)

b2-review-final points to this lesson as the start of C1 (now as plain text; restore the LessonLink when the file exists).

- **Recap (2 sentences):** recognition of the 3rd person and *fut, eut, fit, vint, prit* from b2-arts-and-literature; the passé composé and imparfait from a2 and b1.
- **Teaches:**
  - all persons of the three regular patterns: *-ai, -as, -a, -âmes, -âtes, -èrent*; *-is…-îmes, -îtes, -irent*; *-us…-ûmes, -ûtes, -urent*
  - the main irregular stems: *être (je fus)*, *avoir (j'eus)*, *faire (je fis)*, *venir / tenir (je vins, je tins)*, *voir (je vis)*, *naître (je naquis)*, *mourir (il mourut)*
  - use: the narrative past of written stories and history; the passé simple for events, the imparfait for background; never in speech or dialogue
  - the forms that look like the present (*il finit*, *il dit*) and how context decides
- **Key vocab (6):** *le récit* (the narrative), *le narrateur* (recap), *raconter au passé simple*, *soudain*, *à ce moment-là* (recap), *le lendemain* (recap).
- **Model examples:**
  - *Le roi entra, regarda la foule et se tut.* (The king came in, looked at the crowd and fell silent.)
  - *Ils partirent à l'aube et arrivèrent le soir.* (They left at dawn and arrived in the evening.)
  - *Nous fûmes surpris : personne ne nous attendait.* (We were surprised: no one was waiting for us.)
  - *Elle naquit à Lyon en 1820.* (She was born in Lyon in 1820.) [invented person]
- **Exercises (16):** fill-in (forms, all persons); multiple-choice (passé simple or present: *il finit*); reformulation (passé composé → passé simple in a story); error-correction (*ils partèrent* → *partirent*); translate (narrative sentences); connector-choice (passé simple or imparfait in a tale); reorder; a write-out of a five-sentence tale.
- **Links:** b2-arts-and-literature, b1-plus-que-parfait, a2-telling-a-story, b2-culture-french-history.
- **Flags:** none.

## 2. c1-passe-anterieur: The passé antérieur (grammar · easier)

- **Teaches:** formation (*eut fini, fut parti*); use after *quand, lorsque, dès que, après que, à peine… que* when the main verb is in the passé simple; its spoken equivalent (the plus-que-parfait or passé composé).
- **Vocab:** *à peine… que*, *aussitôt que*, *dès que* (recap), *après que* (recap).
- **Exercises:** fill-in, reformulation, multiple-choice (passé antérieur or plus-que-parfait), translate, reorder.
- **Links:** b1-plus-que-parfait, b2-futur-anterieur, b2-subjunctive-conjunctions.

## 3. c1-imperfect-subjunctive: Imperfect and pluperfect subjunctive (grammar · harder)

- **Teaches:** recognition of *qu'il fût, qu'il eût, qu'il fît, qu'il vînt* and *qu'il eût fini*; where they appear (classic literature, very formal writing); the modern equivalents (present and passé subjunctive); light production in formal writing only, never required in a typed answer.
- **Vocab:** *la concordance des temps* (recap), *qu'il fût*, *qu'il eût*, *le registre littéraire*.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (identify), reformulation (to modern French), translate, fill-in with both forms accepted.
- **Links:** b2-subjonctif-passe, b2-reported-speech-past, b2-registers.

## 4. c1-reading-literary-narrative: Reading literary narrative (communication · harder)

- **Teaches:** reading a narrative in the passé simple: tense pairs, free indirect speech (*discours indirect libre*), point of view.
- **Vocab:** *le point de vue*, *le discours indirect libre*, *le personnage* (recap), *l'intrigue*, *le dénouement*.
- **Longer text:** an original pastiche of a 19th-century short story (labelled as invented), 300 to 350 words.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (comprehension), reformulation (free indirect → reported speech), translate, write-out.
- **Links:** b2-arts-and-literature, b2-reported-speech-past.

## 5. c1-culture-literary-canon: The French literary canon (culture · harder)

- **Teaches:** a short neutral overview: the classical 17th century, the Enlightenment (link to Unit 6), the 19th-century novel, the 20th century; how French schools read these works.
- **Vocab:** *un classique*, *le roman réaliste*, *un courant littéraire*, *une œuvre majeure*.
- **Quotations:** public-domain authors only, under 15 words each; otherwise paraphrase.
- **Links:** b2-arts-and-literature, a2-culture-french-school-system.
- **Flags:** facts and dates to review.

## 6. c1-culture-french-poetry: French poetry (culture · harder)

- **Teaches:** the alexandrine and the sonnet, rhyme and rhythm, reading a poem aloud (the mute *e*); two or three public-domain poets by name.
- **Vocab:** *l'alexandrin*, *le sonnet*, *la césure*, *le e muet*.
- **Longer text:** an original poem in classical form, labelled as written for the lesson, with a commentary.
- **Links:** b2-arts-and-literature.
- **Flags:** facts to review; quotations under 15 words.

## 7. c1-review-literary: Review: Literary French (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *le style*, *une tournure*, *soutenu* (recap as instruction word), *pasticher*.
- **Model:** an original tale of 300 words in the passé simple and imparfait, with one passé antérieur and one recognized imparfait du subjonctif.
- **Exercises:** one per lesson of the unit, plus reformulation, error-correction and a write-out.
