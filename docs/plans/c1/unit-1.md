## 1. c1-passe-simple: The passé simple (grammar · easier)

b2-review-final mentions this lesson as the start of C1 (as plain text; restore the LessonLink when the file exists).

- **Recap (2 sentences):** recognition of the 3rd person and *fut, eut, fit, vint, prit* from b2-arts-and-literature; the passé composé and imparfait from A2 and B1.
- **Teaches:**
  - the three regular patterns, all persons: *-ai, -as, -a, -âmes, -âtes, -èrent*; *-is, -is, -it, -îmes, -îtes, -irent*; *-us, -us, -ut, -ûmes, -ûtes, -urent*
  - full paradigms of *être (je fus)*, *avoir (j'eus)*, *faire (je fis)*, *venir / tenir (je vins, je tins)*, *voir (je vis)*
  - third person, for recognition, of the verbs a reader meets most: *dire (il dit)*, *prendre (il prit)*, *mettre (il mit)*, *pouvoir (il put)*, *vouloir (il voulut)*, *savoir (il sut)*, *croire (il crut)*, *lire (il lut)*, *vivre (il vécut)*, *écrire (il écrivit)*, *connaître (il connut)*, *courir (il courut)*, *naître (il naquit)*, *mourir (il mourut)*
  - use: the narrative past of written stories and history; the passé simple for events, the imparfait for background; not in everyday speech or in modern dialogue
  - forms that look like the present (*il finit*, *il dit*) and how context decides
- **Key vocab (new):** *le récit* (the narrative), *s'écrier* (to exclaim), *se mettre à* (to start to), *à l'aube* (at dawn). Recaps: *soudain* (a2-passe-compose-vs-imparfait), *s'apercevoir que* (b1-plus-que-parfait), *aussitôt que* (b1-time-connectors), *le narrateur* (b2-arts-and-literature), *le lendemain* and *à ce moment-là* (b2-reporting-verbs). Fill to 12 with narrative verbs and time words.
- **Model examples:**
  - *Le roi entra, regarda la foule et se tut.* (The king came in, looked at the crowd and fell silent.)
  - *Ils partirent à l'aube et arrivèrent le soir.* (They left at dawn and arrived in the evening.)
  - *Nous fûmes surpris : personne ne nous attendait.* (We were surprised: no one was waiting for us.)
  - *« Attention ! » s'écria-t-elle, et tout le monde se mit à courir.* ("Watch out!" she cried, and everyone started running.)
  - *Elle naquit à Lyon en 1820.* (She was born in Lyon in 1820.) [invented person]
- **Exercises (16):** fill-in (forms, all persons); multiple-choice (passé simple or present: *il finit*); reformulation (passé composé → passé simple in a story); error-correction (*ils partèrent* → *partirent*); translate; connector-choice (passé simple or imparfait in a tale); reorder; a write-out of a five-sentence tale.
- **Links:** b2-arts-and-literature, b1-plus-que-parfait, a2-telling-a-story, b2-culture-french-history.

## 2. c1-passe-anterieur: The passé antérieur (grammar · easier)

- **Teaches:** formation (*il eut fini, elle fut partie*); use after *quand, lorsque, dès que, après que, aussitôt que, à peine… que* when the main verb is in the passé simple. Its spoken equivalent is the passé composé or the passé surcomposé (*quand il a eu fini, il est parti*), not the plus-que-parfait: the plus-que-parfait pairs with the imparfait (*quand il avait fini, il partait*).
- **Vocab (new):** *à peine… que*, *le passé surcomposé*. Recaps: *dès que* and *aussitôt que* (b1-time-connectors), *après que* (b2-subjunctive-conjunctions, text).
- **Exercises:** fill-in; multiple-choice (choose the tense by the main verb's tense: passé simple → passé antérieur; passé composé → surcomposé; imparfait → plus-que-parfait); reformulation; translate; reorder.
- **Links:** b1-plus-que-parfait, b1-time-connectors, b2-futur-anterieur.

## 3. c1-future-in-the-past: The future in the past and tense nuance (grammar · easier)

Moved from Unit 5: it belongs with the narrative tenses.

- **Recap:** the conditionnel as future in the past in reported speech (b2-reported-speech-past).
- **Teaches:** *allait* + infinitive versus the conditionnel; *devait* + infinitive (was to); the conditionnel passé as a future perfect in the past; the historical present in narrative.
- **Vocab (new):** *devait* (was to), *ne pas tarder à*, *le présent de narration*. Recap: *allait* + infinitive.
- **Exercises:** fill-in, reformulation, multiple-choice, translate.
- **Links:** b2-reported-speech-past, b2-futur-anterieur, b2-conditionnel-passe.

## 4. c1-imperfect-subjunctive: Imperfect and pluperfect subjunctive (grammar · harder)

- **Teaches:** recognition of *qu'il fût, qu'il eût, qu'il fît, qu'il vînt* and *qu'il eût fini*; where they appear (classic literature, very formal writing); their modern equivalents. Never required in a typed answer; where it's possible, the present or passé subjunctive is accepted.
- **Vocab (new):** *qu'il fût*, *qu'il eût*, *le registre littéraire*. Recap: *la concordance des temps* (b2-reported-speech-past).
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (identify), reformulation (to modern French), translate, fill-in with both forms accepted.
- **Links:** b2-subjonctif-passe, b2-reported-speech-past, b2-registers.

## 5. c1-reading-literary-narrative: Reading literary narrative (communication · harder)

- **Teaches:** reading a narrative in the passé simple: tense pairs, free indirect speech (*le discours indirect libre*), point of view.
- **Vocab (new):** *le point de vue*, *le discours indirect libre*, *l'intrigue*, *le dénouement*. Recap: *le personnage* (b1-cinema-and-books).
- **Longer text:** an original pastiche of a 19th-century short story, labelled as invented, 300 to 350 words.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (comprehension), reformulation (free indirect → reported speech), translate, write-out.
- **Links:** b2-arts-and-literature, b2-reported-speech-past.

## 6. c1-culture-literary-canon: The French literary canon (culture · harder)

- **Teaches:** a short neutral overview: the classical 17th century, the Enlightenment (link to Unit 6), the 19th-century novel, the 20th century; how French schools read these works.
- **Vocab (new):** *un classique*, *le roman réaliste*, *un courant littéraire*, *une œuvre majeure*.
- **Quotations:** only authors on the CLAUDE.md safe list (died before 1900), under 15 words, our own translations. 20th-century authors are named with their works and paraphrased, never quoted.
- **Links:** b2-arts-and-literature, a2-culture-french-school-system.
- **Flags:** facts and dates to review.

## 7. c1-culture-french-poetry: French poetry (culture · harder)

- **Teaches:** the alexandrine and the sonnet, rhyme and rhythm, reading a poem aloud (the mute *e*, the diérèse); two or three poets from the safe list.
- **Vocab (new):** *l'alexandrin*, *le sonnet*, *la césure*, *le e muet*, *la diérèse*.
- **Longer text:** an original poem in classical form, labelled as written for the lesson, with a commentary. Each line's syllable count (with the mute *e* and diérèse rules) is checked by scripts/scan-alexandrines.ts, written with the lesson; the lesson is listed in docs/review-needed.md for the native speaker. If a verse can't be verified, the poem is in free verse.
- **Quotations:** safe-list poets only, under 15 words, our own translations.
- **Links:** b2-arts-and-literature.
