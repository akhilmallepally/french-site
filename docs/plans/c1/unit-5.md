# C1 Unit 5 plan: Professional mastery

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions". Mostly easier: B2 Unit 5 is the model.

## 30. c1-law-and-institutions: Law and institutions (vocabulary · harder)

- **Teaches:** the vocabulary of law and the State (*un décret, une loi, le Conseil d'État, un tribunal, un recours*), without legal advice or figures.
- **Vocab (20 to 25):** *un décret*, *un recours*, *une juridiction*, *entrer en vigueur*, *abroger*.
- **Flags:** facts to review; no legal advice.
- **Links:** b1-culture-french-republic, b2-politics-and-society.

## 31. c1-finance-and-economics: Finance and economics (vocabulary · easier)

- **Teaches:** budgets, markets and public finance (*le déficit, la dette publique, un taux d'intérêt, la conjoncture* recap).
- **Vocab:** *le déficit*, *la dette publique*, *un taux d'intérêt*, *la fiscalité*, *un placement*.
- **Flags:** no real figures.
- **Links:** b2-economy-and-business, b2-presenting-data.

## 32. c1-future-in-the-past: The future in the past and tense nuance (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** the conditionnel as future in the past in reported speech (b2-reported-speech-past).
- **Teaches:** *allait* + infinitive versus the conditionnel; *devait* + infinitive (was to); the futur antérieur in the past (conditionnel passé); historical present in reports.
- **Vocab:** *devait* (was to), *allait* (was about to), *ne pas tarder à*.
- **Exercises:** fill-in, reformulation, multiple-choice, translate.
- **Links:** b2-reported-speech-past, b2-futur-anterieur, b2-conditionnel-passe.

## 33. c1-diplomatic-language: Diplomatic language (communication · easier)

- **Teaches:** softening a refusal, saying no without saying no, the conditionnel of politeness at C1 level.
- **Vocab:** *il ne nous sera malheureusement pas possible de*, *nous ne manquerons pas de*, *sauf erreur de ma part*.
- **Links:** b2-negotiating, b2-regret-and-reproach.

## 34. c1-formal-correspondence: High-register correspondence (communication · easier)

- **Recap:** b1-formal-email and b2-formal-report.
- **Teaches:** formal letters to institutions, the full closing formulas, *veuillez* + infinitive, references.
- **Longer text:** an original formal letter and its reply (300 words).
- **Links:** b1-formal-email, b2-formal-report.

## 35. c1-leading-a-meeting: Leading a meeting (communication · easier)

- **Recap:** b2-meetings-and-calls.
- **Teaches:** chairing: opening, giving the floor, managing conflict, summing up decisions, the minutes.
- **Links:** b2-meetings-and-calls, b2-debating.

## 36. c1-review-professional: Review: Professional mastery (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *un ordre de mission*, *un cahier des charges*, *donner suite à*.
- **Model:** a formal letter announcing a decision, with a diplomatic refusal.
