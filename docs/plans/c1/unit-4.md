# C1 Unit 4 plan: Debate and rhetoric

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions".

## 23. c1-rhetorical-devices: Rhetorical devices (vocabulary · easier)

- **Teaches:** naming and using *l'anaphore*, *la gradation*, *l'antithèse* (recap of the essay term), *la question rhétorique*, *la litote*, *l'hyperbole*, with original examples.
- **Vocab:** *l'anaphore*, *la gradation*, *la litote*, *l'hyperbole*, *une figure de style*.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (identify the device), reformulation, write-out.
- **Links:** b2-persuasion-and-hedging, b2-argumentative-essay.

## 24. c1-subjunctive-nuances: Subjunctive nuances (grammar · harder)

- **Recap:** the B1 and B2 subjunctive lessons (see docs/c1-coverage.md).
- **Teaches:** indicative or subjunctive after *penser, croire* in questions and negatives; after *le seul, le premier* (recap of b2-subjunctive-relative-clauses); *que* + subjunctive at the start of a sentence (*Qu'il soit fatigué, je le comprends*); the mood that changes the meaning (*il comprend que tu sois parti* / *que tu es parti*).
- **Vocab:** *que… ou non*, *admettre que*, *comprendre que* (two meanings).
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice, fill-in with forms that differ in the two moods, reformulation, error-correction.
- **Links:** b2-subjunctive-relative-clauses, b2-certainty-and-probability, b2-persuasion-and-hedging.

## 25. c1-formal-debate: Formal debate (communication · easier)

- **Recap:** b2-debating.
- **Teaches:** the formal debate format (opening statement, rebuttal, closing), conceding in formal register, keeping to time.
- **Longer text:** a transcript of an invented formal debate on a neutral subject (working hours, school, technology), every side given its strongest arguments.
- **Links:** b2-debating, b2-persuasion-and-hedging.

## 26. c1-public-speaking: Speeches and presentations (communication · easier)

- **Teaches:** the structure of a speech, signposting (*Je commencerai par…*, *J'en viens maintenant à…*), addressing an audience, ending well.
- **Vocab:** *j'en viens à*, *permettez-moi de conclure*, *mesdames et messieurs*.
- **Links:** b2-presenting-data, b2-meetings-and-calls.

## 27. c1-irony-and-understatement: Irony and understatement (communication · harder)

- **Teaches:** recognizing irony and the *litote* (*ce n'est pas mal* = it's good), polite understatement, when irony fails in writing.
- **Vocab:** *l'ironie*, *ce n'est pas mal*, *pas fameux*, *euphémiser* (with Unit 8).
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (literal or ironic meaning), register-shift.
- **Flags:** label every ironic example clearly.

## 28. c1-culture-satire-and-humour: Satire and humour (culture · harder)

- **Teaches:** a neutral overview of satirical traditions (fables, satirical press, sketches); wordplay; why humour is hard to translate.
- **Flags:** sensitive; follow the B2 press rule (no mention of the attacks on the satirical press); no living politicians; facts to review.
- **Links:** b2-culture-french-press, b2-idioms-everyday.

## 29. c1-review-rhetoric: Review: Debate and rhetoric (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *un orateur / une oratrice*, *convaincant* (recap), *un argument d'autorité*.
- **Model:** a short persuasive speech with three devices and one strategic concession.
