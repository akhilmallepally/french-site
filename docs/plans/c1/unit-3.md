# C1 Unit 3 plan: Academic writing

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions". It now holds formal relatives and concession and hypothesis, so the debate (Unit 4) and the DALF practice (Unit 8) can use them.

## 18. c1-participle-clauses: Participle clauses (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** the participe présent, *ayant fini*, the gérondif and the verbal adjective (b2-participles-and-gerund).
- **Teaches:** the absolute participle clause (*La réunion terminée, chacun partit*), *étant donné* and *vu* as fixed participles, the dangling participle as a mistake (the test: the same subject).
- **Vocab (new):** *compte tenu de*, *une fois… terminé*. Recaps: *étant donné* (b2-participles-and-gerund), *vu*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation, error-correction (dangling participle), fill-in, translate.
- **Links:** b2-participles-and-gerund, b2-infinitif-passe.

## 19. c1-formal-relatives-and-apposition: Formal relatives and apposition (grammar · easier)

Moved from Unit 8.

- **Recap:** *dont* (b1-relative-dont), *lequel* and its forms, *au cours duquel* (b2-relative-lequel), *ce dont / ce à quoi* (b2-relative-prepositions).
- **Teaches:** *dont* + noun in long sentences (*l'auteur dont on connaît l'œuvre*), *duquel* after a preposition phrase (*à l'issue duquel*), apposition and detached adjectives (*Fatiguée, elle rentra*), commas in formal prose.
- **Vocab (new):** *à l'issue de laquelle*, *l'apposition*, *en raison duquel*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation (two sentences → one), error-correction, reorder.
- **Links:** b2-relative-lequel, b2-relative-prepositions, c1-participle-clauses.

## 20. c1-advanced-connectors: Advanced connectors and academic hedging (grammar · easier)

Merges the planned c1-conditionnel-journalistique: B2 already teaches the rumour conditionnel and hedging, and the new hedging items are too few for a lesson.

- **Recap:** the B2 connectors (docs/c1-coverage.md), including *or* (b2-logical-connectors) and *en revanche* (b2-concession-opposition), with C1 usage notes; the rumour conditionnel (b2-conditionnel-passe) and hedging (b2-nuancing-opinions, b2-persuasion-and-hedging).
- **Teaches (new):** *dès lors*, *en l'occurrence*, *a fortiori*, *à cet égard*; *par contre* versus *en revanche* as a register point; academic hedging with *à en croire*, *selon toute vraisemblance*, *sous réserve de*.
- **Vocab (new):** *dès lors*, *en l'occurrence*, *a fortiori*, *à cet égard*, *par contre*, *à en croire*, *selon toute vraisemblance*, *sous réserve de*.
- **Exercises:** connector-choice, reformulation (fact → hedged), error-correction, translate.
- **Links:** b2-logical-connectors, b2-concession-opposition, b2-conditionnel-passe.

## 21. c1-concession-and-hypothesis: Concession and hypothesis at C1 (grammar · harder)

Moved from Unit 8, after the connectors.

- **Recap:** *bien que*, *quoique*, *même si* (b2-concession-opposition); *si* + plus-que-parfait and mixed conditionals (b2-si-plus-que-parfait, b2-mixed-conditionals); *au cas où* + conditionnel (b2-mixed-conditionals, verified).
- **Teaches:** *quand bien même* + conditionnel; *pour peu que*, *à supposer que*, *si tant est que* + subjunctive; *encore que*; the inverted hypothesis, both literary: *Fût-il malade, il viendrait* (imparfait du subjonctif, recognition) and *Serait-il malade, il viendrait* (conditionnel).
- **Vocab (new):** *quand bien même*, *pour peu que*, *à supposer que*, *si tant est que*, *encore que*.
- **Exercises:** fill-in (mood, with forms that differ), reformulation, connector-choice, error-correction, translate.
- **Links:** b2-concession-opposition, b2-mixed-conditionals, c1-imperfect-subjunctive.

## 22. c1-abstract-nouns: Abstract nouns (vocabulary · easier)

- **Recap:** the suffixes from b2-nominalisation.
- **Teaches:** abstract nouns for argument; nominal style in academic writing.
- **Vocab (new, 20 to 25):** *la portée*, *la pertinence*, *la légitimité*, *l'ampleur*, *la démarche*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation (verb → noun), fill-in, translate.
- **Links:** b2-nominalisation.

## 23. c1-synthesis: The synthèse de documents (communication · harder)

- **Recap:** summarizing one article (b2-summarizing-an-article).
- **Teaches:** combining two or three documents without quoting them or giving your opinion; the plan and the neutral voice.
- **Longer texts:** two short original documents on a neutral subject (300 words in all) and a model synthesis.
- **Exercises:** reformulation, multiple-choice (which sentence is neutral), write-out.
- **Links:** b2-summarizing-an-article, b2-formal-report.

## 24. c1-dissertation: The French dissertation (communication · harder)

- **Recap:** the dialectical plan and *la problématique* (b2-argumentative-essay).
- **Teaches:** the C1 essay: a problématique that is usually a question opening a debate rather than a yes/no question (a rule of thumb); transitions; the *ouverture*; the analytical plan as an alternative; using concession and hypothesis (lesson 21).
- **Longer text:** an original model essay (350 to 400 words).
- **Links:** b2-argumentative-essay, b2-review-science, c1-concession-and-hypothesis.

## 25. c1-review-academic: Review: Academic writing (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *un corpus*, *mettre en perspective*, *un fil conducteur*. Recap: *la problématique* (b2-argumentative-essay).
- **Model:** a short synthesis paragraph and an essay introduction.
