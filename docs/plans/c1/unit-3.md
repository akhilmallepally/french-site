# C1 Unit 3 plan: Academic writing

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions". Easier to write: B2 Units 3, 5 and 6 give the models.

## 16. c1-participle-clauses: Participle clauses (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** the participe présent, *ayant fini*, the gérondif and the verbal adjective (b2-participles-and-gerund).
- **Teaches:** the absolute participle clause (*La réunion terminée, chacun partit*), *étant donné* and *vu* as fixed participles, the dangling participle as a mistake (same subject).
- **Vocab:** *une fois… terminé*, *compte tenu de*, *étant donné* (recap), *vu*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation, error-correction (dangling participle), fill-in, translate.
- **Links:** b2-participles-and-gerund, b2-infinitif-passe.

## 17. c1-advanced-connectors: Advanced connectors (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** the B2 connectors (b2-logical-connectors, b2-concession-opposition, b2-purpose-expressions; see docs/c1-coverage.md).
- **Teaches:** *or* (now, in an argument), *dès lors*, *en l'occurrence*, *a fortiori*, *à cet égard*, *en revanche* versus *par contre* (register).
- **Vocab:** *or*, *dès lors*, *en l'occurrence*, *a fortiori*, *à cet égard*.
- **Exercises:** connector-choice, reformulation, error-correction, translate.
- **Links:** b2-logical-connectors, b2-concession-opposition.

## 18. c1-conditionnel-journalistique: Hedging and the journalistic conditional (grammar · easier)

- **Recap:** the rumour conditionnel (b2-conditionnel-passe) and hedging (b2-nuancing-opinions, b2-persuasion-and-hedging).
- **Teaches:** the conditionnel of unconfirmed information in all tenses, *selon*, *d'après*, *il semblerait que*; academic hedging (*il semble que*, *on peut penser que*).
- **Vocab:** *sous réserve de confirmation*, *à en croire*, *selon toute vraisemblance*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation (fact → hedged), register-shift, multiple-choice, translate.
- **Links:** b2-conditionnel-passe, b2-certainty-and-probability.

## 19. c1-abstract-nouns: Abstract nouns (vocabulary · easier)

- **Recap:** the suffixes from b2-nominalisation.
- **Teaches:** abstract nouns for argument (*l'enjeu, la portée, la pertinence, la légitimité*), nominal style in academic writing.
- **Vocab (20 to 25):** *la portée*, *la pertinence*, *la légitimité*, *l'ampleur*, *la démarche*.
- **Exercises:** reformulation (verb → noun), fill-in, translate.
- **Links:** b2-nominalisation.

## 20. c1-synthesis: The synthèse de documents (communication · harder)

- **Recap:** summarizing one article (b2-summarizing-an-article).
- **Teaches:** combining two or three documents without quoting them or giving your opinion; the plan and the neutral voice.
- **Longer texts:** two short original documents on a neutral subject (300 words in all) and a model synthesis.
- **Exercises:** reformulation, multiple-choice (which sentence is neutral), write-out.
- **Links:** b2-summarizing-an-article, b2-formal-report.

## 21. c1-dissertation: The French dissertation (communication · harder)

- **Recap:** the dialectical plan (b2-argumentative-essay).
- **Teaches:** the C1 essay: a problématique that isn't a yes/no question, transitions, the *ouverture*, the analytical plan as an alternative.
- **Longer text:** an original model essay (350 to 400 words).
- **Links:** b2-argumentative-essay, b2-review-science.

## 22. c1-review-academic: Review: Academic writing (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *une problématique* (recap), *un corpus*, *mettre en perspective*.
- **Model:** a short synthesis paragraph and an essay introduction.
