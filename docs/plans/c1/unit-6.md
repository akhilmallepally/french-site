# C1 Unit 6 plan: Ideas and society

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions". Harder to write: ideas, history and culture need careful facts and neutral wording.

## 37. c1-philosophy-and-ideas: Philosophy and ideas (vocabulary · harder)

- **Teaches:** the words of ideas (*la liberté, la raison, le libre arbitre, une thèse*), how French schools teach philosophy (the terminale year), neutrally.
- **Vocab:** *le libre arbitre*, *la raison*, *une notion*, *un concept*, *remettre en question*.
- **Quotations:** public-domain philosophers only, under 15 words.
- **Links:** b2-argumentative-essay, a2-culture-french-school-system.
- **Flags:** facts to review.

## 38. c1-culture-enlightenment: The Enlightenment (culture · harder)

- **Teaches:** *les Lumières*: key ideas and figures (public domain), the *Encyclopédie*, the link to the Revolution (recap of b1-culture-french-republic).
- **Flags:** dates and facts to review.

## 39. c1-culture-may-68: May 68 and modern France (culture · harder)

- **Teaches:** what happened in May 1968, neutrally: students, strikes, the government's response, the debates about its legacy.
- **Flags:** sensitive (contested history): present debates as debates; no living politicians; dates to review.
- **Links:** b2-culture-french-history, b2-politics-and-society.

## 40. c1-technology-ethics: Technology and ethics (vocabulary · easier)

- **Teaches:** artificial intelligence, data and privacy, *les données personnelles*, *la vie privée*, ethics committees (recap from b2-review-science).
- **Vocab:** *les données personnelles*, *la vie privée*, *un algorithme*, *la surveillance*.
- **Links:** b1-technology-and-internet, b2-review-science.

## 41. c1-culture-auteur-cinema: Auteur cinema (culture · harder)

- **Recap:** b1-culture-french-cinema.
- **Teaches:** *la Nouvelle Vague*, the idea of the *auteur*, talking about a film's style.
- **Flags:** facts and names to review; no quotations from films.
- **Links:** b1-culture-french-cinema, b2-describing-a-work-of-art.

## 42. c1-tricky-prepositions: Tricky prepositions (grammar · easier)

- **Teaches:** verbs with two constructions (*manquer à / manquer de*, *penser à / penser de*), *de* versus *par* after passives (recap), prepositions with countries and islands at C1 level.
- **Exercises:** fill-in, error-correction, multiple-choice.
- **Links:** a2-verbs-with-a-and-de, b2-passive-voice, a2-prepositions-with-places.

## 43. c1-review-ideas: Review: Ideas and society (review · harder)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *un débat d'idées*, *un héritage* (recap), *une remise en cause*.
- **Model:** an original essay introduction on a neutral question about technology or freedom.
