# C1 Unit 6 plan: Ideas and society

Status: plan only. Rules: CLAUDE.md, "C1 conventions". Harder to write: ideas, history and culture need careful facts and neutral wording. c1-tricky-prepositions has moved to Unit 2.

## 39. c1-philosophy-and-ideas: Philosophy and ideas (vocabulary · harder)

- **Teaches:** the words of ideas (*la raison, le libre arbitre, une notion*), how French schools teach philosophy (the final year of the lycée), neutrally.
- **Vocab (new):** *le libre arbitre*, *la raison*, *une notion*, *un concept*, *remettre en question*.
- **Quotations:** only safe-list philosophers (died before 1900, for example Descartes, Pascal, Rousseau), under 15 words, our own translations; later thinkers are paraphrased.
- **Links:** b2-argumentative-essay, a2-culture-french-school-system.
- **Flags:** facts to review.

## 40. c1-culture-enlightenment: The Enlightenment (culture · harder)

- **Teaches:** *les Lumières*: key ideas and figures (Voltaire, Rousseau, Diderot, Montesquieu: all on the safe list), the *Encyclopédie*, the link to the Revolution (recap of b1-culture-french-republic).
- **Quotations:** safe list only, under 15 words, our own translations.
- **Flags:** dates and facts to review.

## 41. c1-culture-may-68: May 68 and modern France (culture · harder)

- **Teaches:** what happened in May 1968, neutrally: students, strikes, the government's response, the debates about its legacy.
- **Flags:** sensitive (contested history): present debates as debates; no living politicians; no quotations; dates to review.
- **Links:** b2-culture-french-history, b2-politics-and-society.

## 42. c1-technology-ethics: Technology and ethics (vocabulary · easier)

- **Teaches:** artificial intelligence, data and privacy, ethics committees.
- **Vocab (new):** *les données personnelles*, *la vie privée*, *un algorithme*, *la surveillance*. Recap: *un comité d'éthique* (b2-review-science).
- **Links:** b1-technology-and-internet, b2-review-science.

## 43. c1-culture-auteur-cinema: Auteur cinema (culture · harder)

- **Recap:** b1-culture-french-cinema.
- **Teaches:** *la Nouvelle Vague*, the idea of the *auteur*, talking about a film's style.
- **Quotations:** none from films or directors (none are on the safe list); paraphrase only.
- **Flags:** facts and names to review.
- **Links:** b1-culture-french-cinema, b2-describing-a-work-of-art.

## 44. c1-review-ideas: Review: Ideas and society (review · harder)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *un débat d'idées*, *une remise en cause*, *un courant de pensée*. Recap: *un héritage* (b2-review-arts).
- **Model:** an original essay introduction on a neutral question about technology or freedom.
