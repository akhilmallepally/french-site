# C2 Unit 8 plan: Range and mastery

Status: plan only. Rules: the draft "C2 conventions" in docs/plans/c2/overview.md. Write this unit last: its lessons and reviews draw on everything.

## 42. c2-pastiche: Writing in a style (communication · harder)

- **Goal:** imitate a style consciously: a safe-list author's, a press genre's, an administrative register's.
- **Recaps:** pastiche (c1-review-literary: *pasticher*); stylistic analysis (c2-stylistic-analysis); press genres (c2-reading-the-press).
- **New:** identifying the markers of a style (vocabulary, syntax, rhythm, tense), exaggerating them for parody vs reproducing them for pastiche, the ethics of pastiche (label it).
- **Key vocab (new):** *une parodie*, *à la manière de*, *un tic d'écriture*, *imiter* (recap: c1-culture-satire-and-humour).
- **Longer text:** one invented event told *à la manière de* a fable (La Fontaine, safe list), a dépêche and an administrative notice, each labelled as an original pastiche.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (which style?), register-shift, reformulation, reorder, a writing task.
- **Risks:** pastiches of later authors only "in the style of" and labelled as invented (C1 rule); no copied lines.

## 43. c2-idiomatic-precision: Idioms at C2 (vocabulary · harder)

- **Goal:** choose between near-idioms, use idioms at the right strength, and recognize twisted idioms in the press.
- **Recaps:** everyday idioms (b2-idioms-everyday), advanced idioms (c1-advanced-idioms), proverbs (c1-proverbs-and-sayings), collocations (c1-collocations).
- **New:** pairs that learners confuse (chosen with c2-find and a dictionary while writing), idioms with a literary or old-fashioned flavour, idioms in negative form, twisted idioms in headlines (recap of Unit 7), idioms that don't translate.
- **Key vocab (new):** about 20 idioms not taught before (checked with c2-find), each labelled by register.
- **Model:** a dialogue between two editors arguing over idioms in a headline.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice, error-correction (a broken idiom), fill-in, translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** every idiom's meaning and register checked in a dictionary; no repeat of C1 idioms as new entries.

## 44. c2-register-mastery: Moving between registers within one text (communication · harder)

- **Goal:** use a register change on purpose inside one text: for irony, emphasis, quotation or characterization.
- **Recaps:** register shifting between texts (c1-register-shifting); irony (c1-irony-and-understatement); free indirect speech (c1-reading-literary-narrative).
- **New:** the deliberate break (a familiar word in a formal text for effect), quoting someone's register, the signals that tell a reader the break is intended (quotation marks, italics, *pour reprendre l'expression de*), and when a break is just a mistake.
- **Key vocab (new):** *un effet de rupture*, *entre guillemets*, *pour reprendre l'expression de*, *détonner*.
- **Longer text:** an original column that uses three deliberate register breaks, explained.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (intended or mistake?), register-shift, error-correction, translate, reorder, a writing task.
- **Risks:** the line between "deliberate effect" and "mixing registers" (a C1 warning) must be drawn clearly.

## 45. c2-review-range: Review: Range and mastery (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *la palette*, *l'aisance* (recap? check: b-level entry), *la justesse*, *nuancer* (recap).
- **Model:** an original text showing range: one paragraph each in three registers and two styles.
- **Exercises:** one per lesson, plus a writing task.

## 46. c2-review-final: C2 final review (review · harder)

- **Goal:** a can-do checklist for the whole level, one model sentence per unit (with LessonLinks), and a final piece of writing: *Mon rapport à la langue française*.
- **New words (3 to 8):** *la maîtrise*, *un locuteur natif / une locutrice native*, *un perfectionnement*.
- **Exercises:** sample all eight units (18 at most), with a writing task.
- **Risks:** the model uses only taught material; check each structure against the unit that teaches it (rule from Prompt 38).
