# C2 Unit 3 plan: Specialized French

Status: plan only. Rules: the draft "C2 conventions" in docs/plans/c2/overview.md.

Four registers that C2 users read and sometimes write. The lessons teach reading first: how these texts are built, their fixed phrases, and how to restate them in plain French. Facts about institutions and procedures are general and neutral, with no figures and no legal advice.

## 14. c2-legal-french: Legal French (vocabulary · harder)

- **Goal:** read a contract, a set of terms and conditions and a court decision summary, and restate them plainly.
- **Recaps:** courts, *former un recours*, *en vertu de*, *abroger* (c1-law-and-institutions); formal letters (c1-formal-correspondence).
- **New:** contract vocabulary (*une clause*, *les parties*, *résilier*, *une échéance*, *à titre gracieux*, *sous peine de*, *nonobstant*, *ledit / ladite*); the structure of a judgment and of its summary; the future and the present of obligation in legal texts (*le locataire s'engage à*, *est tenu de* (recap)).
- **Key vocab (new):** about 18 items above, each used in an original contract extract.
- **Model:** an original lease clause and a plain-French version for a tenant.
- **Exercises:** register-shift (legal → plain), multiple-choice, fill-in, translate, reorder, a writing task (explain a clause to a friend).
- **Risks:** no legal advice and no statement of current law beyond the C1 facts; any procedure described is generic. Whether French courts still use the "attendu que" style is a fact to verify before mentioning it.

## 15. c2-medical-french: Medical French (vocabulary · harder)

- **Goal:** read a patient leaflet, a consultation summary and a health article.
- **Recaps:** the health system (b2-health-system); science and research (b2-science-and-research).
- **New:** *une posologie*, *une contre-indication*, *les effets indésirables*, *un antécédent*, *un diagnostic*, *un pronostic*, *bénin / malin*, *chronique / aigu*, learned roots (*-ite*, *-algie*, *-ectomie*), the impersonal style of leaflets.
- **Model:** an original medicine leaflet (an invented medicine) and its plain-language version.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice, fill-in, register-shift, translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** no medical advice; the medicine and doses are invented and labelled; no figures that look like real dosages (use "the dose your doctor prescribes").

## 16. c2-scientific-french: Scientific French (vocabulary · harder)

- **Goal:** read an abstract and a popular-science article; hedge claims the way French science writing does.
- **Recaps:** hedging (b2-nuancing-opinions, b2-persuasion-and-hedging); *selon toute vraisemblance* (c1-advanced-connectors).
- **New:** *une hypothèse*, *un protocole*, *un échantillon*, *un biais* (recap: c1-technology-ethics), *corréler / une corrélation* vs *causer*, *il ressort de… que*, *les résultats suggèrent*, *à l'inverse*, the passive and *on* in method sections.
- **Model:** an original abstract and its popular-science version.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (corrélation or causalité?), reformulation, translate, error-correction (overclaiming), reorder.
- **Risks:** invented studies, labelled as invented; no real findings or figures.

## 17. c2-administrative-french: Reading administrative French (vocabulary · easier)

- **Goal:** understand official letters and forms, and answer them correctly.
- **Recaps:** formal correspondence and its layout (c1-formal-correspondence, b1-formal-email); the institutions (c1-culture-laicite-and-institutions).
- **New:** *une pièce justificative*, *un justificatif de domicile*, *un avis d'imposition* (named, not explained in figures), *dans un délai de* (no figures in the lesson), *à compter de*, *sous réserve de* (recap), *il vous appartient de*, *le cas échéant*, abbreviations (*cf.*, *NB*, *P.J.*, *réf.*).
- **Model:** an original letter from an invented administration, and a reply with the full layout (C1 rule).
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice, fill-in, register-shift, translate, reorder, a writing task (reply to the letter).
- **Risks:** no real procedure or deadline; the administration is invented.

## 18. c2-review-specialized: Review: Specialized French (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *un jargon*, *vulgariser*, *la vulgarisation*, *un lexique spécialisé*.
- **Model:** one invented situation (a tenant with a health problem who must answer an official letter) told in three registers: legal, medical and plain.
- **Exercises:** one per lesson, plus register-shift and a writing task (vulgarize a paragraph).
