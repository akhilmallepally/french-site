# C2 Unit 4 plan: Reading between the lines

Status: plan only. Rules: the draft "C2 conventions" in docs/plans/c2/overview.md.

C1 taught irony, understatement, rhetorical devices and free indirect speech. This unit turns them into reading tools: what a text says without saying it, how an argument works, and the French method for commenting on a text.

## 19. c2-implicit-meaning: Presuppositions and implied meaning (communication · harder)

- **Goal:** identify what a text presupposes, implies and suggests through word choice.
- **Recaps:** irony and understatement (c1-irony-and-understatement); euphemism (c1-euphemism-and-synonyms); reporting verbs that judge a claim (b2-reporting-verbs).
- **New:** *le présupposé* (*il a enfin compris*), *le sous-entendu* (recap: c1-culture-satire-and-humour), connotation, modalisation (adverbs, the conditionnel, verbs of opinion), quotation marks of distance.
- **Key vocab (new):** *un présupposé*, *la connotation*, *la modalisation*, *un modalisateur*, *prendre ses distances avec*.
- **Longer text:** an original opinion piece full of implicit judgments; a second version written neutrally.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (what is presupposed?), reformulation (make explicit), error-correction (a neutral summary that isn't), translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** analysis answers are interpretive: keep typed keys to clear-cut cases, and use multiple-choice for the rest.

## 20. c2-argument-analysis: Analysing an argument (communication · harder)

- **Goal:** name the type of each argument and spot fallacies.
- **Recaps:** argument from authority, concession and rebuttal (c1-review-rhetoric, c1-formal-debate); connectors of logic (c1-advanced-connectors).
- **New:** *l'argument par l'exemple, par l'analogie, ad hominem, la pente glissante, le faux dilemme, la généralisation hâtive, l'appel à la majorité*; implicit premises; how to answer each.
- **Key vocab (new):** the names above, *un sophisme*, *une prémisse*, *réfuter*.
- **Longer text:** an original debate transcript (invented speakers) with six named moves.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (name the move), error-correction (a fallacy), reformulation, translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** examples must not target real groups or living politicians (C1 sensitive-topic rules).

## 21. c2-commentaire-de-texte: The commentaire de texte (communication · harder)

- **Goal:** plan and write the French close commentary of a short passage.
- **Recaps:** reading literary narrative, point of view, free indirect speech (c1-reading-literary-narrative); rhetorical devices (c1-rhetorical-devices); the dissertation's plan and problématique (c1-dissertation); the commentaire named in c1-culture-literary-canon.
- **New:** the method (first impressions, *le relevé*, *les axes de lecture*, the problématique, the plan by axes, short embedded quotations, the conclusion and *l'ouverture*); the difference from a summary and from a dissertation.
- **Key vocab (new):** *un axe de lecture*, *un relevé*, *une citation enchâssée*, *une ouverture*, *le champ lexical*, *le registre* (literary sense: *lyrique, tragique, comique, épique*).
- **Longer text:** a safe-list passage of under 15 words quoted, plus an original pastiche passage (labelled) as the text to comment, and a model commentary introduction and one developed axis.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (which axis?), reorder (plan), fill-in, reformulation, a writing task (the introduction).
- **Risks:** the passage to comment is an original pastiche unless a full safe-list text under 15 words is enough; never quote later authors (C1 rule). The literary "registres" must not be confused with the language registers (familier, courant, soutenu): say so explicitly.

## 22. c2-stylistic-analysis: Style: rhythm, sound and syntax (communication · harder)

- **Goal:** describe how a sentence produces its effect: length, rhythm, sound, word order.
- **Recaps:** the alexandrine, the caesura and the mute e (c1-culture-french-poetry); stylistic inversion and fronting (c1-stylistic-inversion, c1-dislocation-and-fronting); figures (c1-rhetorical-devices).
- **New:** *la période* (the long balanced sentence), the ternary rhythm, *l'allitération*, *l'assonance*, *le chiasme*, *l'asyndète*, *la parataxe / l'hypotaxe*, the effect of the short sentence.
- **Key vocab (new):** the terms above.
- **Longer text:** two original passages on the same scene, one in long periods and one in short sentences, with a comparison.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (identify), reorder, reformulation (change the rhythm), translate, a writing task.
- **Risks:** figures already taught in C1 are recapped, not re-listed; any verse goes through scripts/scan-alexandrines.ts.

## 23. c2-reading-the-press: Press genres and styles (culture · easier)

- **Goal:** recognize press genres by their style and read each one critically.
- **Recaps:** the French press and its titles (b2-culture-french-press); the satirical press (c1-culture-satire-and-humour); summarizing an article (b2-summarizing-an-article).
- **New:** *la dépêche*, *l'éditorial*, *la chronique*, *le billet d'humeur*, *la tribune* (recap), *le reportage*, *l'enquête*, *l'interview*, *la brève*; the inverted pyramid; *le chapeau*, *l'accroche* (recap), *la titraille*; how each signals opinion.
- **Key vocab (new):** the genres and parts above.
- **Longer text:** one invented event covered in three original genres (dépêche, chronique, billet).
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (genre), register-shift, reformulation, translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** no named living journalists; no copying or close paraphrase of real articles (B2 rule); no claims about a real paper's political line beyond widely documented facts, each in review-needed.

## 24. c2-review-reading: Review: Reading between the lines (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *une lecture critique*, *décrypter*, *une grille d'analyse*.
- **Model:** an original short op-ed with a model analysis: genre, implied meaning, arguments, style.
- **Exercises:** one per lesson, plus a writing task (a short analysis).
