# C2 Unit 1 plan: Precision in form

Status: plan only. Rules: the draft "C2 conventions" in docs/plans/c2/overview.md. Key vocab lists headline items only; "(recap)" items are taught earlier and aren't new entries.

This unit refines points a C2 writer gets wrong under pressure. It re-teaches nothing: each lesson recaps the earlier rule in two or three sentences with a LessonLink, then teaches only the edge cases.

## 1. c2-agreement-pronominal-verbs: Past participles of pronominal verbs (grammar · harder)

- **Goal:** agree the past participle of any pronominal verb correctly in writing.
- **Recaps:** agreement with *être* and with a preceding direct object (a2-reflexive-passe-compose, a2-pronouns-in-passe-compose); the passive and agreement (b2-passive-voice).
- **New:** the real test: is the reflexive pronoun the direct object? *Elles se sont lavées* / *elles se sont lavé les mains* / *les mains qu'elles se sont lavées*; reciprocal verbs with an indirect pronoun (*ils se sont parlé, téléphoné, succédé, plu, souri, nui*); essentially pronominal verbs agree with the subject (*elles se sont souvenues, évanouies*); *se rendre compte* (invariable *rendu*); *se faire* + infinitive (invariable, recap of b2-faire-causatif).
- **Key vocab (new):** *un verbe pronominal*, *un verbe essentiellement pronominal*, *réciproque*, *le complément d'objet direct (COD)*, *s'évanouir*, *se succéder*, *se nuire*. Recaps: *le participe passé* (a2), *s'apercevoir que* (b1-plus-que-parfait).
- **Model:** a short news item in the passé composé with eight pronominal verbs, each agreement explained in "How the text works".
- **Exercises (16):** fill-in (agreement endings), error-correction, multiple-choice (who is the COD?), reformulation (move the COD before the verb), translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** many speakers don't hear these agreements; never present a common spoken form as "wrong" without saying it's a written-French rule. The 1990 rectifications don't change these rules (to verify).

## 2. c2-agreement-with-infinitives: Participles followed by an infinitive (grammar · harder)

- **Goal:** agree (or not) a past participle followed by an infinitive.
- **Recaps:** *faire* + infinitive and *je l'ai fait réparer* (b2-faire-causatif); verbs of perception + infinitive (check with c2-find).
- **New:** the test "does the preceding object do the action of the infinitive?" (*les musiciens que j'ai entendus jouer* / *les airs que j'ai entendu jouer*); *fait* + infinitive always invariable; *laissé* + infinitive: traditional rule and the 1990 rectification (invariable allowed), both accepted in keys; participle + *en* (*des pommes, j'en ai mangé*), with the variation explained as a rule of thumb.
- **Key vocab (new):** *les rectifications orthographiques*, *un verbe de perception*, *le sujet de l'infinitif*. Recaps: *faire réparer* (b2).
- **Model:** a review of a concert, with each participle + infinitive annotated.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (agree or not), fill-in, error-correction, translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** the *en* rule varies between grammars: state it as a rule of thumb and accept both forms where usage varies. Every claim about the 1990 rectifications goes to review-needed.

## 3. c2-tout-quelque-meme: Tout, quelque, même (grammar · harder)

- **Goal:** spell and agree *tout*, *quelque* and *même* in every function.
- **Recaps:** *tout* as determiner, pronoun and adverb, and set phrases (b2-tout); *même* and its position (c1-adverb-placement).
- **New:** adverbial *tout* before a feminine adjective starting with a consonant or an aspirated h (*toute contente*, *toutes honteuses*, but *tout étonnée*); *tout autre* (determiner or adverb); *quelque(s)* (some), *quelque* + number (about, invariable), *quelque… que* + subjunctive (concession), *quel que* + être (*quelle que soit la raison*); *même* adverb (invariable) vs adjective (*eux-mêmes*, *les mêmes*).
- **Key vocab (new):** *quel que soit*, *quelque… que*, *tout autre*, *un adverbe invariable*. Recaps: *tout de même*, *quiconque* (b2-tout).
- **Model:** an editorial paragraph where every form appears once, with a self-correction section.
- **Exercises:** fill-in (forms), multiple-choice, error-correction, reformulation (*bien que* → *quelque… que*), translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** *quel que* vs *quelque*: give the test (is it followed by *être* or a pronoun + *être*?). Concession with *quelque… que* is formal: label it.

## 4. c2-agreement-colours-compounds-numbers: Colours, compound nouns and numbers (grammar · easier)

- **Goal:** spell the plurals and agreements that even native writers check.
- **Recaps:** adjective agreement (a1-adjective-agreement); numbers (a1-numbers-21-100).
- **New:** colour adjectives from nouns (invariable: *des robes marron, orange*, with the listed exceptions *rose, mauve, fauve, écarlate…*) and compound colours (*des yeux bleu clair*); plurals of compound nouns (*des grands-parents, des porte-monnaie / porte-monnaies* after 1990); *vingt* and *cent* (*quatre-vingts*, *quatre-vingt-deux*, *deux cents*, *deux cent un*); *mille* invariable; the 1990 hyphenation of all compound numbers (*vingt-et-un*), accepted.
- **Key vocab (new):** *un nom composé*, *un adjectif de couleur*, *invariable*, *un trait d'union*.
- **Model:** an invoice-like inventory and a description of a painting, both original.
- **Exercises:** fill-in, multiple-choice, error-correction, translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** recognition of both traditional and 1990 spellings: every key accepts both where both are correct (check whether lib/check-answer.ts normalizes hyphens before writing the keys).

## 5. c2-concordance-in-writing: The full sequence of tenses (grammar · harder)

- **Goal:** keep tenses coherent across a long written text, in reported speech, subordinate clauses and the subjunctive.
- **Recaps:** reported speech in the past and the concordance (b2-reported-speech-past); the imperfect subjunctive and its modern replacements (c1-imperfect-subjunctive); the future in the past (c1-future-in-the-past).
- **New:** the full table for the indicative (present, past and future anchors); the subjunctive in modern and literary usage (recognition of the literary concordance only); when to keep the present for a general truth (*il m'a dit que la Terre est ronde*); keeping one time anchor per paragraph.
- **Key vocab (new):** *la concordance des temps* (recap: c1-imperfect-subjunctive), *une vérité générale*, *le repère temporel*, *l'antériorité*, *la postériorité*, *la simultanéité*.
- **Model:** a report of a meeting rewritten from the present to the past, sentence by sentence.
- **Exercises:** transform (present → past report), error-correction, multiple-choice, translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** the literary subjunctive concordance stays recognition-only; no typed key may require it (C1 rule).

## 6. c2-ellipsis-and-nominal-style: Ellipsis and the nominal style (grammar · harder)

- **Goal:** write and read compressed French: headlines, notes, minutes, slides, and coordinated clauses with ellipsis.
- **Recaps:** nominalisation (b2-nominalisation); the formal report (b2-formal-report); minutes (c1-leading-a-meeting).
- **New:** verb ellipsis in coordination (*Paul a choisi le train ; Marie, l'avion*); headline grammar (no article, no verb, the colon, the present for the past); note-taking style; when ellipsis is ambiguous; turning a nominal text back into full sentences (and the reverse).
- **Key vocab (new):** *l'ellipse*, *une proposition elliptique*, *le style télégraphique*, *une prise de notes*, *une puce* (bullet point).
- **Model:** a news article and the same news as five headlines and a slide.
- **Exercises:** reformulation (both directions), error-correction (an ambiguous ellipsis), multiple-choice, translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** *style télégraphique* is fine in notes, not in an essay: label where each fits.

## 7. c2-review-form: Review: Precision in form (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *la relecture* (recap? check c1-exam-written-tasks *une grille de relecture*), *une coquille* (a typo), *un correcteur / une correctrice* (a proofreader), *corriger les épreuves*.
- **Model:** an original 350-word text with ten deliberate errors to find, then the corrected text.
- **Exercises:** one per lesson, plus error-correction across the text and a writing task (proofread your own paragraph with a list).
