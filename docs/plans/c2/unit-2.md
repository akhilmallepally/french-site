# C2 Unit 2 plan: Words with precision

Status: plan only. Rules: the draft "C2 conventions" in docs/plans/c2/overview.md.

C1 taught near-synonyms by register and strength (c1-euphemism-and-synonyms), precise verbs (c1-precise-verbs) and collocations (c1-collocations). This unit adds the word problems that remain at C2: words that look alike, words with many meanings, how words are built, and the pull of English.

## 8. c2-paronyms: Paronyms (vocabulary · harder)

- **Goal:** tell apart words that look or sound alike and are often confused.
- **Recaps:** false friends (b2-false-friends); precise verbs (c1-precise-verbs).
- **New:** pairs by family: *éminent / imminent*, *conjecture / conjoncture*, *perpétrer / perpétuer*, *infecter / infester*, *prodige / prodigue*, *effraction / infraction*, *collision / collusion*, *recouvrer / recouvrir*, *acception / acceptation*, *amener / apporter* (recap if taught: check with c2-find).
- **Key vocab (new):** *un paronyme* and the pairs above, as two entries per pair (one concept per entry).
- **Model:** a short article with ten paronyms, half used correctly.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (pair in context), error-correction, fill-in, translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** distractors are always grammatical here: each sentence must make only one member of the pair possible (C1 rule on distractors).

## 9. c2-polysemy: One word, several meanings (vocabulary · harder)

- **Goal:** recognize and use the main senses of common polysemous words.
- **Recaps:** *le cadre* and *la portée* (c1-abstract-nouns); NEW_MEANINGS entries from B2 (*un bilan*, *le marché*).
- **New:** *la pièce* (room, coin, play, part, document), *la note* (mark, bill, musical note, memo), *le coup*, *la matière*, *la valeur*, *le titre*, *relever* (to note, to raise, to come under), *soutenir* (to support, to defend a thesis), *tenir* in its main constructions.
- **Key vocab (new):** *la polysémie*, *un sens propre / un sens figuré*, *une acception*; each new sense as its own entry, listed in NEW_MEANINGS with the reason.
- **Model:** a dialogue where misunderstandings come from polysemy, then resolved.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (which sense?), fill-in, reformulation, translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** NEW_MEANINGS must be updated for each repeated French form; the audit will flag the rest.

## 10. c2-word-formation: How words are built (vocabulary · easier)

- **Goal:** understand and form words with prefixes, suffixes and composition; read neologisms.
- **Recaps:** nominalisation suffixes (b2-nominalisation); abstract nouns (c1-abstract-nouns).
- **New:** prefixes (*dé-, in-/im-/il-/ir-, re-, pré-, sur-, sous-, contre-, anti-, auto-*); suffixes (*-age, -ment, -tion, -ité, -isme, -iste, -able, -iser, -ifier*); learned roots (*-logie, -cratie, -phobe, -cide*); compounding and *mots-valises*; how the press creates words.
- **Key vocab (new):** *un préfixe*, *un suffixe*, *un radical*, *un néologisme*, *un mot-valise*, *la dérivation*.
- **Model:** a short column inventing (and explaining) three neologisms, labelled as invented.
- **Exercises:** fill-in (build the word), multiple-choice (meaning from parts), error-correction (wrong suffix), translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** not every regular-looking derivation exists: each formed word in a key must be a real dictionary word (check against a dictionary, list in review-needed).

## 11. c2-advanced-false-friends: Advanced false friends (vocabulary · harder)

- **Goal:** avoid the false friends that remain after B2, especially in formal and professional writing.
- **Recaps:** the B2 list (b2-false-friends): check which of these are already taught before writing.
- **New (subject to c2-find):** *achever*, *versatile*, *sensible*, *chance*, *délai*, *formel*, *conséquent*, *opportunité*, *contrôler*, *réaliser* (to carry out vs to realize, and the debated anglicized use), *supporter*, *éventuellement*, *actuellement*: only those not yet taught.
- **Key vocab (new):** each false friend with its real meaning and the French for the English word.
- **Model:** a business email full of false friends, then corrected.
- **Exercises:** error-correction, translate (EN → FR), multiple-choice, reformulation, reorder.
- **Risks:** some uses are anglicisms now common in French (*réaliser que*, *opportunité*): label the register and the debate rather than marking them wrong (C1 rule: label before you correct).

## 12. c2-anglicisms-and-official-terms: Anglicisms and their official replacements (vocabulary · easier)

- **Goal:** know the common anglicisms, their official French replacements, and which ones people actually use.
- **Recaps:** *courriel* (c1-culture-francophonie, b1-culture-quebec).
- **New:** *mot-dièse* (hashtag), *infolettre*, *divulgâcher*, *écrasement* vs *crash*, *mercatique* (marketing), *covoiturage*, *baladodiffusion* (Québec) / *podcast*, *logiciel*, *ordinateur*; how official terms are published (the French terminology commission and FranceTerme; Québec's Office québécois de la langue française); the Loi Toubon in one neutral paragraph.
- **Key vocab (new):** *un anglicisme*, *un équivalent officiel*, *recommander un terme*, *le Journal officiel* (recap: c1-law-and-institutions).
- **Model:** an original article on an invented company's "language week", using both forms with labels.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (official term), register-shift, error-correction, translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** every date and institution goes to review-needed; present the debate (purism vs usage) neutrally, attributed; don't claim an official term is "the correct one" in speech.

## 13. c2-review-words: Review: Words with precision (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *le mot juste*, *une impropriété*, *un barbarisme*, *une faute de sens*.
- **Model:** an original 350-word text where the writer replaces ten imprecise words, with a "before / after" table.
- **Exercises:** one per lesson, plus error-correction and a writing task.
