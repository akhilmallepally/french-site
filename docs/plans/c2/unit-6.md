# C2 Unit 6 plan: Mediation and negotiation

Status: plan only. Rules: the draft "C2 conventions" in docs/plans/c2/overview.md.

Speaking skills practised in writing: the site is text-only, so each lesson works through scripts, transcripts and written exchanges. C1 already taught debate, speeches, diplomacy, meetings and the short exposé; this unit adds what C2 asks for: mediating between people and texts, negotiating towards an agreement, and building a structured oral argument.

## 31. c2-mediation: Explaining a text to someone else (communication · harder)

- **Goal:** relay the content of a document to a person who hasn't read it, adapted to their needs and register.
- **Recaps:** synthesis and attribution (c1-synthesis); register shifting (c1-register-shifting); reformulating a question (c1-exam-oral-presentation).
- **New:** selecting for the listener, simplifying without distorting, signalling what the source says vs what you add (*le texte dit que… ; à mon avis…*), handling a question you can't answer from the document.
- **Key vocab (new):** *servir d'intermédiaire*, *relayer une information*, *en substance*, *si je résume*, *le texte ne précise pas*.
- **Longer text:** an original notice from an invented town hall and a model message explaining it to an elderly neighbour (written dialogue).
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (faithful or not?), register-shift, reformulation, translate, reorder, a writing task.
- **Risks:** the mediator's summary must match the source exactly (C1 rule on summaries).

## 32. c2-negotiation: Negotiating in writing (communication · harder)

- **Goal:** lead a negotiation by email to an agreement: opening position, concessions, conditions, closing.
- **Recaps:** negotiating vocabulary (b2-negotiating); diplomatic language (c1-diplomatic-language); hypothesis (*pour peu que*, *à supposer que*: c1-concession-and-hypothesis).
- **New:** anchoring, conditional offers (*nous pourrions… à condition que…*), trading concessions, saying no without closing the door, summarizing an agreement in writing (*sous réserve de* recap), the final confirmation.
- **Key vocab (new):** *une contre-proposition*, *une marge de négociation*, *un terrain d'entente*, *trouver un compromis*, *acter*.
- **Longer text:** an original four-email exchange between two invented companies.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (the best next move), register-shift, error-correction, translate, reorder, a writing task (the closing email).
- **Risks:** every fact in later emails matches the earlier ones (C1 rule on facts across texts); no real companies.

## 33. c2-structured-oral-argument: A structured oral argument (communication · harder)

- **Goal:** prepare and write out a spoken argument of a few minutes: a clear thesis, signposted reasons, a rebuttal, a memorable close.
- **Recaps:** public speaking (c1-public-speaking); formal debate (c1-formal-debate); spoken markers (c1-spoken-discourse-markers).
- **New:** the oral plan (announce, develop, recap), spoken signposting vs written connectors, anticipating the objection, the strength of repetition in speech, timing by the number of points (no figures for timing).
- **Key vocab (new):** *un fil rouge*, *une objection*, *prévenir une objection*, *marteler*, *en un mot*.
- **Longer text:** an original speech transcript, with margin notes on its structure.
- **Exercises:** reorder (the argument), multiple-choice, reformulation (written → spoken), translate, a writing task.
- **Risks:** don't repeat C1's six-step speech plan: recap it and add only the C2 skills.

## 34. c2-interviews-and-questioning: Asking precise questions (communication · easier)

- **Goal:** prepare and conduct an interview in writing: open, closed and follow-up questions, reformulation, politely pressing for an answer.
- **Recaps:** questions and inversion (a1-yes-no-questions, b2-reported-questions-orders, c1-stylistic-inversion).
- **New:** *relancer*, *recadrer* (recap: c1-leading-a-meeting), *une question ouverte / fermée*, *une question piège*, *revenir à la charge*, *si je peux me permettre*.
- **Longer text:** an original interview transcript (an invented craftsperson) with the interviewer's notes.
- **Exercises:** multiple-choice (which question type?), reformulation, error-correction (a leading question), translate, reorder.
- **Risks:** the interviewee is invented; no real people.

## 35. c2-review-mediation: Review: Mediation and negotiation (review · easier)

- **New words (3 to 8):** *un médiateur / une médiatrice*, *une médiation*, *désamorcer un conflit*.
- **Model:** an invented neighbourhood dispute resolved through mediation, as a written exchange.
- **Exercises:** one per lesson, plus a writing task.
