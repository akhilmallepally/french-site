# B1 Unit 1 plan: Opinions and feelings

Status: draft for approval. No lesson files written yet.

Format of each entry: vocab is French followed by the English gloss (respellings come later). "Not yet taught" lists structures the lesson would naturally reach for but that belong to a later lesson; each one is either avoided or used as a labelled fixed phrase (and then listed in docs/review-needed.md).

---

## 1. b1-expressing-opinions: Expressing opinions

- **Type:** communication · **Order:** 1
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-comparatives: opinions are often given by comparing two options (*c'est plus intéressant que…*). It implies a1-likes-and-dislikes, which the syllabus also listed.
- **Teaches:** *à mon avis*, *selon moi*, *d'après moi*, *pour moi* (known from a1-ordering-at-a-cafe, now for opinions); *je pense que*, *je crois que*, *je trouve que* + indicative; *trouver* + noun + adjective (*je trouve ce film long*); asking: *qu'est-ce que tu en penses ?*, *tu trouves ?*; hedging with *ça dépend*.
- **Not yet taught:** *je ne pense pas que* + subjunctive (b1-expressing-doubt-certainty). Avoided: negative opinions use *je ne suis pas d'accord* or *je trouve que… ne… pas*.
- **Vocab (16):**
  - *à mon avis*: in my opinion
  - *selon moi*: in my view
  - *d'après moi*: as I see it
  - *à vrai dire*: to tell the truth
  - *penser que*: to think that
  - *croire que*: to believe that
  - *trouver que*: to find that, to think that
  - *Qu'est-ce que tu en penses ?*: What do you think of it?
  - *Tu trouves ?*: Do you think so?
  - *Ça dépend.*: It depends.
  - *un avis*: an opinion
  - *un point de vue*: a point of view
  - *original / originale*: original, unusual
  - *ennuyeux / ennuyeuse*: boring
  - *génial / géniale*: great, brilliant
  - *franchement*: honestly, frankly
- **Dialogue:** Two flatmates compare their opinions of a new café and a TV series, each asking *qu'est-ce que tu en penses ?*.
- **Longer text:** A group-chat thread in which four friends choose a weekend activity, each giving a reasoned opinion.
- **Production tasks:** translate two opinion sentences; write out a short paragraph about your town using three different opinion markers.
- **Needs review:** "*Je pense que* and *je crois que* take the indicative when they're affirmative." "*Je trouve que* suggests an opinion based on your own experience." "*Selon moi* and *d'après moi* mean the same in everyday speech."

## 2. b1-agreeing-disagreeing: Agreeing and disagreeing

- **Type:** communication · **Order:** 2
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-expressing-opinions: you agree or disagree with the opinions built there.
- **Teaches:** *je suis d'accord (avec toi)* / *je ne suis pas d'accord*; *tu as raison* / *tu as tort*; *c'est vrai*, *tout à fait*, *absolument*, *pas du tout*; partial agreement (*oui, mais…*, *ce n'est pas faux*, *d'un côté… de l'autre*); *moi si* (with *moi aussi* and *moi non plus* revisited from a2-stressed-pronouns); *je ne suis pas sûr / sûre* on its own.
- **Not yet taught:** *je ne suis pas sûr que* + subjunctive (b1-expressing-doubt-certainty). Avoided.
- **Vocab (17):**
  - *être d'accord (avec)*: to agree (with)
  - *avoir raison*: to be right
  - *avoir tort*: to be wrong
  - *tout à fait*: absolutely, quite
  - *pas du tout*: not at all
  - *absolument*: absolutely
  - *c'est vrai*: that's true
  - *ce n'est pas faux*: that's a fair point
  - *je ne suis pas sûr / sûre*: I'm not sure
  - *en partie*: partly
  - *je suis du même avis*: I see it the same way
  - *moi si*: I do, I am (contradicting a negative)
  - *au contraire*: on the contrary
  - *exactement*: exactly
  - *bien sûr*: of course
  - *d'un côté… de l'autre*: on the one hand… on the other
  - *un désaccord*: a disagreement
- **Dialogue:** Two colleagues (*vous*) disagree politely about working from home, ending in partial agreement.
- **Longer text:** A forum thread on whether shops should open on Sundays, with three replies that agree, disagree and half agree.
- **Production tasks:** translate "I don't agree with you at all"; write a three-sentence reply that partly agrees.
- **Needs review:** "*Ce n'est pas faux* is a polite way to concede a point." "*Tu as tort* can sound blunt with someone you don't know." "*Moi si* contradicts a negative statement."

## 3. b1-ce-qui-ce-que: ce qui, ce que

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 3
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-relative-qui-que-ou: *ce qui* and *ce que* extend *qui* and *que*.
- **Teaches:** *ce qui* (subject) and *ce que* (object) for "what"; *ce qui me plaît, c'est…*; *ce que j'aime, c'est…*; *tout ce qui* / *tout ce que*; elision *ce qu'il*. Reuses: opinions.
- **Not yet taught:** indirect questions (*je ne sais pas ce que tu veux*) belong to b1-reported-speech-present: avoided here. *ce dont* belongs to B2 (b2-relative-prepositions): avoided.
- **Vocab (14):**
  - *ce qui*: what (as subject)
  - *ce que / ce qu'*: what (as object)
  - *tout ce qui*: everything that (as subject)
  - *tout ce que*: everything that (as object)
  - *plaire à*: to please, to appeal to
  - *Ce qui me plaît, c'est…*: What I like is…
  - *Ce qui compte, c'est…*: What matters is…
  - *intéresser*: to interest
  - *énerver*: to annoy
  - *choquer*: to shock
  - *gêner*: to bother
  - *surprendre*: to surprise
  - *comprendre*: to understand
  - *la vérité*: the truth
- **Dialogue:** Two friends leaving a concert say what they liked and what bothered them.
- **Short text:** A blog paragraph, *Ce que j'aime à Lyon*.
- **Production tasks:** rewrite two sentences with *ce qui… c'est*; translate "Everything you say is true."
- **Needs review:** "Use *ce qui* when a verb follows (an object pronoun may come first: *ce qui me plaît*), and *ce que* when a subject follows."

## 4. b1-relative-dont: The relative pronoun dont

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 4
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-verbs-with-a-and-de: *dont* replaces *de* + noun after the de-verbs taught there. It implies a2-relative-qui-que-ou, which the syllabus also listed.
- **Teaches:** *dont* with verbs and expressions + *de* (*parler de*, *avoir besoin de*, *rêver de*, *se souvenir de*, *s'occuper de*); *dont* for possession (*l'ami dont le père est médecin*); choosing between *que* and *dont*. Reuses: opinions, emotions.
- **Not yet taught:** *duquel*, *de laquelle* (b2-relative-lequel): avoided.
- **Vocab (14):**
  - *dont*: of which, whose, about which
  - *rêver de*: to dream of
  - *se souvenir de*: to remember
  - *s'occuper de*: to look after, to deal with
  - *se servir de*: to use
  - *manquer de*: to lack
  - *se moquer de*: to make fun of
  - *avoir honte de*: to be ashamed of
  - *être responsable de*: to be in charge of
  - *le rêve*: the dream
  - *un outil*: a tool
  - *un projet*: a project, a plan
  - *une raison*: a reason
  - *une époque*: a period, a time
- **Dialogue:** A grandmother shows photos and talks about the people and places *dont elle se souvient*.
- **Production tasks:** join two sentences with *dont* (twice); translate "It's the book I need."
- **Needs review:** "*Dont* replaces *de* + a noun, and it never changes form." "After *dont*, the word order stays subject, verb, object: *l'ami dont le père est médecin*."

## 5. b1-relationships: Relationships and feelings

- **Type:** vocabulary · **Order:** 5
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-emotions-and-feelings: builds on the feelings vocabulary.
- **Teaches:** friendship, love and conflict vocabulary; reciprocal verbs in the present and passé composé (revisited); *tomber amoureux de* with *être*.
- **Not yet taught:** *j'ai peur qu'il parte* (b1-subjonctif-wishes-emotions): avoided.
- **Vocab (21):**
  - *un ami proche / une amie proche*: a close friend
  - *l'amitié*: friendship
  - *l'amour*: love
  - *un couple*: a couple
  - *être en couple*: to be in a relationship
  - *un / une célibataire*: a single person
  - *s'entendre bien avec*: to get on well with
  - *se disputer (avec)*: to argue (with)
  - *une dispute*: an argument
  - *se réconcilier*: to make up
  - *tomber amoureux / amoureuse de*: to fall in love with
  - *se marier (avec)*: to get married (to)
  - *le mariage*: marriage, a wedding
  - *se séparer*: to separate
  - *rompre (avec)*: to break up (with)
  - *divorcer*: to get divorced
  - *faire confiance à*: to trust
  - *la confiance*: trust
  - *la jalousie*: jealousy
  - *un conflit*: a conflict
  - *se faire des amis*: to make friends
- **Dialogue:** Two friends (*tu*) talk about a couple who argued and then made up.
- **Short text:** A letter to an advice column from someone who argued with their best friend.
- **Production tasks:** translate "We get on well."; write four sentences about a friendship in the passé composé.
- **Needs review:** "*Rompre* is the usual verb for breaking up." "*Tomber amoureux* takes *être* in the passé composé." "*Mon copain / ma copine* often means boyfriend / girlfriend."

## 6. b1-review-opinions: Review: Opinions and feelings

- **Type:** review · **Order:** 6
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-agreeing-disagreeing: the reply that disagrees.
  - b1-ce-qui-ce-que: *ce qui me plaît, c'est…* in the model.
  - b1-relative-dont: a *dont* sentence in the model.
  - b1-relationships: the topic of the model (friendship).
- **Teaches:** the shape of an opinion piece (position, two reasons with examples, conclusion) and of a reply that disagrees.
- **Not yet taught:** *je ne pense pas que* + subjunctive: avoided.
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *un texte d'opinion*: an opinion piece
  - *un lecteur / une lectrice*: a reader
  - *réagir*: to react
  - *une réaction*: a reaction
  - *de plus*: moreover
  - *par exemple*: for example
  - *en conclusion*: in conclusion
  - *convaincre*: to convince
  - *convaincant / convaincante*: convincing
  - *un sujet*: a topic
  - *un argument*: an argument, a point
  - *partager*: to share
- **Model text:** An opinion piece answering "Is it better to have many friends or a few close friends?", followed by a reader's reply that disagrees.
- **Production tasks:** the writing task itself; one write-out paragraph exercise; translate a disagreeing sentence.
- **Needs review:** "*Un argument* means a point in a discussion, not a quarrel."
