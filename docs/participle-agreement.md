# Participle agreement with a preceding direct object

**Finding: it's taught at A2 and recycled at B1.**

## Where it's taught

- **a2-pronouns-in-passe-compose:** the main rule with *avoir*: *je l'ai vue*, *je les ai vus*, *il m'a vue*. There's no agreement with *lui*, *leur*, *y*, *en* or an indirect *me* / *te* (*elle m'a téléphoné*). It has a table, a warning and exercises.
- **a2-relative-qui-que-ou:** agreement after *que*: *la lettre que j'ai écrite*.
- **a2-passe-compose-etre:** *être* verbs agree with the subject.
- **a2-reflexive-passe-compose:** pronominal verbs agree with the subject. There's no agreement when a direct object follows (*elle s'est cassé le bras*). *Ils se sont vus* agrees; *se téléphoner* doesn't.
- **Recycled:** a2-review-pronouns, a2-review-health, b1-double-object-pronouns (*je les lui ai données*), b1-plus-que-parfait (*les clés que j'avais oubliées*), b1-ce-qui-ce-que.
- **Planned:** b2-passive-voice (agreement in the passive) and b2-faire-causatif (*fait* + infinitive doesn't agree).

## What's missing

- **Pronominal verbs with a direct object before them:** *les mains qu'elle s'est lavées*.
- **Reciprocal verbs with an indirect pronoun, beyond *se téléphoner*:** *ils se sont parlé*.
- **The new B2 compound tenses:** no lesson says the *avoir* rule also applies in the conditionnel passé, futur antérieur and subjonctif passé (*je l'aurais vue*).
- **Participle + infinitive** (*je les ai entendus chanter*): leave it for C1.
