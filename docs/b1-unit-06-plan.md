## 35. b1-media-and-news: Media and news

- **Type:** vocabulary · **Order:** 35
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-expressing-opinions: reacting to the news.
- **Teaches:** newspapers, broadcasting and online news; headline style.
- **Not yet taught:** headlines with a bare participle (*Le musée fermé dimanche*) look like the passive (b1-passive-voice-intro). Shown only as "headline style", listed in review-needed.md.
- **Vocab (21):**
  - *les infos / les informations*: the news
  - *le journal télévisé / le JT*: the TV news
  - *un quotidien*: a daily paper
  - *un hebdomadaire*: a weekly
  - *un magazine*: a magazine
  - *un éditorial*: an editorial
  - *un titre*: a headline
  - *à la une*: on the front page
  - *un reportage*: a report (on TV or radio)
  - *une émission*: a programme
  - *une chaîne*: a channel
  - *la presse*: the press
  - *l'actualité*: current affairs
  - *un événement*: an event
  - *une enquête*: an investigation, a survey
  - *une interview*: an interview (in the media)
  - *s'informer*: to keep informed
  - *une source*: a source
  - *fiable*: reliable
  - *une fausse information*: a piece of fake news
  - *en direct*: live
- **Dialogue:** Flatmates react to the headlines over breakfast.
- **Production tasks:** translate "I read the news on my phone."; write out three headlines as full sentences.
- **Needs review:** "*Le journal* (from a2-irregular-verbs-present) also means the TV news, short for *le journal télévisé*." "*Une interview* is for the media, *un entretien* for a job." "Headlines often drop the verb *être*."

## 36. b1-technology-and-internet: Technology and the internet

- **Type:** vocabulary · **Order:** 36
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-verbs-with-a-and-de: *essayer de se connecter*, *oublier de recharger*.
- **Teaches:** phones, computers, apps, accounts and social media.
- **Not yet taught:** *lequel* (b1-interrogative-lequel): avoided.
- **Vocab (23):**
  - *un smartphone*: a smartphone
  - *une application / une appli*: an app
  - *un ordinateur portable*: a laptop
  - *une tablette*: a tablet
  - *un écran*: a screen
  - *télécharger*: to download
  - *se connecter*: to log in
  - *se déconnecter*: to log out
  - *un mot de passe*: a password
  - *un identifiant*: a username
  - *un compte*: an account
  - *les réseaux sociaux*: social media
  - *publier*: to post
  - *un abonné / une abonnée*: a follower, a subscriber
  - *un lien*: a link
  - *un site*: a website
  - *en ligne*: online
  - *la batterie*: the battery
  - *recharger*: to charge
  - *mettre à jour*: to update
  - *une mise à jour*: an update
  - *enregistrer*: to save
  - *supprimer*: to delete
- **Dialogue:** A grandparent asks a grandchild for help setting up a video call.
- **Production tasks:** translate "I forgot my password."; write out instructions using the imperative.
- **Needs review:** "*Un portable* means a mobile phone, and *un ordinateur portable* a laptop." "*Courriel* is the official word for email, but *mail* is common in France."

## 37. b1-reported-speech-present: Reported speech (present)

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 37
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-ce-qui-ce-que: *demander ce que*, *demander ce qui*. It implies a2-indirect-object-pronouns, which the syllabus also listed.
- **Teaches:** *dire que*, *répondre que*, *expliquer que*; *demander si*, *demander ce que* / *ce qui*, *demander où / quand / comment / pourquoi*; pronoun and possessive changes (*je* → *il*, *mon* → *son*); no inversion and no *est-ce que* in indirect questions; *qu'est-ce que* → *ce que*. Reuses: media and news, phone calls.
- **Not yet taught:** reporting in the past and tense changes (b2-reported-speech-past), *dire de* + infinitive (b2-reported-questions-orders): avoided. The reporting verb stays in the present.
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *dire que*: to say that
  - *répondre que*: to reply that
  - *expliquer que*: to explain that
  - *demander si*: to ask whether
  - *demander ce que / ce qui*: to ask what
  - *vouloir savoir si*: to want to know whether
  - *se demander si*: to wonder whether
  - *préciser*: to specify, to add
  - *un appel manqué*: a missed call
  - *un / une porte-parole*: a spokesperson
  - *une déclaration*: a statement
  - *un témoin*: a witness
- **Dialogue:** Someone relays a voice message to a flatmate: *Il dit qu'il arrive à huit heures, et il demande si tu peux l'attendre.*
- **Production tasks:** change three direct questions into reported ones; translate "She asks what you want."
- **Needs review:** "In an indirect question, *qu'est-ce que* becomes *ce que* and *qu'est-ce qui* becomes *ce qui*." "There's no inversion after *demander si*."

## 38. b1-gerondif: The gérondif

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 38
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-time-connectors: the gérondif is another way to say "while". It implies a2-irregular-verbs-present (the *nous* stems), which the syllabus also listed.
- **Teaches:** *en* + *nous* stem + *-ant*; irregular *étant*, *ayant*, *sachant*; simultaneity, manner and means; *tout en* for doing two things at once; the same-subject rule. Reuses: technology habits, daily routine.
- **Not yet taught:** the present participle without *en* (b2-participles-and-gerund): avoided.
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *en* + *-ant*: while …ing, by …ing
  - *en marchant*: while walking
  - *en écoutant*: while listening
  - *en faisant*: while doing, by doing
  - *étant*: being
  - *ayant*: having
  - *sachant*: knowing
  - *tout en*: while (at the same time)
  - *En attendant…*: In the meantime…
  - *un casque*: headphones
  - *un podcast*: a podcast
  - *distrait / distraite*: distracted
- **Dialogue:** Two friends compare what they do on their phones while walking, cooking and travelling.
- **Production tasks:** join pairs of sentences with a gérondif; translate "I learn French by listening to podcasts."
- **Needs review:** "The gérondif must share the subject of the main verb." "*En attendant* is also a fixed phrase meaning 'meanwhile'."

## 39. b1-indefinite-pronouns: Indefinite words

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 39
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-negative-expressions: *quelqu'un*, *quelque chose*, *ne… personne* and *ne… rien*.
- **Teaches:** *chaque* / *chacun*, *aucun* (*ne… aucun*), *plusieurs*, *quelques* / *quelques-uns*, *certains*, *d'autres*, *n'importe qui / quoi / où / quand*, *quelque chose de* + adjective. Reuses: social media, news.
- **Not yet taught:** *tout* in all its uses (b2-tout): only *tout le monde* (already known) appears.
- **Vocab (16):**
  - *chaque*: each, every
  - *chacun / chacune*: each one
  - *aucun / aucune*: no, none
  - *ne … aucun*: not a single
  - *plusieurs*: several
  - *quelques*: a few
  - *quelques-uns / quelques-unes*: a few (of them)
  - *certains / certaines*: some (people)
  - *d'autres*: others
  - *n'importe qui*: anyone at all
  - *n'importe quoi*: anything at all; nonsense
  - *n'importe où*: anywhere
  - *n'importe quand*: any time
  - *quelque chose de* + adjective: something (interesting, new…)
  - *un commentaire*: a comment
  - *un utilisateur / une utilisatrice*: a user
- **Dialogue:** Two friends read the comments under a post: *chacun dit n'importe quoi !*
- **Production tasks:** translate "Each person has an account."; rewrite sentences with *ne… aucun*.
- **Needs review:** "*Chaque* is always singular." "*Tu dis n'importe quoi* means 'you're talking nonsense'." "*Plusieurs* never takes an article."

## 40. b1-interrogative-lequel: Lequel, laquelle

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 40
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-demonstrative-pronouns: *celui-ci ou celui-là ?* is the natural answer to *lequel ?*. It implies a1-question-words, which the syllabus also listed.
- **Teaches:** *lequel, laquelle, lesquels, lesquelles* for choices; *auquel*, *duquel* and their forms in questions; *quel* + noun versus *lequel*. Reuses: technology choices, shopping.
- **Not yet taught:** relative *lequel* after prepositions (b2-relative-lequel): avoided.
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *lequel / laquelle*: which one
  - *lesquels / lesquelles*: which ones
  - *auquel / à laquelle*: to which one
  - *duquel / de laquelle*: of which one, about which one
  - *un forfait*: a phone plan
  - *une marque*: a brand
  - *une option*: an option
  - *la mémoire*: storage, memory
  - *un appareil*: a device
  - *une garantie*: a guarantee, a warranty
  - *une offre*: an offer
  - *une réduction*: a discount
- **Dialogue:** A customer in a phone shop chooses between two models and two plans.
- **Production tasks:** translate "Which one do you prefer?"; rewrite *quel* + noun questions with *lequel*.
- **Needs review:** "*Lequel* agrees with the noun it replaces." "*À* and *de* contract with *lequel*: *auquel*, *duquel*."

## 41. b1-review-media: Review: Media and technology

- **Type:** review · **Order:** 41
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-media-and-news, b1-technology-and-internet, b1-reported-speech-present, b1-gerondif, b1-indefinite-pronouns, b1-interrogative-lequel: the unit's six lessons, none of which implies another (six is the maximum).
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *un fait divers*: a news item (crime, accidents, oddities)
  - *un témoignage*: a testimony, an account
  - *devenir viral*: to go viral
  - *une vidéo*: a video
  - *la vie privée*: privacy
  - *les données*: data
  - *la désinformation*: disinformation
  - *vérifier*: to check
  - *une capture d'écran*: a screenshot
  - *signaler*: to report (a post)
  - *un internaute / une internaute*: an internet user
  - *partager une publication*: to share a post
- **Model text:** A short news item about a lost dog found thanks to social media, then a report of what it says and a reaction.
- **Production tasks:** report the news item in writing (write-out); one translation; one reorder.
- **Needs review:** "*Un fait divers* covers minor crime, accidents and odd stories."
