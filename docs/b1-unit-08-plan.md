## 48. b1-housing-and-renting: Housing and renting

- **Type:** vocabulary · **Order:** 48
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-home-and-chores: rooms and furniture.
- **Teaches:** flat hunting, renting, sharing and moving.
- **Not yet taught:** none.
- **Vocab (21):**
  - *louer*: to rent
  - *un / une locataire*: a tenant
  - *un / une propriétaire*: a landlord, an owner
  - *le loyer*: the rent
  - *les charges*: service charges
  - *charges comprises*: charges included
  - *le bail*: the lease
  - *la caution*: the deposit
  - *une agence immobilière*: an estate agent's
  - *la colocation*: flat-sharing
  - *un studio*: a studio flat
  - *meublé / meublée*: furnished
  - *un état des lieux*: an inventory check
  - *un déménagement*: a move (house)
  - *emménager*: to move in
  - *un garant / une garante*: a guarantor
  - *lumineux / lumineuse*: bright
  - *le chauffage*: the heating
  - *une assurance habitation*: home insurance
  - *une visite*: a viewing
  - *la surface*: the floor area
- **Dialogue:** A viewing of a flat with the landlord, *vous* throughout.
- **Production tasks:** translate "The rent is 700 euros, charges included."; write out a flat-share ad.
- **Needs review:** "An *état des lieux* is done when you move in and when you move out." "Landlords often ask for a *garant*." (Leave legal amounts such as the size of the deposit out of the lesson.)

## 49. b1-money-and-banking: Money and banking

- **Type:** vocabulary · **Order:** 49
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a1-shopping-for-clothes: prices and paying.
- **Teaches:** accounts, cards, paying, saving, borrowing and budgets.
- **Not yet taught:** *ne… que* (b1-ne-que-ni-ni): avoided; use *seulement*.
- **Vocab (22):**
  - *un compte en banque*: a bank account
  - *ouvrir un compte*: to open an account
  - *une carte bancaire*: a bank card
  - *un distributeur*: a cash machine
  - *retirer de l'argent*: to withdraw money
  - *payer en espèces*: to pay cash
  - *payer par carte*: to pay by card
  - *le code*: the PIN
  - *un virement*: a bank transfer
  - *faire des économies*: to save money
  - *emprunter*: to borrow
  - *un prêt*: a loan
  - *rembourser*: to pay back
  - *le budget*: the budget
  - *dépenser*: to spend
  - *une dépense*: an expense
  - *un relevé de compte*: a bank statement
  - *les frais*: fees, charges
  - *être à découvert*: to be overdrawn
  - *le conseiller / la conseillère*: the bank adviser
  - *sans contact*: contactless
  - *seulement*: only
- **Dialogue:** A student opens a bank account, *vous* throughout.
- **Production tasks:** translate "I'd like to open an account."; write out a monthly budget in five sentences.
- **Needs review:** "*Carte bleue* is often used for any bank card." "*Être à découvert* means being overdrawn."

## 50. b1-ne-que-ni-ni: ne…que and ni…ni

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 50
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-negative-expressions: *ne… plus*, *ne… rien* and the other two-part negatives.
- **Teaches:** *ne… que* for restriction (*je n'ai que dix euros*), placed before the restricted word, and *seulement* (from lesson 49) and *uniquement* as alternatives; *ne… ni… ni* (*je n'ai ni frère ni sœur*), with the article dropped after *ni*; *ne… plus que*; *il ne reste que*. Reuses: money, housing.
- **Not yet taught:** none.
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *ne … que*: only
  - *uniquement*: only, solely
  - *ne … ni … ni*: neither … nor
  - *ni l'un ni l'autre*: neither (of them)
  - *ne … plus que*: only … left
  - *il ne reste que*: there's only … left
  - *à peine*: barely
  - *dépensier / dépensière*: a big spender
  - *la monnaie*: change (coins)
  - *une pièce*: a coin
  - *un billet*: a banknote
  - *fauché / fauchée*: broke (informal)
- **Dialogue:** Two students check their money at the end of the month: *je n'ai que dix euros !*
- **Production tasks:** rewrite sentences with *ne… que*; translate "I have neither time nor money."
- **Needs review:** "*Ne… que* isn't a negative, so the article doesn't change to *de*: *je n'ai que des pièces*." "After *ni*, the indefinite and partitive articles disappear." "*Une pièce* (a coin) and *un billet* (a banknote) are new meanings of words already taught as 'room' and 'ticket'."

## 51. b1-making-a-complaint: Making a complaint

- **Type:** communication · **Order:** 51
- **Prerequisites (changed from the syllabus):**
  - b1-cover-letter: the formal letter layout and closings that the written complaint reuses. It implies b1-conditionnel-present, which the syllabus listed.
  - b1-housing-and-renting: the tenant–landlord setting.
- **Teaches:** complaining politely but firmly in person (*Excusez-moi de vous déranger, mais…*, *il y a un problème avec…*, *ce n'est pas normal*, *est-ce qu'il serait possible de…*, *je voudrais que vous* + subjunctive) and in writing; asking for a solution (refund, exchange, repair); escalating (*sinon, je serai obligé de…*).
- **Not yet taught:** *se faire rembourser* (*faire* + infinitive, b2-faire-causatif): avoided; use *être remboursé*.
- **Vocab (17):**
  - *se plaindre (de)*: to complain (about)
  - *une plainte*: a complaint
  - *une réclamation*: a formal complaint (to a company)
  - *Ce n'est pas normal.*: That's not acceptable.
  - *une panne*: a breakdown
  - *être en panne*: to be out of order
  - *une fuite*: a leak
  - *réparer*: to repair
  - *une réparation*: a repair
  - *un remboursement*: a refund
  - *être remboursé / remboursée*: to get your money back
  - *un échange*: an exchange
  - *le service client*: customer service
  - *insatisfait / insatisfaite*: dissatisfied
  - *dans les plus brefs délais*: as soon as possible (formal)
  - *exiger*: to demand
  - *être obligé / obligée de*: to be forced to
- **Dialogue:** A tenant phones the landlord about a broken heater, *vous* throughout.
- **Longer text:** A formal complaint letter to the landlord about the heating, with a translation.
- **Production tasks:** write out a complaint paragraph; translate "The heating has been broken for a week."
- **Needs review:** "*Une réclamation* is the word companies use for a formal complaint." "*Dans les plus brefs délais* is formal written French."

## 52. b1-travel-problems: Travel problems

- **Type:** communication · **Order:** 52
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-booking-a-hotel: hotel vocabulary.
  - b1-plus-que-parfait: *j'avais réservé une chambre*.
- **Teaches:** explaining what went wrong with the plus-que-parfait; lost luggage, delays, cancellations and missed connections; asking for compensation at the desk, on the phone and by email.
- **Not yet taught:** *on m'a dit que le vol serait…* (reported speech in the past, b2-reported-speech-past): avoided.
- **Vocab (16):**
  - *l'enregistrement*: check-in
  - *une escale*: a stopover
  - *être retardé / retardée*: to be delayed
  - *le tapis à bagages*: the baggage carousel
  - *rater sa correspondance*: to miss your connection
  - *perdu / perdue*: lost
  - *le comptoir*: the desk
  - *une déclaration de perte*: a loss report
  - *une indemnisation*: compensation
  - *surbooké / surbookée*: overbooked
  - *l'embarquement*: boarding
  - *la porte d'embarquement*: the boarding gate
  - *une carte d'embarquement*: a boarding pass
  - *le guichet*: the ticket office
  - *un bagage à main*: a piece of hand luggage
  - *la livraison*: delivery
- **Dialogue:** A traveller at the airline desk whose suitcase hasn't arrived, *vous* throughout.
- **Longer text:** An email to a hotel explaining that a booking had been lost: *j'avais réservé deux nuits…*
- **Production tasks:** translate "I had booked a room for two nights."; write out the email from prompts.
- **Needs review:** "*Rater sa correspondance* builds on *la correspondance* from a2-travel-and-transport." Don't state compensation rules (amounts, EU regulations) in the lesson.

  - b1-making-a-complaint: the complaint email.
  - b1-travel-problems: the airline setting.
- **Vocab (12):**
  - *un litige*: a dispute
  - *un dédommagement*: compensation
  - *une facture*: a bill, an invoice
  - *une erreur*: a mistake
  - *facturer*: to charge
  - *une compagnie aérienne*: an airline
  - *la responsabilité*: responsibility
  - *un justificatif*: proof, a receipt
  - *joindre*: to attach
  - *ci-joint*: attached (fixed phrase)
  - *une copie*: a copy
  - *un numéro de dossier*: a reference number
- **Model text:** A complaint email to an airline about a cancelled flight and the hotel night it cost.
- **Production tasks:** the writing task; one write-out paragraph; one translation with *ne… que*.
- **Needs review:** See the overview, section (e): this review asks for a formal email before b1-formal-email (57) teaches it.
