## 7. b1-si-present-futur: Si + present, future

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 7
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-futur-simple: the result clause uses the futur simple.
- **Teaches:** real conditions *si* + present, futur (*s'il pleut, on restera à la maison*); *si* + present, imperative; *si* + present, present for general truths; *si* elides only before *il* and *ils*; *sinon*. Reuses: travel plans, weather.
- **Not yet taught:** *même si*, *sauf si* (b2-mixed-conditionals): avoided.
- **Vocab (13):**
  - *si*: if (a new meaning: a1-yes-no-questions taught *si* as "yes" after a negative question)
  - *s'il / s'ils*: if he / if they
  - *sinon*: otherwise
  - *rater*: to miss (a train, a bus)
  - *prévoir*: to plan, to forecast
  - *reporter*: to postpone
  - *un parapluie*: an umbrella
  - *un imprévu*: something unexpected
  - *tant pis*: never mind, too bad
  - *tant mieux*: so much the better
  - *un bouchon*: a traffic jam
  - *à l'heure*: on time
  - *en avance*: early
- **Dialogue:** Friends planning a picnic: *s'il pleut, on ira au musée*.
- **Production tasks:** translate "If it rains, we'll stay at home."; write out three plans with *si*.
- **Needs review:** "Never put the futur straight after *si* when it means 'if'." "*Si* elides only before *il* and *ils*."

## 8. b1-conditionnel-present: The conditionnel présent

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 8
- **Prerequisites:**
  - a2-polite-requests: *je voudrais* and *tu pourrais* were fixed phrases there and become a full tense here. It implies a2-futur-simple (the stems), which the syllabus also listed.
- **Teaches:** formation (future stem + imparfait endings); irregular stems (*serais*, *aurais*, *irais*, *ferais*, *pourrais*, *voudrais*, *devrais*, *saurais*, *viendrais*, *faudrait*); uses: politeness, wishes (*j'aimerais*, *ce serait bien*), unconfirmed news (*selon la presse, le ministre serait malade*). Reuses: polite requests, travel.
- **Not yet taught:** *si* + imparfait (b1-si-imparfait-conditionnel): avoided. *tu devrais* for advice is shown as a form only; b1-giving-advice owns the use.
- **Vocab (19):**
  - *je parlerais / tu parlerais / il parlerait*: I / you / he would speak
  - *nous parlerions / vous parleriez / ils parleraient*: we / you / they would speak
  - *je serais*: I would be
  - *j'aurais*: I would have
  - *j'irais*: I would go
  - *je ferais*: I would do, I would make
  - *il pourrait*: he could, he might
  - *je devrais / tu devrais*: I / you should
  - *je saurais*: I would know
  - *je viendrais*: I would come
  - *il faudrait*: we'd need to, someone should
  - *j'aimerais*: I'd like
  - *je préférerais*: I'd prefer
  - *ce serait*: it would be
  - *selon*: according to
  - *il paraît que*: apparently, it seems that
  - *apparemment*: apparently
  - *possible*: possible
  - *un / une ministre*: a minister
- **Dialogue:** A customer at a travel agency asks politely and states wishes: *J'aimerais partir en mai, ce serait possible ?*
- **Production tasks:** translate "I would like to leave in May."; rewrite three direct requests as polite ones.
- **Needs review:** "News reports use the conditionnel for information that isn't confirmed." "*Il paraît que* takes the indicative."

## 9. b1-si-imparfait-conditionnel: Si + imparfait, conditionnel

- **Type:** grammar · **Order:** 9
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-conditionnel-present: the result clause. It implies a2-imparfait-formation, which the syllabus also listed.
  - b1-si-present-futur: the contrast with real conditions.
- **Teaches:** unreal present and unlikely future: *si j'avais le temps, je voyagerais*; either clause first; *si j'étais toi* as a pattern; contrast with *si* + present. Reuses: dreams, travel, work.
- **Not yet taught:** *si* + plus-que-parfait (b2-si-plus-que-parfait): avoided.
- **Vocab (13):**
  - *gagner au loto*: to win the lottery
  - *riche*: rich
  - *la célébrité*: fame
  - *au bord de la mer*: by the sea
  - *avoir le temps*: to have time
  - *si j'étais toi*: if I were you
  - *imaginer*: to imagine
  - *supposer*: to suppose
  - *un superpouvoir*: a superpower
  - *invisible*: invisible
  - *voler*: to fly
  - *changer de vie*: to change your life
  - *déménager*: to move house
- **Dialogue:** Two colleagues on a coffee break: *Qu'est-ce que tu ferais si tu gagnais au loto ?*
- **Production tasks:** translate "If I had time, I would travel."; write out three sentences starting *Si j'étais…*.
- **Needs review:** "Never put the conditionnel straight after *si*." "Either clause can come first."

## 10. b1-giving-advice: Giving advice

- **Type:** communication · **Order:** 10
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-si-imparfait-conditionnel: *si j'étais toi, je…* is one of the main advice patterns. It implies b1-conditionnel-present and a2-il-faut-and-advice, which the syllabus listed. (Syllabus change: see the overview.)
- **Teaches:** *tu devrais* / *vous devriez* + infinitive; *tu pourrais*; *à ta place* / *à votre place*, *je* + conditionnel; *si j'étais toi*; *je te conseille de* / *je vous conseille de*; *il vaudrait mieux* + infinitive; *n'hésite pas à*; the negative infinitive, with *ne pas* together before the infinitive (*je te conseille de ne pas partir*, *essaie de ne pas oublier*); reacting to advice.
- **Not yet taught:** *il faut que tu…* and *il vaut mieux que* + subjunctive (Unit 4): avoided. Advice uses *il faut* + infinitive, as in A2.
- **Vocab (16):**
  - *tu devrais / vous devriez*: you should
  - *à ta place / à votre place*: if I were you, in your place
  - *je te conseille de / je vous conseille de*: I advise you to
  - *conseiller*: to advise
  - *un conseil*: a piece of advice
  - *il vaudrait mieux*: it would be better to
  - *n'hésite pas à / n'hésitez pas à*: don't hesitate to, feel free to
  - *ce serait une bonne idée de*: it would be a good idea to
  - *demander conseil à*: to ask someone for advice
  - *suivre un conseil*: to follow advice
  - *une solution*: a solution
  - *le stress*: stress
  - *se détendre*: to relax
  - *le sommeil*: sleep
  - *Je vais essayer.*: I'll try.
  - *ne pas* + infinitive: not to (*essaie de ne pas oublier*: try not to forget)
- **Dialogue:** A student (*tu*) tells a friend they're stressed about exams; the friend gives three pieces of advice.
- **Longer text:** A letter to an advice column and the columnist's reply (*vous*).
- **Production tasks:** translate "If I were you, I would sleep more." and "Try not to forget your keys."; write a four-sentence advice reply.
- **Needs review:** "*Tu devrais* is softer than *il faut*." "*Il vaudrait mieux* is followed by an infinitive." "With an infinitive, *ne* and *pas* go together before the verb: *de ne pas partir*."

## 11. b1-dreams-and-ambitions: Dreams and ambitions

- **Type:** communication · **Order:** 11
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-si-imparfait-conditionnel: dreams use *si* + imparfait and the conditionnel.
- **Teaches:** plans (*j'ai l'intention de*, *je compte* + infinitive, *mon but est de*), hopes (*j'espère que* + indicative, often futur), dreams (*je rêve de*, *si je pouvais*, *dans l'idéal*), the ideal life.
- **Not yet taught:** *j'aimerais que* + subjunctive (b1-subjonctif-wishes-emotions): avoided.
- **Vocab (17):**
  - *une ambition*: an ambition
  - *un but*: a goal, an aim
  - *avoir l'intention de*: to intend to
  - *compter* + infinitive: to plan to, to intend to
  - *espérer*: to hope
  - *réaliser un rêve*: to make a dream come true
  - *dans l'idéal*: ideally
  - *idéal / idéale*: ideal
  - *l'avenir*: the future
  - *à l'avenir*: in future
  - *créer*: to create
  - *monter sa propre entreprise*: to set up your own business
  - *faire du bénévolat*: to do voluntary work
  - *une carrière*: a career
  - *motivé / motivée*: motivated
  - *la retraite*: retirement
  - *prendre sa retraite*: to retire
- **Dialogue:** Two friends turning thirty talk about where they'd like to be in ten years.
- **Longer text:** A blog post, *Ma vie idéale*, mixing plans (futur, *compter*) and dreams (conditionnel).
- **Production tasks:** translate "I intend to live abroad."; write out a five-sentence paragraph about your ideal life.
- **Needs review:** "*Espérer que* takes the indicative, often the futur, not the subjunctive." "*Compter* + infinitive means 'to intend to'."

## 12. b1-review-hypotheses: Review: Hypotheses and wishes

- **Type:** review · **Order:** 12
- **Prerequisites:**
  - b1-giving-advice: the advice reply.
  - b1-dreams-and-ambitions: the "what would you do if…" piece.
- **Teaches:** recap of real and unreal conditions, the conditionnel and advice.
- **Vocab (6):**
  - *un choix*: a choice
  - *hésiter*: to hesitate
  - *prendre une décision*: to make a decision
  - *peser le pour et le contre*: to weigh up the pros and cons
  - *changer d'avis*: to change your mind
  - *réfléchir*: to think it over
- **Model text:** A forum post, *Que feriez-vous si vous pouviez changer de métier ?*, with one answer, then an advice reply to someone torn between two jobs.
- **Production tasks:** the writing task; one write-out paragraph; one translation with a *si*-clause.
- **Needs review:** none beyond the unit's lessons.
